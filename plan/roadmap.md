# Roadmap

This is a rolling six-month roadmap. It names outcomes, not every task. GitHub Issues hold
assignments, owners and live status. Add a project view only if the issue list becomes hard to
follow. Review this file quarterly or when the project's direction changes.

## Goal for this cycle

Make the manual useful across the main early-stage founder journey, then prove that people can
improve it through a clear, trustworthy human review loop.

## 1. Complete connected founder journeys

Prioritize 10–12 flagship guides that make these paths genuinely usable:

1. **Idea to evidence:** market research, problem selection, customer interviews and small tests.
2. **Set up correctly:** business structure, founder agreement, registration, banking, tax, VAT and
   payments.
3. **First customers:** pricing, Facebook and messaging-led sales, delivery, support and retention.
4. **Team and funding readiness:** first hires, basic metrics, cap table, pitch, data room and deal
   terms.

Finish the missing links in these paths before expanding into long-tail topics. A journey is useful
only when a founder can follow it without meeting a critical stub.

## 2. Establish the human contribution and review loop

- Keep no more than 12 scoped content assignments active at once.
- Give each assignment a reader outcome, contributor, maintainer reviewer and expected evidence.
- Accept small corrections, browser drafts and structured conversations or voice notes. When a
  maintainer turns someone's input into a draft, confirm attribution and reuse permission.
- Review the page, not the contributor's status. A respected name does not override weak evidence
  or unclear advice.
- Aim to give a first useful response to a contribution within two working days.
- Keep publication human-gated through pull requests.

## 3. Prove quality and freshness

- Walk the flagship guides with real founders and record where they hesitate, misunderstand or
  cannot take the next step.
- Correct those problems before producing more pages.
- Keep legal, tax, registration, payment and funding claims current through the maintenance
  calendar.
- Show expert review only when a relevant person reviewed a defined scope and allowed the project
  to name them.

## 4. Improve the product only where it removes friction

Technical work supports the founder journey and shares capabilities across the manual and its
discovery tools:

1. Keep the browser editor and pull-request flow reliable, including sign-in, draft recovery, media
   review and clear errors.
2. Keep route, citation, language, media and freshness checks dependable.
3. Fix observed problems in navigation, search, mobile reading, editing and review.
4. Preserve fast, mostly static article pages, public reading without login and the existing
   security boundaries.

Open technical work from a reproduced failure or a clear reader or contributor need, not from a
speculative feature list.

Do not build a second CMS, a GitHub wiki, a complex admin dashboard or an AI assistant during this
cycle. Extend the existing website and review infrastructure for a concrete user need. A narrow
review queue for structured company/problem submissions can support the direction below without
replacing the guide editor or its GitHub review flow.

## Startup ideas and shared company profiles

**Direction confirmed, 16 September 2026:** browse individual startup ideas, with shared problem
research underneath. Give each company a shared identity reused by problem connections,
Startup 50 and case studies. Suggest existing companies before accepting new profile proposals.
Keep new records and connections subject to human review.

The local slice separates problems and ideas, reuses company IDs from existing research,
and includes a D1-backed company-selection and review flow with static publication.
The public catalogue is `/startup-ideas`, with supporting `/companies/<slug>` profiles. This is not deployed yet.
The model and remaining launch requirements are owned by
[`ecosystem-data.md`](./ecosystem-data.md); local commands are documented in
[`docs/startup-ideas.md`](../docs/startup-ideas.md). Preserve public reading, bilingual parity and
article performance. Database work is not a reason to migrate all editorial content.

## Immediate priorities

1. Choose the next set of no more than 12 assignments from the four journeys above.
2. Finish and review the active funding-readiness set, then open the next content set.
3. Check the registration, tax and payment path for missing or stale high-risk guidance.
4. Run founder walkthroughs on the strongest validation, setup and payment guides.
5. Turn the resulting content gaps and product friction into small GitHub issues with an owner and
   reviewer.

## Measures that matter

- The four journeys have no critical missing step.
- Flagship guides pass editorial, source and founder-usefulness review.
- High-risk pages are within their review window.
- Contributions receive a timely first response and accepted contributors return.
- Observed reader or contributor blockers are resolved.

Page count, raw traffic and a large backlog are not success by themselves.

## Case-study presentation and deferred work

The approved direction is a gallery of company-specific portrait covers. Pathao, 10 Minute School
and bKash stay in the first desktop row. ShopUp / SILQ, Revora and Shikho follow in the second;
Truck Lagbe, Chaldal and Shohoz form the third. Dorik, Arogga and iFarmer keep their covers below.
Finished studies link to the full article, and unwritten studies use "To be written" in the
bottom CTA, linking to starting sources. Preserve the existing full articles while adding
company banners, sourced
turning-point timelines and a closer look at a decision or experiment. Background, setbacks,
lessons, evidence limits and interview links remain part of the articles. The Startup 50 companies
are included in `content-backlog.csv`; listing a company does not mean its case study is written.

**Deferred, 10 September 2026:** transferable exercises and reader-designed experiments are not

have been reviewed. They would supplement the stories, not replace their body content.
