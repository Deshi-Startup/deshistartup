# Intellectual Property: Know What You Own and Use

> **In short:** Start with an inventory of the names, content, designs, code and other work your business uses. Identify ownership and permission questions before commissioning work or committing to a brand.

## Make an asset record

| Record | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Brand and product names | What checks have we made? Which names are still provisional? |
| Code, designs and written content | Who created each item? What agreement or license covers our use? |
| Contractor and founder work | Where are the ownership terms recorded? Are any terms unresolved? |
| Third-party material | What use is permitted, and where is the permission recorded? |
| Confidential business information | Who needs access, and how is access removed? |

Keep a link to the relevant agreement or license beside each item. Possessing a file, paying an invoice or obtaining a domain name does not by itself answer every ownership or protection question.

## Resolve the next uncertainty

Before sharing sensitive work or signing an agreement, list the exact question for an appropriately qualified adviser. Use the [legal and accounting directory](/en/directory/legal-accounting) to begin a shortlist. For founder responsibilities, see [co-founder agreements](/en/cofounders/founder-agreement); for entity-name checks, see [name clearance](/en/registration/name-clearance).

Name clearance and protection of a brand are different questions. Do not use a company-name result as a complete IP assessment.
