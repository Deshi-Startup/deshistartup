# Privacy Policy

> **In short:** You do not need an account or need to identify yourself to read the guides. If you
> sign in with Google to edit a page, we receive your name, email, profile image and a temporary
> identity token. Submitted text, images and your name are used to review the contribution.
> Public credit for accepted work may appear on the contributors page. A message sent through the
> contact form reaches our inbox and is used only to reply to you.
> Measurement tags may also run through Google Tag Manager to help us understand site use.

**Effective: 16 August 2026**

## When you only read the site

When you open a page, hosting and security services may process ordinary technical details about
the request. These can include the IP address, browser type, time and requested URL.

The site loads Google Tag Manager. Measurement tags configured inside it may send data such as page
views, device information, approximate location and the referring page to Google. The application
code does not currently show advertising or build its own profiles of readers. Browser privacy and
tracking controls may limit this measurement.

## When you send us a message

The [contact form](/en/contact) asks for your name, email address, a topic and your message. Those
four fields are emailed to the Deshi Startup inbox and are used only to answer you. Your address is
not added to a mailing list, and nothing else from the page is attached to the message.

The form has no CAPTCHA. It checks that browser requests have this site's origin and use JSON,
discards messages that fill a hidden field, limits the request size, and applies per-IP and
per-Cloudflare-location rate limits. The IP address is used for the one-minute rate-limit window; it
is not written into the email or stored separately by the application.

Email sent to hello@, security@ or conduct@deshistartup.com is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing
into a Google mailbox, so both companies process it under their own policies.

## When you edit a page

When you open the editor and sign in with Google, Google provides your name, email, profile image
and a signed temporary ID token.

- The token stays in browser local storage. It normally expires in about one hour.
- An unfinished local draft may stay in the same browser for up to 14 days.
- The server verifies the token on every request and keeps no separate login session.
- Submitted text, the change summary and your name appear in a public GitHub pull request. Your
  email does not.
- Account-based rate limits and mute/ban records may be used to prevent abuse.

## Names on the contributors page

When your contribution is accepted and goes live, your public name, GitHub profile, profile photo
and accepted-work count may appear on the [contributors page](/en/contributors). For work sent
through the inline editor, the page uses the name already published in the pull request. It does
not include email addresses, Google identity tokens or private Google profile data. Work is not
assigned to a person when attribution is ambiguous.

Profile photos come from GitHub's own servers, so opening the page sends GitHub an ordinary image
request that may carry the IP address, browser information and request time. A letter monogram
stands in when there is no photo, or when you would rather not show one.

To rename or remove an entry, [open a GitHub request](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/issues/new?title=Contributor%20listing%20request).
The issue is public, so do not include an email address or other sensitive information.

## When you contribute an image

Image bytes, type, dimensions, alt text, source/credit, page path and a hashed account identifier
are stored in a private quarantine bucket. Only the owner and authorized reviewers can view a
pending image.

- Approved images become part of the public media library and GitHub history.
- Rejected images are deleted immediately.
- Undecided pending images expire after seven days.
- Before publication, review checks privacy, copyright, personal information and whether anything
  sensitive has been properly redacted.

Do not submit another person's private information, confidential documents or sensitive
screenshots without permission.

## How long information remains

Expired tokens and local drafts older than 14 days are removed when the browser next uses the
editor. Accepted contributions may remain in the public GitHub version history. Approved media may
remain in content history and caches.

Contact messages and replies may remain in the Google mailbox while they are needed for the
conversation or ordinary record-keeping. You can ask us to delete a conversation; provider backups
or legal obligations may keep a copy for longer.

Google, GitHub and Cloudflare process information under their own policies.

## Questions or removal requests

[Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/issues/new), but do not place
sensitive information in it because the issue will be public. For anything private, write to
hello@deshistartup.com or use the [contact form](/en/contact). Once a contribution is published,
copies may spread through git history, forks or caches, so complete removal may not always be
possible.
