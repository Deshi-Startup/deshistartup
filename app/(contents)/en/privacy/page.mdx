# Privacy Policy

> **In short:** You do not need an account to read Deshi Startup. We do not collect information to show ads or track individual readers. If you sign in with Google to edit a page, we receive your name, email address and profile picture. Your submitted text and name appear in a public GitHub pull request; your email address does not.

**Effective: 27 July 2026**

## When you read the site

You do not need to provide a name, email address or account to read the guides. Our hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as an IP address, browser type and request time, to deliver and protect the site. We do not use that information to build individual reader profiles.

The site does not use our own advertising or behavioural analytics.

## When you edit a page

The inline editor requires Google sign-in. Google provides your name, email address, profile picture and a signed, short-lived identity token.

- The token is kept in your browser's local storage and normally expires in about one hour.
- An unsent draft may remain in the same browser's local storage for up to 14 days. It is removed when submitted or discarded.
- The server verifies the token on each request and does not create a separate login session.
- Your email address identifies your contribution branch. It is not printed in the pull request.
- When you submit an edit, the text, your summary and your name are published in a public GitHub pull request.

Google and GitHub process information under their own policies. Their privacy terms also apply when you use those services.

## Local storage and cookies

We use browser local storage for the sign-in token and unsent drafts. Google's sign-in service may use its own cookies or similar technology. You can clear local data through your browser settings, but doing so will remove your sign-in state and any unsent draft.

## How long information remains

Expired sign-in tokens are removed when you next open the site. The editor also removes unsent drafts older than 14 days when it next checks local storage. A submitted contribution becomes part of GitHub's public history and may be retained to preserve that history and support open-source collaboration.

## Questions and removal requests

To ask about, correct or request removal of your information, [open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/issues/new). Do not post sensitive information in a public issue. Complete removal of a public contribution may not always be possible because others may have copied or forked the repository.

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date on this page.
