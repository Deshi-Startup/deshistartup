# Guided Journeys: Work Through One Practical Goal

> **In short:** Choose the job you need to finish, then use its journey to put the work in order. Start at the first step you have not completed; you do not need to follow every journey or begin the business again.

Some tasks cross several subjects. Accepting digital payments, for example, involves choosing a payment method, preparing the account, testing a payment and keeping records. Looking up each subject separately can leave you unsure what to do first.

A journey brings the relevant guides together in a sequence. Use one when you know the result you want but need help organising the work. If you only need an answer about a particular subject, such as VAT or hiring, [Browse by Topic](/en/guides) is the shorter route.

## Choose the task in front of you

These are starting points for common tasks. Open the journey that matches your situation; the full list follows below.

| Your situation | Start here |
|---|---|

| You know the problem but have not chosen a first version | [I have an idea but no product](/en/journeys/idea-but-no-product) |
| You have decided a private limited company is appropriate | [Register a private limited company](/en/journeys/register-a-private-limited-company) |
| Customers need a way to pay you digitally | [Accept digital payments](/en/journeys/accept-digital-payments) |
| You want to approach small businesses as customers | [Sell to SMEs](/en/journeys/sell-to-smes) |
| There is work you need a first employee to take on | [Hire the first employee](/en/journeys/hire-first-employee) |

The journeys help organise these tasks; they do not decide whether every step applies to you. For instance, a company-registration journey is useful after you have chosen that business structure, not proof that every founder should incorporate immediately.

## Start from what you have already done

Read the journey once before acting. For each step, check whether you have actually completed the work or only discussed it. An account application you submitted may still be awaiting approval. A payment setup is not tested until a transaction has gone through and you have checked the result.

Then work on the earliest unfinished step that the later work depends on. If you already have the necessary account, you can concentrate on testing and records rather than applying again. When you share the work with a teammate, agree who will handle that step and when you will check progress together.

The detailed guides explain the individual tasks. Where a topic is marked as still to be written, it is not a substitute for those instructions. For legal, tax or regulated activities, confirm the requirements for your situation with the relevant official source or professional before acting.

If you are unsure which goal matters now, the [four-stage roadmap](/en/roadmap) helps you think about the wider business. Otherwise, choose one journey and begin with its first unfinished task.
