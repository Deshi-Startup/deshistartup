# Journeys – step-by-step paths by goal

> **TL;DR:** You do not need every guide at once. Choose the present goal, complete the first task in that path, then use the result to move forward. If a linked page carries a **“to be written”** notice, it is still unwritten; continue the previous task or use the nearest completed guide.

Reading guides one by one is easy, but a single guide can't answer "what exactly should I do now, and what comes after that?" These paths do exactly that: they break a specific goal (like "start an online business" or "register a company") into a few ordered steps and point you to the most relevant guide for each.

Not sure which path fits you? Start with the [starter roadmap](/en/start-here) or the [founder path chooser](/en/start-here/founder-path-chooser).

## Business Model & Sector Playbooks

Select the step-by-step playbook designed specifically for your startup's business model:

- **E-Commerce & F-Commerce Playbook:** Trade license, cash on delivery, bKash/Nagad payments, courier contracts & digital commerce rules. → [Start an online business](/en/journeys/start-an-online-business)
- **B2B & Software Export Playbook (SaaS):** Company incorporation, BASIS membership, encashment certificates & international payments. → [Launch a SaaS](/en/journeys/launch-a-saas)
- **Fintech & Payments Playbook:** Bangladesh Bank regulations, PSP/PSO licensing, bank partnerships & payment gateways. → [Build a fintech startup](/en/journeys/build-a-fintech-startup)
- **Agritech & Agri-Processing:** Supply chain, cold storage logistics, contract farming & market linkage. → [Build an agritech startup](/en/journeys/build-an-agritech-startup)
- **Edtech & Online Learning:** Content copyright, payment subscriptions, acquiring student users & institutional partnerships. → [Build an edtech startup](/en/journeys/build-an-edtech-startup)
