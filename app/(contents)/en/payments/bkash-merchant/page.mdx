# bKash Personal vs Merchant Account: Where the Wall Is

> **Key Takeaway:** Running a business on a personal bKash account always ends at a wall. Under bKash's own published rules, an account can hold at most BDT 500,000 at any moment, and you can withdraw at most BDT 200,000 a month across agents and ATMs combined. On top of that, cashing out at an ordinary agent costs BDT 18.50 per thousand — roughly 1.85% just to get your own sales revenue into your hand. For business payments, compare the separate Personal Retail Account and standard Merchant routes below. Each has its own charges and limits.

## Choose the account before applying

Check which route fits your business:

| Account | What it means here |
|---|---|
| Personal | The cash-out limits and worked cost example below describe this account. |
| Personal Retail Account (PRA) | A separate business account for eligible small retail and Facebook-based businesses. bKash directs businesses without a trade licence to this route.[^bkash-online-business][^bkash-pra] |
| Standard Merchant | Ask for the documents, charges and terms for your business. The enquiry form asks about a national identity card (NID), trade licence and bank account.[^bkash-merchant-features] |

For PRA, prepare your NID, proof that you own the SIM, and a mobile number registered in your name that has never had a bKash account. If a nominee is required, provide the NID of the person you name. The Bangla FAQ says applicants must be Bangladeshi and at least 18. An NID already used for a bKash business account is excluded. An existing personal account cannot be converted into PRA.[^bkash-pra]

PRA has its own fees and limits. Some published fees are promotional, and bank transfer is labelled “next phase”. Check the current [PRA terms and application link](https://www.bkash.com/en/page/personal-retail-account) before using it.[^bkash-pra] Account eligibility does not decide your business's [trade-licence requirements](/en/trade-license).

## Where the Wall Is

What bKash's charges-and-limits page listed for a personal account on 28 July 2026:[^bkash-limits]

| What you are doing | Daily maximum | Monthly maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Cash out from an agent | BDT 30,000 | BDT 200,000 (agent and ATM combined) |
| Sending money | BDT 50,000 | BDT 300,000 |
| bKash to bank | BDT 50,000 | BDT 300,000 |
| Balance held in the account | – | BDT 500,000 at any moment |
| **Merchant payment** | **No limit** | **No limit** |

The Payment row describes customer payments, not an unlimited merchant collection or settlement facility. The same bKash page says maximum payment limits can vary by merchant type. Obtain the limits for your own merchant account in writing.

Two consequences follow. First, a wholesale customer cannot send you more than BDT 50,000 in a day by send money, so large orders have to be split. Second, no matter how much comes in, you cannot pull more than BDT 200,000 out per month through agents and ATMs.

While the business is small these limits never surface. As sales grow, one day arrives when money is coming in and will not come out — and that day usually falls in the last week of the month, when the supplier needs paying.

## What It Costs to Withdraw Your Own Money

Worse than the limits are the charges. bKash's published cash-out rates:[^bkash-cashout-charges]

| Where you withdraw | Per thousand | As a percentage | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Priyo agent (two can be set) | BDT 13.95 | 1.395% | Up to BDT 50,000 a month |
| ATM (BRAC, City, and Q-Cash booths) | BDT 14.90 | 1.49% | – |
| Any other agent | BDT 18.50 | 1.85% | – |

Work it through. Say you withdraw the full BDT 200,000 in a month, right against the limit. On the cheapest route — BDT 50,000 through a Priyo agent and the remaining BDT 150,000 by ATM — that costs 697.50 + 2,235 = **BDT 2,932.50**. Withdrawing wherever is convenient, at ordinary agents, costs 200 × 18.50 = **BDT 3,700**.

Over a year that is BDT 35,190 to BDT 44,400. Purely to move your own sales revenue into your own hand.

Set that number against the [gateway comparison](/en/payments/gateway-comparison). SSLCOMMERZ's entire setup fee is BDT 25,500, paid once. A year of cash-out charges exceeds that setup fee, but this is not a total-cost comparison: the gateway also charges recurring transaction fees. Compare setup, processing and settlement costs on the same annual sales volume.

There are send-money concessions, but not at business scale. bKash states that setting five Priyo Numbers allows up to BDT 25,000 a month of send money free of charge, plus unlimited free transfers of up to BDT 50 to any number.[^bkash-send-money] A BDT 25,000 monthly allowance does not run a business.

## What Changes on a Merchant Account

bKash's merchant page lists what the account is meant to provide: the bKash Merchant App, Merchant QR, counter payment, round-the-clock payment collection, and transaction history. The company claims payments can be collected from more than 70 million bKash users.[^bkash-merchant-features]

These are the company's own claims, not independent verification. Even so, three things genuinely change for the business:

- **Business payments use a separate service.** Confirm collection, balance, transaction and settlement limits for your merchant type rather than assuming they disappear.
- **QR and counter payment arrive.** Less chasing customers to type a number and send a screenshot before you can match an order.
- **Transaction history accrues in the business's name.** Later, for a bank account, a gateway, or a loan application, that record is the only proof you have.

The third is the one everyone defers, because it pays nothing today. When the bank asks for six months of business transactions, a personal number's statement will not do.

## Ask for the standard Merchant rate

**The standard Merchant enquiry page does not quote a payment rate.** This was checked on 12 October 2026.[^bkash-merchant-features] Ask bKash for the charges for your business. PRA has its own published fee table, so the two routes need separate comparisons.[^bkash-pra]

The route in matches. You fill in a form on the website with the shop name, district, and contact details, and they get in touch. This is an enquiry, not a published price quote.

So be ready when the call comes. Get these in writing:

1. The merchant charge per transaction, and who bears it — you or the customer.
2. Whether moving money to a bank account carries a separate charge, and how much.
3. How many days until money reaches the bank.
4. Whether merchant accounts really carry no transaction limit, or whether limits are set by business type.
5. Which documents are needed, and whether it is possible without a trade licence.
6. Whether the QR and Merchant App cost extra.

The first matters most. Plenty of shops in Bangladesh push the merchant charge onto the customer, who then gets annoyed and wants to go back to send money. Decide at the outset whether you absorb the charge or build it into the price.

## When to Switch

These signs tell you it is time to compare a business account with your current personal account:

- monthly sales have passed BDT 100,000 and are still climbing
- you need to withdraw more than BDT 30,000 in a single day
- you are hitting the cash-out limit at month end
- customers hesitate at a personal number, especially on higher-value items
- the trade licence has come through
- you are thinking about a bank account or a gateway application

If any one of these fits, choose the account route above and start its application. Contact and verification take time, so starting after you hit the wall means sales are already stuck.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Result |
|---|---|
| Scaling before learning the limits | Money arrives at month end and cannot be withdrawn |
| Cashing out at whichever agent is nearest | You forfeit what Priyo agents and ATMs would have saved |
| Taking the account without knowing the merchant charge | The cost lands on margin after prices are already set |
| Pushing the charge onto customers | They revert to send money and reconciliation gets messy again |
| Using the wrong account application | A standard Merchant enquiry and a PRA application have different requirements |

## Checklist

- [ ] Know how close monthly sales are to bKash's BDT 200,000 cash-out limit
- [ ] Two Priyo agents are set
- [ ] Added up last month's total cash-out charges
- [ ] Checked the documents for the chosen account, including trade-licence requirements
- [ ] Submitted the application for the account that fits the business
- [ ] Six questions written down for the call
- [ ] Decided who bears the merchant charge

Rule of thumb: **once monthly cash-out reaches half of the personal account's BDT 200,000 limit, start comparing business-account options.** This is a planning cue, not a bKash eligibility rule.
