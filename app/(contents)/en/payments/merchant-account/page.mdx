# What You Need to Open a Merchant Account

> **Key Takeaway:** Collecting the documents one at a time wastes weeks, because each one depends on another. There is no business bank account without a trade licence, and without a bank account a gateway has nowhere to send your settlements. So work through the order, not the list. And before you submit anything, do one thing without fail: check that the business name and address match letter for letter across every document.

## Who Asks for What

Three kinds of organisation may ask you for paperwork — <Term name="mfs">MFS</Term> merchant services (bKash, Nagad, Rocket), payment gateways, and banks. Here is what two gateways asked for on their own sites as of 28 July 2026:[^sslcommerz-onboarding][^aamarpay-faq]

| Document | On SSLCOMMERZ's list | On aamarPay's list |
|---|---|---|
| Trade licence | For every structure | Yes |
| <Term name="e-tin">TIN</Term> | For every structure | Yes |
| <Term name="vat-bin">VAT registration</Term> | For every structure | If any |
| NID | Not listed separately | Yes |
| Photograph | Not listed separately | 1 copy |
| DBID | For every structure | Not listed |
| Partnership deed | For partnerships | For partnerships |
| COI and MOA/AOA | For limited companies | For limited companies |
| Their own forms | MEF | Application form and bank sub-merchant form |

The two lists are not the same, and that is the useful part. Ask the providers you are considering for the current requirements for your legal structure and activity. A provider's request for VAT or DBID is not by itself a universal legal registration rule; resolve any mismatch before applying.

bKash's merchant application asks about identity, trade-licence and bank information.[^bkash-merchant] Confirm the complete requirements and the account type that fits your business with bKash. Details are in [bKash Personal vs Merchant Account](/en/payments/bkash-merchant).

## Where Each Document Comes From

| Document | Issued by | In short |
|---|---|---|
| Trade licence | City corporation, municipality, or union parishad | This business, at this address, is listed in your name – [details](/en/trade-license) |
| e-TIN | NBR, online | The 12-digit taxpayer identification number – [details](/en/tax/e-tin) |
| BIN (VAT) | NBR, via the Mushak-2.1 form | The 9-digit VAT identification number – [details](/en/tax/vat-bin) |
| Business bank account | A scheduled bank | Where settlement money lands – [details](/en/payments/bank-account) |
| COI, MOA, and AOA | RJSC | If you incorporate – [details](/en/registration/private-limited) |
| DBID | Government portal | A separate identifier for online business, see below |
| MEF and other forms | The provider | They send them; you complete, sign, seal, and return |

## The Order That Avoids Backtracking

Dependencies vary by structure. If applying as a company, complete incorporation before obtaining documents in that company's name. Use the following as a planning sequence, then confirm it with your bank and provider:

1. **Have your NID.** Everything else starts here.
2. **Get the trade licence.** Whatever address you put here is the address every later document must carry.
3. **Get the e-TIN.** It is done online. In your own name for a sole proprietorship, in the company's name if you have incorporated.
4. **Open a bank account in the business name.** With the trade licence and TIN in hand, this step gets much easier.
5. **Register for VAT or turnover-tax enlistment if it applies.** Work out which route is yours first.
6. **If you sell online, find out about DBID.** Where a gateway requires it, the application will not move without one.
7. **Now apply for the merchant account or gateway.**

Some work can run in parallel. For example, a sole proprietor may already have a personal TIN, while a company needs its incorporation documents for company-name applications.

## Check DBID separately

DBID stands for Digital Business Identification, a separate registration number for doing business online. SSLCOMMERZ asks for it against every business structure on its onboarding list,[^sslcommerz-onboarding] while aamarPay's published list does not mention it at all.[^aamarpay-faq]

Use the [DBID application guide](/en/registration/dbid) for the current public process and its evidence limits. Check both the registration rules for your activity and your gateway's onboarding requirements. One provider omitting DBID from its checklist does not establish a legal exemption.

## Check These Before You Submit

The usual reason an application stalls is not a missing document. It is details that do not match across the documents you have.

- **Business name.** One spelling on the trade licence and another at the bank is enough to stop verification. Keep spacing, spelling, and the English-versus-Bangla form identical everywhere.
- **Address.** The same address, spelled the same way, on the trade licence, at the bank, and on the application.
- **Owner's name.** Exactly as it appears on the NID, everywhere.
- **State of the website.** The gateway opens your site and looks at it, so it needs to be live when you apply.
- **Validity.** Check whether the trade licence renewal date has passed. Submitting an expired licence stalls the whole file.

The fourth catches people out. Since they do look at the site, an application can come back if there is no pricing, contact, or refund-policy page. Open your own site with a stranger's eyes before you apply.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Result |
|---|---|
| Going to the bank before the trade licence | The account cannot be opened and you make the trip twice |
| Offering a personal-name bank account | Settlement records never build up in the business's name |
| Names and addresses differing across documents | Verification stalls and corrections eat weeks |
| Assuming one provider’s list fits every provider | Confirm the chosen provider’s current requirements before applying |
| Applying with an unfinished website | The gateway looks and sends it back |
| Submitting an expired trade licence | The whole file hangs |

## Checklist

- [ ] A clear scan of the NID
- [ ] Trade licence in hand and still valid
- [ ] e-TIN taken in the correct entity's name
- [ ] Bank account active in the business name
- [ ] Know whether VAT registration applies
- [ ] COI, MOA, and AOA in hand if incorporated
- [ ] Asked the provider whether DBID is required
- [ ] Business name and address match letter for letter across every document
- [ ] Website has pricing, contact, and refund-policy pages

Rule of thumb: **confirm the requirements for your chosen providers and legal structure before assembling the application.**
