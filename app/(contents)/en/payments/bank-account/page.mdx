# Business Bank Account: Which Bank Will Actually Open One

> **Key Takeaway:** "You need a lot of money to open a business account" is half true, and it is the half that stops people. The minimum opening deposit ranges from about a thousand taka to five lakh depending on which bank you walk into. Do not give up after hearing one bank's answer. The paperwork is nearly identical everywhere, so taking the same file to three or four banks will almost always turn up one that opens.

## Do Not Judge by One Bank

Put two banks' published terms side by side and the gap speaks. As of 28 July 2026:[^sc-business-current][^brac-prapti]

|  | Standard Chartered – Business Current Account | BRAC Bank – PRAPTI Current Account |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum opening deposit | BDT 500,000 | BDT 1,000 |
| Interest | None | Yes, calculated on daily balance |
| Who can open one | Sole proprietorships, partnerships, private limited companies, self-employed professionals | Sole proprietorships, partnerships, private limited companies, educational institutions, NGOs, co-operative societies |

The same working account, and a five-hundred-fold difference in what it takes to start. New founders often go to whichever bank holds their personal account, hear that five lakh is required, and conclude that a business account is out of reach. They knocked on the wrong door.

Two cautions belong with that table. First, the BRAC Bank figure comes from the FAQ on their legacy site, so confirm the current terms at a branch.[^brac-sme-faq] Second, this is not a recommendation list — Bangladesh has many scheduled banks, and most large ones run an SME-friendly account.

## What Documents You Need

Standard Chartered sets its list out most explicitly:[^sc-business-current]

| For everyone | Extra, depending on structure |
|---|---|
| Account opening form | Partnerships and companies: board or partnership resolution |
| Valid current trade licence | Partnerships: partnership deed and registrar of firms certificate |
| NID or passport of owners and shareholders | Companies: MOA, AOA, and certificate of incorporation |
| 12-digit e-TIN certificate | Companies: updated Form X and Form XII |
| Personal information form and photo ID for the proprietor, directors, signatories, and anyone holding 10% or more of the shares |  |

Note that last row. Not just you — everyone holding 10% or more of the shares. With four or five shareholders in a company, gathering everyone's NID and photograph takes time, so start it before you go to the bank.

By the bank's own admission the list is not exhaustive; they may ask for more case by case. Call the branch and get the list for your structure first.

Where each document comes from, and the order to collect them in, is in [What You Need to Open a Merchant Account](/en/payments/merchant-account).

## The Charges Nobody Mentions

Everyone asks about the minimum deposit. Almost nobody asks what gets deducted month after month. BRAC Bank publishes these for the PRAPTI account:[^brac-prapti]

- Debit card BDT 600 plus VAT, for sole proprietorships only
- First chequebook of 25 pages, up to BDT 250 plus VAT
- No charge from the second chequebook onward if last month's average balance was BDT 100,000 or more

The one that bites hardest is the intercity charge. Depositing in one division and withdrawing in another attracts a fee scaled to the amount:

| Transaction amount | Charge (VAT extra) |
|---|---|
| Below BDT 20,000 | Up to BDT 50 |
| BDT 20,000 to below 1 lakh | Up to BDT 100 |
| 1 lakh to below 2 lakh | Up to BDT 200 |
| 2 lakh to below 5 lakh | Up to BDT 300 |
| 5 lakh to below 10 lakh | Up to BDT 500 |
| 10 lakh to below 20 lakh | Up to BDT 1,000 |
| 20 lakh and above | Up to BDT 1,500 |

If you sell outside Dhaka and bank in Dhaka, or the reverse, this recurs every month. Where you collect cash-on-delivery money from the courier and where you deposit it may sit in two different divisions — work that cost out once.

## What to Ask Before You Go

Get these seven answered on the phone before visiting a branch, and you save yourself the trips.

1. What is the minimum opening deposit?
2. Is there a minimum monthly or half-yearly average balance, and what is the penalty for missing it?
3. What is the annual account maintenance charge?
4. Exactly which documents apply to my structure?
5. Do you provide internet banking and a business debit card, and at what cost?
6. How long does opening take?
7. Is anything extra required to receive payment gateway settlements into this account?

Knowing the seventh in advance saves an unpleasant surprise later, when the gateway application is already under way.

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Result |
|---|---|
| Giving up after one bank's answer | The business keeps running through a personal account |
| Choosing on the minimum deposit alone | Monthly charges quietly erase the saving |
| Visiting a branch without a trade licence | You go back a second time |
| Leaving shareholder documents until late | The file stays incomplete |
| Not costing in intercity charges | Margin drops on sales outside your own division |
| Trying to settle into a personal account | Gateways require an account in the business name |

## Checklist

- [ ] Compared minimum deposit and monthly charges at three banks
- [ ] Trade licence and e-TIN in hand
- [ ] NID and photograph collected for every 10%-or-more shareholder
- [ ] For companies, MOA, AOA, COI, and Forms X and XII are current
- [ ] Know the minimum average balance requirement and its penalty
- [ ] Worked out what intercity charges cost given how I sell
- [ ] Confirmed the account can receive gateway settlements

Rule of thumb: **take the same file of documents to at least three banks before choosing.**
