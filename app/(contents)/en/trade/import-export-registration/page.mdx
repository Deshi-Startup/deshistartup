# Import and Export Registration: Which One You Need

> **In short:** An Export Registration Certificate (<Term name="erc">ERC</Term>) and an Import Registration Certificate (<Term name="irc">IRC</Term>) are two different certificates. Both last one year. Both come from the same office. Getting one does not get you the other. The ERC costs the same for everyone. The IRC costs more if you say you will import more, so a big number on the form means a bigger bill every year. The costly mistake is applying before the paperwork exists. For an ordinary first application, get the trade licence, e-TIN and return receipt, nominated-bank certificate and chamber or association membership ready. A BIN is not on that initial list; Form-3 makes it compulsory at the first renewal.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023] Your next step: read the list below and write down one word. ERC, IRC, both, or neither.

Say Tanvir runs a leather-goods studio in Dhaka with nine staff. He has sold bags at home for two years. Last month a buyer in Poland ordered about USD 9,000 worth as a trial. To make them he wants better buckles and zips from a supplier in Guangzhou, about BDT 18 lakh worth a year once the line is running.

His accountant told him to "get the import-export licence". No such licence exists. There are two separate certificates. They cost different amounts, and you apply for each on its own. Tanvir needs both. He also has a problem he has not noticed yet. His <Term name="trade-license">trade licence</Term> still says he is in a line of business that has nothing to do with leather.

---

## Who needs this, and when

Find your line before you read on.

- **You sell only inside Bangladesh and buy only from Bangladeshi suppliers.** You need neither certificate. Come back when a foreign buyer or supplier turns up.
- **You will send physical goods out of Bangladesh.** You need an **ERC**. Bangladesh Customs lists it as a required export document, so your shipment cannot clear without it. It does not matter that the buyer has already paid.[^customs-hub-docs]
- **You will bring physical goods into Bangladesh.** Raw material, parts, machines, or stock to resell. You need an **IRC**. Read the fee table closely. This is the one that costs real money.
- **You will do both, like Tanvir.** You need both. Two applications, two fees, two renewal dates.
- **You sell software, design, data work or another service over the internet.** You need an **ERC** under the 2023 order, although the EXP procedure does not apply to services delivered online. Read [the section on services](#if-you-export-services-over-the-internet) for the distinction.
- **You want to sell a foreign company's product here for commission.** That is called *indenting*. It is a third certificate, and it costs five times what an ordinary ERC costs.
- **You want to order a sample or two from AliExpress.** That is not importing in the sense this page means. See [sample and small imports](/en/trade/sample-imports).
- **You are setting up inside an EPZ or economic zone.** Your zone authority has its own rules. Start with [BEPZA and BEZA](/en/trade/bepza-beza), then come back.

By the end you will know which certificate you need, what it costs in year one with VAT, what you must have ready before you apply, and what date to put in your calendar.

## What these certificates are

All three come from one office: the **Office of the Chief Controller of Imports and Exports (CCI&E)**, under the Ministry of Commerce.

One set of rules covers all three. It is the **Importers, Exporters and Indentors (Registration) Order, 2023**. The government issued it as S.R.O. No. 326-Law/2023 on 29 November 2023 and published it in the Bangladesh Gazette on 3 January 2024. It cancelled the old 1981 order completely.[^reg-order-2023]

That cancellation matters, because a lot of official guidance has not caught up. Bangladesh Customs still names the 1981 order in its document list. The Bangladesh Trade Portal does the same, and its IRC page also points to an Import Policy Order that ran out in 2018 and describes collecting a paper form from the third floor of the CCI&E building.[^customs-hub-docs] [^btp-irc-procedure] None of that is how it works now. The whole process is online. If a page tells you to visit a counter, treat everything else on that page as out of date too.

| Certificate | What it lets you do | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| **ERC**, the Export Registration Certificate | Send goods, and services, out of Bangladesh | Anyone selling a product abroad. It is step 1 of the government's own 14-step export process[^export-policy-2024] |
| **IRC**, the Import Registration Certificate | Bring goods, and services, into Bangladesh | Anyone bringing in raw material, parts, machines or stock to resell |
| **ERC (indenting service)** | Act as a sole agent or distributor here for one named foreign supplier, for commission | Agency businesses, not product businesses |

Notice the words "goods **or** services" in the table. That wording comes straight from the 2023 order, which is why the services question further down has a real answer and not an obvious one.[^reg-order-2023] Bangladesh Bank points at the same order. It tells banks they "should ensure that exporters are registered under the Importers, Exporters and Indentors (Registration) Order, 2023."[^bb-export-consolidated]

Think of these as three separate products sold by one shop. Getting one does not bring you closer to another. And the third row is a different business, not a bigger version of the first.

## The establishment rule that catches family businesses

Check this before you budget anything. Article 4 limits which establishment can be registered when businesses share an owner, family or shareholder group. It does not limit an eligible establishment to one certificate type.[^reg-order-2023]

> **Rule:** Only **one establishment** will be registered where one owner has several sole-proprietor businesses, where the owners of two or more businesses are in the same family, or where two or more private limited companies share the same shareholder or the same family of shareholders.

That registered establishment may still need both an IRC and an ERC if it imports and exports. The rule chooses the establishment that may register; it does not turn two certificate types into one.

"Family" here means a person's husband or wife, plus their dependent father, mother or daughter.

There is one exception. The limit does not apply to a husband or wife who started a separate business **before** the marriage and has paid income tax on it. And if separate firms really do handle different classes of goods, the Chief Controller can decide the grouping rule does not apply to them.[^reg-order-2023]

This rule matters when a proposed applicant has one of the owner, family or shareholder relationships listed above with another business. Do not assume the new company is the establishment CCI&E will register. Check which entity is eligible before either one applies.

## If you export services over the internet

Founders who export software, design, data annotation or consulting need to keep two rules separate: the rule for registering as an exporter, and the paperwork for receiving an export payment.

**What the law says.** Article 3 of the 2023 order makes registration a condition for importing or exporting **goods or services**. Its definition of an exporter also includes a firm registered to export goods or services from Bangladesh.[^reg-order-2023] Services are inside the requirement, not an exception to it.

**What the export policy says.** Step 1 of the government's export process is to get an ERC from the CCI&E. The policy covers products and services together.[^export-policy-2024]

**What the central bank says.** Banks must check that exporters are registered under the 2023 order.[^bb-export-consolidated] But the same circular then drops the customs paperwork for services sent over the internet. If you deliver software or data-processing work online instead of on a disk, the EXP procedure does not apply. The payment is handled as an inward remittance, and the bank collects its purpose and supporting record digitally. Bangladesh Bank discontinued Form-C and Form-C (ICT) in the same July 2026 circular.[^bb-export-consolidated]

**What this means for you.** The online-service exemption is from the EXP procedure and physical customs paperwork, not from exporter registration. A service delivered over the internet has no customs checkpoint, but the 2023 order still requires registration and Bangladesh Bank tells banks to check it. A branch's operational practice cannot waive that rule.

> **Keep in mind:** Apply for the ERC before treating service export as a regular line of business. The registration fee is BDT 10,000 plus VAT. Ask your bank's foreign-exchange desk which digital channel and supporting records it needs to credit your inward remittances, and whether an ERQ account or cash-incentive claim adds any documents. Get that process in writing. The bank's answer can settle its workflow; it does not remove the legal need for the ERC.

## What it costs, every year

The fee table is in the Schedule to the 2023 order. Two facts explain most of it.

**Exporters all pay the same.** No slabs, no ceiling, no link to how much you ship. An ERC costs BDT 10,000 to register and BDT 7,000 to renew. That is true whether you export USD 9,000 a year or USD 9 million.[^reg-order-2023]

**Importers pay by the ceiling they declare.** The IRC fee follows the yearly import value you write on the form. There are eight slabs, from BDT 5,000 up to BDT 80,000. Writing a big ceiling to look serious is not caution. It is a bill you pay again every year.[^reg-order-2023]

Add **15% VAT** to every fee. You pay it on a separate treasury challan. The CCI&E service standard asks for both challans, the fee one and the VAT one, so budget the total and not the number in the table.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023]

### Import registration fees, by declared yearly import value

The slab you land in decides what you pay every year, so this number is worth getting right.

| Declared annual import value (BDT) | Registration fee | Renewal fee, per year |
|---|---|---|
| Up to 5,00,000 | 5,000 | 3,000 |
| 5,00,001 – 25,00,000 | 10,000 | 6,000 |
| 25,00,001 – 50,00,000 | 24,000 | 16,000 |
| 50,00,001 – 1,00,00,000 | 40,000 | 15,000 |
| 1,00,00,001 – 5,00,00,000 | 50,000 | 22,000 |
| 5,00,00,001 – 20,00,00,000 | 60,000 | 24,000 |
| 20,00,00,001 – 50,00,00,000 | 70,000 | 28,000 |
| 50,00,00,001 – 100,00,00,000 or above | 80,000 | 32,000 |

These are the figures printed in the Schedule to the 2023 order. They do not include 15% VAT.[^reg-order-2023] Look at the fourth row. Its renewal fee of BDT 15,000 is lower than the BDT 16,000 in the row above. That is what the gazette says. Check what the online system actually charges you before you count on it.

The third row is a decision point. Once your yearly imports pass BDT 25 lakh, both your registration fee and your renewal fee more than double. If your plan is near that threshold, calculate the fee on both sides before choosing the ceiling on the form.

### Export and indenting fees

There are no slabs on the export side. Just three named services.

| Service | Registration fee | Renewal fee, per year |
|---|---|---|
| Export Registration Certificate (ERC) | 10,000 | 7,000 |
| Export Registration Certificate (indenting service) | 50,000 | 25,000 |
| Multinational ERC | 10,000 | 7,000 |

Fees do not include 15% VAT.[^reg-order-2023] Watch the middle row. If someone tells you to "register as an indentor, it is the same office", they are pointing you at a certificate that costs five times more and licences a different business. Indenting means selling a foreign company's goods for commission. It is not exporting your own product.

### What Tanvir actually pays

He needs both certificates. His hardware imports run about BDT 18 lakh a year, which puts him in the second import slab.

```
ERC registration fee                         10,000
ERC VAT at 15%                                1,500
IRC registration fee (5,00,001-25,00,000)    10,000
IRC VAT at 15%                                1,500
                                            -------
Year one, CCI&E fees                      BDT 23,000

Renewal from year two:
ERC renewal 7,000 + VAT 1,050                 8,050
IRC renewal 6,000 + VAT   900                 6,900
                                            -------
Every following year                      BDT 14,950
```

Bangladesh Bank's four-month rule is the deadline for bringing export proceeds into Bangladesh. It is not a typical buyer-payment term or a promise that payment will arrive after four months. Put the payment dates from Tanvir's signed contract into the model alongside freight, inspection and working capital, then check that the final payment date still leaves him inside the repatriation deadline.[^bb-export-consolidated] The BDT 23,000 registration fee is only one input in a shipment decision worth lakhs.

## What you need before you apply

CCI&E checks the documents required for the application; it does not assess whether the business is commercially promising. Use the list below as a pre-submission check so a missing or mismatched document does not become a preventable application risk.[^reg-order-2023] [^ccie-charter]

1. **A current [trade licence](/en/trade-license) that names the business you are actually doing.** This is the first item Tanvir should check. If the activity on his licence does not match his leather-goods business, he should ask the issuing authority how to update it before filing the CCI&E application.
2. **An <Term name="e-tin">e-TIN</Term>, plus the receipt showing you filed your last income-tax return.** The receipt has been required since 1 July 2022. A TIN on its own is not enough.[^ccie-charter]
3. **A plan for your <Term name="vat-bin">BIN</Term>, not a BIN as an initial CCI&E prerequisite.** The current service standard does not list a BIN for an ordinary first commercial IRC or ERC application. Form-3 makes it compulsory at the first renewal.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023] Your VAT obligations may require one earlier for a different reason; check [VAT and BIN](/en/tax/vat-bin). Do not let the BIN hold up this application unless CCI&E identifies another service-specific requirement in your case.
4. **A nominated bank, and a certificate from it.** You name one branch on the form. That branch handles your import and export transactions from then on. Pick the one that will really do your foreign trade, not the one closest to your house. See [business bank account](/en/payments/bank-account).
5. **Current membership of a recognised chamber of commerce, or a trade body for your line of work.** A leather-goods exporter joins the sector association. A software firm joins something like BASIS. The membership certificate is part of the application, so confirm the current fee, required documents and expected issue date directly with the body before you file.

   > **Keep in mind:** It does not have to be a big national body. In a 2024 Accelerating Bangladesh webinar on export basics, Jahid Hussain of Export Sheba told founders that a **chamber of commerce in your home district** usually charges a small membership fee and is accepted for an ERC application. He put the ERC fee itself at roughly BDT 10,000 to 12,000, close to the BDT 10,000 plus VAT in the gazette.[^ab-export-essentials] The 2023 order backs up the wider option. It asks for a current membership certificate from a recognised chamber **or** a trade association connected to your business.[^reg-order-2023]

6. **Your company papers.** A limited company needs its <Term name="incorp-certificate">certificate of incorporation</Term> and a current Form XII. A partnership needs its partnership deed registered with RJSC. A sole proprietor needs neither. See [private limited registration](/en/registration/private-limited).
7. **The applicant's National ID or passport, and a passport-size photo.**

> **Warning:** Items 1 and 5 come from authorities outside CCI&E, so their timelines are not part of CCI&E's service standard. CCI&E sets a delivery time of **3 working days** once the online application is complete.[^ccie-charter] Confirm the trade-licence and membership timelines with the organisations that issue them, and obtain both documents before you submit.

## What registration does not give you

The certificate lets you trade. It does not make you ready to trade. It does not give you:

- **Customs clearance.** Every shipment still needs its own declaration, <Term name="hs-code">HS code</Term> and documents. See [HS codes](/en/trade/hs-codes) and [trade documents](/en/trade/trade-documents).
- **The right to keep foreign earnings abroad.** Export payments must reach Bangladesh through your bank inside the time limit. See [export proceeds](/en/trade/export-proceeds).
- **A bonded warehouse or duty-free raw material.** That is a separate facility from the National Board of Revenue. See [bonded warehouses](/en/trade/bonded-warehouses).
- **Export cash incentives.** Those run on a different registration. See [EPB registration](/en/trade/epb-registration).
- **Safety from cancellation.** The authority can suspend or cancel your certificate. The listed grounds include breaking the Act or the order, breaking foreign-exchange or anti-money-laundering rules, **under-invoicing or over-invoicing** goods or services, getting the certificate by fraud, making a false declaration, or not being at the address you registered. You get a fair hearing first, and a cancellation notice goes up on the authority's website.[^reg-order-2023]

Read that under-invoicing point twice. Writing an invoice low to cut duty, or high to move money, is not just a paperwork risk. It is a listed reason to lose the certificate your whole business runs on.

## Common mistakes

The first decision is which entity may register; the second is which certificate that entity needs.

| Mistake | What happens | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Thinking "import-export licence" is one thing | You reach customs with an IRC and no ERC, or the reverse, after the goods are packed | Decide ERC, IRC, both or neither before you promise a delivery date |
| Declaring a big import ceiling to look serious | A higher fee slab every year, for imports you never make | Declare what you will import in the next twelve months. You can change it at renewal[^reg-order-2023] |
| Applying before fixing the trade licence | Rejected on documents, then a licence queue on top of that | Fix the trade licence first. It is the slowest item |
| Following a government page that cites the 1981 order | You prepare an attested paper file for an online-only process | Work from the 2023 order and the CCI&E service standard. Skip any page that describes a counter visit |
| Registering as an indentor because it "covers more" | BDT 50,000 plus VAT for an agency licence you cannot use | Register as an exporter, unless you really do sell a foreign company's goods for commission |
| Treating article 4 as a one-certificate rule | You skip a certificate you need, or apply through an entity CCI&E may not register | Identify the establishment that may register, then apply for its IRC, ERC or both[^reg-order-2023] |

Four of these six get decided before you open the form. The expensive errors here are choices, not slips of the pen.

## Checklist

- [ ] Written down which certificate you need: ERC, IRC, both, or neither
- [ ] Checked the one-establishment grouping rule against other businesses in your family and among your shareholders
- [ ] If you export services, included the ERC in your plan and confirmed the bank's digital remittance workflow in writing
- [ ] Trade licence current, and its stated business matches what you trade
- [ ] e-TIN plus the receipt for your latest income-tax return
- [ ] BIN timing planned: by the first renewal, or earlier if your separate VAT obligations require it
- [ ] Nominated bank branch chosen, and its certificate requested
- [ ] Chamber or trade-association membership current
- [ ] Company papers ready: incorporation and Form XII, or a registered partnership deed
- [ ] Import ceiling set honestly, with the fee slab and 15% VAT in your budget
- [ ] Renewal date in a calendar, with a reminder 90 days ahead

**You are done when:** you can say in one sentence which certificate you are applying for and why, name the fee slab and its VAT, and show that all seven items above already exist. After that, submit the online form through CCI&E's Online Licensing Module.[^ccie-charter]
