# How to Apply for an IRC or ERC, Step by Step

> **In short:** Apply online through the Chief Controller of Imports and Exports' OLM system. CCI&E's published service standard sets **3 working days for a complete application**, and the authority may ask for more information or papers while checking it.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023] Before opening the portal, make sure the trade licence names the right business, the association membership is current, and every required document is ready to upload. For renewal, apply **at least 60 days before the certificate expires** or the late fee applies.[^reg-order-2023] [^olm-instructions] Your next step is the preparation list below.

Say Rumana runs a jute and cotton homeware workshop in Narayanganj with six staff. A Dutch retailer has confirmed a first order: 1,200 storage baskets, about USD 14,000, shipping in eleven weeks. Her freight forwarder asked for her <Term name="erc">ERC</Term> number. She does not have one.

She has ten weeks of production ahead of her, and she thinks the certificate is the risky bit. CCI&E publishes a three-working-day standard once an application is complete, but Rumana's file is not complete.[^ccie-charter] Her <Term name="trade-license">trade licence</Term> was issued for "handicraft retail" and says nothing about export. She needs to correct it before submitting a matching file.

---

## Before you start: three things to check

This page assumes you already know **which** certificate you need. If you do not, read [import and export registration](/en/trade/import-export-registration) first. An ERC and an <Term name="irc">IRC</Term> are separate, cost different amounts, and the fee is not refundable if you apply for the wrong one.

Check these three before you begin:

1. **You know which establishment may register.** Article 4 of the 2023 order groups businesses with the same owner, family or shareholders and permits only one establishment in the listed cases. It does not stop that registered establishment from holding both an IRC and an ERC when it imports and exports.[^reg-order-2023]
2. **Your trade licence names this business.** This is Rumana's blocker. If the licence describes a different business, correct it before opening OLM.
3. **Your last income-tax return is filed and you have the receipt.** OLM checks the return online before it will complete a renewal, and asks you to upload the certificate or return slip as proof.[^olm-instructions]

> **Warning:** OLM says clearly that fees are not refundable if you pay them against the wrong service. It also asks you not to pay twice if money has already been taken for the same service.[^olm-instructions] Read the service name on the screen before you create a challan.

## Step 0: prepare the files before you log in

Every scanned document below needs to be a **colour scan, JPG or PDF, under 2 MB, at 100 DPI**, ready before you log in. One row is not a scan: you generate and pay the fee and VAT challans inside the application itself, at step 4, and the challan copy then becomes one of the documents you attach. OLM's Bangla note says scan in colour and its English note says grayscale. CCI&E's service standard asks for colour copies, so scan in colour and you satisfy both.[^olm-instructions] [^ccie-charter]

| Document | Who needs it | Where you get it |
|---|---|---|
| Trade licence, current, naming the right business | Everyone | City corporation or municipality |
| <Term name="incorp-certificate">Certificate of incorporation</Term> **and** current Form XII | Limited companies | RJSC |
| Registered partnership deed | Partnership firms | RJSC |
| <Term name="e-tin">e-TIN</Term> or income-tax certificate, **plus** the return receipt | Everyone | NBR |
| Nominated bank's certificate (or a no-objection letter if you are switching banks) | Everyone | Your bank branch |
| Current membership certificate from a chamber or trade association | Everyone | Your chamber or association |
| National ID or passport of the applicant | Everyone | — |
| Fee amount and VAT amount worked out in advance | Everyone | You create the challans inside the application at step 4, not before |
| Agency agreement, principal's name and country, commission rate | Indenting service only | Your foreign principal |
| Factory and office address, bank certificate, fire licence, raw-material list with HS codes | Industrial IRC only | Your sponsor authority and the fire service |

Sources: the Schedule and Annexure-1 to the 2023 order, and services 01 and 15 of the CCI&E service standard.[^reg-order-2023] [^ccie-charter]

Notice what is not in the ordinary first-application rows: a BIN. The current service standard does not list one for a new commercial IRC or ERC, while Form-3 makes it compulsory at the first renewal.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023] Your separate VAT obligations may require it earlier, but it is not an automatic blocker to this initial CCI&E application.

The table splits in two. The first seven rows are the ordinary company file you may keep already. The last two rows require documents from outside your company. An industrial IRC needs a recommendation from a sponsoring authority and a fire licence at the factory address. An indenting ERC needs a signed agreement with a foreign supplier. Confirm those documents before opening OLM.

> **Keep in mind:** In a 2024 Accelerating Bangladesh webinar on export basics, Jahid Hussain of Export Sheba gave founders an order to work in. Trade licence first, then e-TIN, then the bank account, then one association membership (a local chamber of commerce is enough), then the ERC, then the BIN. He treated EPB and product-specific registrations as later steps rather than blockers to a first shipment.[^ab-export-essentials] That is one practitioner's sequence, not a legal rule. But it matches what the form actually asks you to upload, and it is cheaper than starting with a national sector body.

**Rumana's gaps** take four lines to list. Incorporation and Form XII, yes. e-TIN and return receipt, yes. Bank certificate, still to collect. Trade licence, wrong business. Association membership, does not exist. Three items need action. None of them is fixed inside OLM.

## Step 1: open the OLM account

Go to **olm.ccie.gov.bd** and choose **New Account / ID**. You register once and use the same account for every future service and renewal.

Every login sends a one-time password to the mobile number on your profile.[^olm-instructions] Register with a number the firm controls. If you use a staff member's number and they leave, future login codes will still go to that number.

---

## Step 2: choose the service and the office

Pick the registration type from the left menu. Two choices decide whether your file reaches the right desk.

- **Service.** New registration, renewal and re-registration are separate menu items. Rumana wants a new Export Registration Certificate, not a renewal.
- **Regional office.** Besides the head office, CCI&E runs fourteen regional offices: Dhaka, Chattogram, Khulna, Rajshahi, Barishal, Sylhet, Mymensingh, Cumilla, Bogura, Rangpur, Sirajganj, Pabna, Dinajpur and Naogaon.[^ccie-charter] Pick the one that covers your registered address.

For an **industrial IRC** there is an extra rule, and it catches anyone reviving an old certificate. If the certificate has been unrenewed for **three years or more**, choose the *Office of the Chief Controller of Imports and Exports, Dhaka*. If it is under three years, choose the *Office of the Controller of Imports and Exports, Dhaka*.[^olm-instructions]

Two kinds of application go to the head office for approval before a regional office issues the certificate: first ad-hoc industrial IRCs, and multinational ERCs. The exception is applicants under the Dhaka or Chattogram regional offices, which approve and issue these themselves.[^ccie-charter]

---

## Step 3: fill in the form

Required fields have a red asterisk, and the form will not submit without them.[^olm-instructions] The ERC application is Form-2 of the 2023 order, and it asks for:[^reg-order-2023]

- **The firm:** name, TIN, address in Bangla and English, firm name in Bangla and English, mobile, phone, firm type, division and district, an authorised contact person with their number, and the domestic shareholding percentage
- **The owner:** name, present and permanent address, personal TIN, phone, National ID or passport number, and a passport-size photo
- **The nominated bank:** bank name, branch name, branch address
- **The trade licence:** number, issue date, expiry date, who issued it, and the type of business
- **The agency agreement:** principal's name and country, agreement number, commission rate and term. Indenting service only
- **The fee:** amount, challan number and date, the bank and branch where you paid, and the deposit code

Under **Owner Information**, click *Add New Person / Owner*. Choose your **Organization Type** first, then add every partner or director your entity type requires before you move on.[^olm-instructions]

> **Keep in mind:** Type the firm name in English exactly as it appears on your incorporation certificate or trade licence. Do not use a short trading name. Your bank will match the ERC against your export declaration and your invoice. A name that differs by one word creates a query at the worst possible time.

Finish by ticking the undertaking box. Tick **Draft** and click *Save as Draft* if you want to come back later. Leave Draft unticked and click *Save and Submit* to file it now.[^olm-instructions]

---

## Step 4: pay the fee and the VAT as two challans

Inside the application, use the **Fees and VAT** control to add your payment through a **T.R. Challan**. The fee and the 15% VAT are two separate deposits, and the service standard asks for both challans.[^ccie-charter] [^reg-order-2023]

For Rumana's new ERC:

```
ERC registration fee (same for all exporters)  10,000
VAT at 15% of the fee                           1,500
                                              -------
Total to pay                               BDT 11,500
```

Two rules matter here. **Manual challans are not accepted.** OLM says so in its own renewal notice. The challan is created in the system and paid online.[^olm-instructions] The CCI&E service standard describes the same flow for both new registration and renewal: apply first, then pay online against your OLM reference.[^ccie-charter]

An exporter pays the same fee whether they ship USD 14,000 or USD 14 million. An importer does not. The IRC fee follows the yearly import ceiling you declare, across eight slabs from BDT 5,000 to BDT 80,000 before VAT.[^reg-order-2023] The full table is on [import and export registration](/en/trade/import-export-registration). Declare the ceiling you will actually use, because you pay against it every year.

---

## Step 5: upload, submit, and track the application

Upload every document with a red asterisk, tick the undertaking, and submit. Once your application is complete, CCI&E's service standard sets **3 working days** for both new registration and renewal. The certificate arrives in the system. You do not collect it in person.[^ccie-charter]

Three working days applies to a complete file. The clock does not run while the office waits for a document you forgot to attach, and the authority can ask for more information or papers while it checks your file.[^reg-order-2023]

---

## Step 6: check the certificate before you file it away

When the certificate shows up in your account, check four things.

1. **Language.** The authority issues the certificate online **in Bangla, English, or both, if you ask**.[^reg-order-2023] Ask for both. Your buyer's bank, your freight forwarder and any future investor will want the English one, and asking now is free.
2. **The firm name and address** match your trade licence and incorporation certificate exactly.
3. **The registration number**, which is what your forwarder and bank will quote.
4. **The expiry date.** Registration lasts **one year from the date it was issued**. Not to the end of the financial year, and not to the end of the calendar year.[^reg-order-2023]

**You are done when:** you have the certificate downloaded in both languages, the registration number saved where you can send it in one message, and a calendar reminder set as described in step 7.

---

## Step 7: the renewal trap

The late-fee risk starts before the expiry date.

> **Rule:** Apply to renew **at least 60 days before the certificate expires**. Miss that window and you can still apply, but you pay the late fee set out in the Schedule to the 2023 order.[^reg-order-2023] OLM says the same thing on its own front page. To renew an IRC or ERC without a penalty, apply at least 60 days before expiry.[^olm-instructions]

The penalty-free window closes 60 days before the date printed on your certificate. Set a reminder for **90 days before expiry**. That leaves 30 days to submit before the cutoff; confirm that OLM records the application before the deadline.

Form-3 asks for your last registration or renewal tracking number, the firm's details, the expiry date, the term you want, the TIN, and the **<Term name="vat-bin">BIN</Term>**, your VAT number, which is **compulsory from your first renewal**.[^reg-order-2023] Attach the fee and VAT challan, the receipt for your latest income-tax return, and anything else that has changed since last time.[^reg-order-2023] [^ccie-charter]

Two choices are worth thinking about:

- **Renew for one to five years.** The authority can renew for up to five years at a time, and OLM confirms you can pick any whole term from one to five.[^reg-order-2023] [^olm-instructions] A longer term removes four chances to forget. The Schedule prices renewal "for each one year", so check what the system charges for a multi-year term before you pay.
- **Change your import ceiling at renewal, not in between.** OLM says the import limit can only go up or down once the renewal for the current financial year is done.[^olm-instructions] If you expect to cross a slab, plan the change into your renewal.

### Late fees, as gazetted

The Schedule sets the late fee by how long the registration has been unrenewed and what kind of registration it is.

| How long unrenewed | Importer (commercial and industrial) | Exporter | Exporter (indenting) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 to 3 years | 2,000 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| 4 to 5 years | 5,000 | 4,000 | 5,000 |
| 6 years or more | 25,000 | 10,000 | 25,000 |

Figures in taka, from the Schedule to the 2023 order.[^reg-order-2023]

The late fee is not the only risk of a lapse. While your certificate is expired, you cannot get a Bill of Export. That can leave finished goods and working capital tied up while a buyer waits. One more thing to note: the Schedule's brackets are written in years, but the 60-day rule triggers the fee straight away. Check what OLM actually charges rather than assuming a short overshoot is free.

## If your application is refused

The authority can refuse an application after checking your documents. It must **write down the reason and tell you**.[^reg-order-2023] When you read that reason, check the trade licence, tax-return receipt, association membership, and the service named on the paid challan against your application.

Correct a missing or mismatched document and apply again. If you think the refusal itself is wrong, the 2023 order sends you to review, appeal and revision under the Review, Appeal and Revision Order, 1977 (S.R.O. No. 313-L/77 of 10 October 1977).[^reg-order-2023] Consider professional advice before choosing that route.

## Is there another door? BanglaBiz

Since **1 February 2026**, BIDA's BanglaBiz portal has brought more than twenty common business approvals behind one login and one business ID. Its published list includes import and export registration certificates, along with VAT registration, environmental clearances and fire-safety licences.[^bida-banglabiz] OLM now carries a banner pointing to BanglaBiz.[^olm-instructions]

What has not changed: CCI&E's service standard still describes applying, paying and getting the certificate through OLM, and OLM is where your certificate and renewal history live.[^ccie-charter] Check BanglaBiz, especially if you also need a trade licence or an e-TIN. Those two sit in its starter package, alongside name clearance, a temporary bank account and incorporation. VAT registration is on BanglaBiz too, but as one of the separately integrated approvals rather than part of that package. Treat OLM as the record. If the two disagree about a fee or a document, the gazette and the service standard win.

## Cost and time in one place

Everything you pay, and everything you wait for.

| Item | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ERC registration fee | BDT 10,000 + 15% VAT | Same for everyone, whatever you export[^reg-order-2023] |
| ERC renewal fee | BDT 7,000 + 15% VAT, per year | Renewable for one to five years[^reg-order-2023] |
| IRC registration fee | BDT 5,000 to 80,000 + 15% VAT | Depends on the import ceiling you declare[^reg-order-2023] |
| Indenting ERC | BDT 50,000 + 15% VAT | A different business, not a bigger ERC[^reg-order-2023] |
| CCI&E processing | 3 working days | Once your online application is complete[^ccie-charter] |
| Trade licence amendment | Varies by city corporation | Check the current process with the issuer before opening OLM |
| Chamber or association membership | Varies by body | Has its own fee and waiting time |

Only the CCI&E row has an official processing standard here. The trade-licence issuer and membership body set their own timing, so confirm both before opening OLM.

## Common mistakes

Check this table before you submit an application or choose a renewal term.

| Mistake | What happens | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| Opening OLM before the documents exist | A half-finished draft sits there while you chase a licence | Finish step 0 first. The portal is the last step, not the first |
| Paying a challan against the wrong service | You do not get the money back, by OLM's own notice[^olm-instructions] | Read the service name on screen before creating the challan |
| Uploading a 6 MB phone photo of a licence | The upload fails, or the document is rejected as unreadable | Colour scan, 100 DPI, JPG or PDF, under 2 MB[^olm-instructions] |
| Registering with a staff member's mobile number | Every future login OTP goes to someone who has left | Use a number the firm controls |
| Taking the certificate in Bangla only | A buyer's bank or an investor asks for an English copy and you have to go back | Ask for both languages when it is issued[^reg-order-2023] |
| Setting the renewal reminder for the expiry date | A late fee, plus a gap where you cannot ship | Set it for 90 days before expiry |
| Renewing for one year out of habit | Four more chances to forget | Think about a three- or five-year term |

These mistakes can delay the application, add a fee, or interrupt a shipment. Use the right-hand column as a final check before you submit or renew.

## Checklist

- [ ] Decided ERC, IRC, both, or indenting, and checked which establishment may register under article 4
- [ ] Trade licence current, and its stated business matches what you trade
- [ ] Incorporation certificate and Form XII, or registered partnership deed, scanned
- [ ] e-TIN plus the receipt for your latest income-tax return, scanned
- [ ] Nominated bank certificate in hand
- [ ] Chamber or trade-association membership current
- [ ] National ID or passport and passport-size photo ready
- [ ] Every file in colour, JPG or PDF, 100 DPI, under 2 MB
- [ ] OLM account opened on a phone number the firm controls
- [ ] Correct service and regional office chosen before you create any challan
- [ ] Fee challan and separate 15% VAT challan paid online
- [ ] Certificate downloaded in both Bangla and English
- [ ] Renewal reminder set for 90 days before expiry, with the term you plan to take
