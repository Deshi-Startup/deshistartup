# Step-by-Step Roadmap: Choose the Next Stage of Work

> **In short:** Use the roadmap to connect decisions across the business. Start at the stage that matches your current uncertainty; you can revisit an earlier stage whenever the evidence changes.

| Stage | Use it when | Main output |
|---|---|---|
| [Validate](/en/roadmap/validate) | You need evidence about a customer problem or offer | A test result and a reasoned next decision |
| [Build](/en/roadmap/build) | You need to deliver the first useful version reliably | A working delivery process and clear responsibilities |
| [Grow](/en/roadmap/grow) | You have operating evidence and want to improve repeatable sales | A bounded growth test with cash and capacity checks |
| [Scale](/en/roadmap/scale) | More people and complexity require stronger management | Owners, controls and a review rhythm |

These are working stages, not a requirement to raise investment, expand geographically or follow a fixed timetable. A growing business may need to validate a new offer again.

## Choose this week's outcome

Write your largest unresolved decision. Pick the matching stage, choose one task and record who will do it and when the result will be reviewed. Keep a short note of the evidence and the next action.

Before launching an activity, check the relevant permissions and risks through the [legal roadmap](/en/legal-roadmap). Legal, safety and recordkeeping work can matter at every stage; they are not a final box after testing.

For a single practical goal, such as testing demand or setting up payments, use [guided journeys](/en/journeys). For one subject, use [Browse by Topic](/en/guides).
