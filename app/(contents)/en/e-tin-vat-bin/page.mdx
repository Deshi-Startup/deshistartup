# TIN vs BIN in Bangladesh: Which Document Do You Need?

> **In short:** An <Term name="e-tin">e-TIN</Term> identifies an income-tax payer. A <Term name="vat-bin">BIN</Term> identifies a business in the VAT system. Neither proves that you have filed a return. Start by asking which document is required, whose name it must carry and which tax period it covers.

If a bank, payment gateway or business customer asks for “tax papers,” ask them to name the document. A TIN certificate, proof of return submission and a VAT registration certificate answer different questions.

## What Is the Difference Between TIN and BIN?

Use this comparison to identify the document before starting an application. NBR issues a TIN under the income-tax system; VAT registration and turnover-tax enlistment have their own certificate and BIN.[^income-tax-return-rules][^vat-registration-rules]

| Document | What it tells you | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| e-TIN certificate | The taxpayer's identity and income-tax number | That a return was filed or tax was paid |
| Proof of return submission | That the named taxpayer submitted a return for a stated assessment year | That a separate company's return was filed |
| BIN / Mushak-2.3 certificate | The business's VAT registration or turnover-tax enlistment status | That its periodic VAT or turnover-tax returns are up to date |

**VAT is the tax; BIN is the identification number.** Read the status on the certificate rather than treating every BIN holder as fully VAT-registered. Registration and enlistment carry different invoicing and return duties.[^vat-registration-rules]

## Match the Request to Your Situation

- **You need a taxpayer number:** use the [e-TIN application guide](/en/tax/e-tin). Decide whose TIN is needed before applying.
- **A bank asks for proof of filing:** check the requested assessment year and supply the return acknowledgment or other accepted proof. Downloading your TIN certificate again does not answer that request.
- **You have incorporated a company:** distinguish its tax identity from the founders' personal identities. Use [personal vs company TIN](/en/tax/personal-vs-company-tin).
- **A customer asks for a VAT invoice:** first check your registration status. A fully registered business uses [Mushak-6.3](/en/tax/mushak-63); turnover-tax enlistment has a different invoice.
- **You are deciding whether VAT registration is required:** check both your business activity and turnover. Use the [registration conditions](/en/tax/when-vat-required) and [rolling twelve-month turnover examples](/en/tax/vat-thresholds).

Do not apply for a certificate simply because its name appears in a generic document checklist. Confirm the requirement for your legal structure and activity, then understand the duties that follow.

## Example: The Certificate Is Correct, but It Is the Wrong Document

Suppose a founder gives a bank her e-TIN certificate. The bank asked for proof of return submission for a particular assessment year. Her next action is to find the matching filing acknowledgment, or address the missing filing, rather than create another TIN.

Section 166 of the Income Tax Act, 2023 sets out return-filing requirements; section 264 defines proof of submission for specified transactions.[^income-tax-return-rules] Check the year and taxpayer name on the requested proof. For the filing workflow, read [submitting income-tax returns](/en/tax/filing-returns).

## Before You Apply or Send a Document

- [ ] I know the exact certificate or proof being requested.
- [ ] I have checked whether it belongs to me personally or to the company.
- [ ] The name and identification number match the underlying records.
- [ ] Any requested return proof covers the correct assessment year or tax period.
- [ ] I know which returns, invoices and records the registration requires afterward.

For a BIN application, follow [VAT/BIN registration](/en/tax/vat-bin). Once registered, keep sales, purchases, refunds and payment settlements in records you can reconcile. The certificate is the start of that workflow.
