# Growth: Test Expansion Before Committing

> **In short:** Growth means serving more customers while keeping delivery, cash and customer outcomes workable. First identify what is limiting the business; expansion is only one possible response.

## Choose the next decision

- **Existing customers do not return:** investigate the experience using [cohort retention](/en/metrics/cohort-retention) before increasing acquisition spend.
- **Sales grow but cash becomes tighter:** examine [cash flow versus profit](/en/metrics/cashflow-vs-profit) and payment timing.
- **A new city looks promising:** use the [city launch playbook](/en/growth/city-launch-playbook) to design a bounded launch.
- **You are considering another country:** begin with [cross-border rules](/en/growth/cross-border-rules) and check the requirements for the specific activity and destination.

## Write an expansion test

Choose one customer group, area or channel. Record the expected demand, delivery capacity, spending limit, person responsible and review date. Decide in advance what would justify continuing and what would make you stop or revise the test.

Compare contribution per completed sale, complaints, repeat behaviour and cash collection with your existing operation. Extra orders alone cannot show whether the expansion works.

Your output is a decision memo: **what we tried / what happened / what it cost / what remains uncertain / next decision**. For the wider operating transition, use the [growth stage roadmap](/en/roadmap/grow).
