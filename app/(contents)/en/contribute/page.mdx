# How to Contribute

> **In short:** You do not need to apply to report a problem, fix a page or write a new guide.
> Submit the work directly from your browser. To contribute regularly, [apply to join the Deshi
> Startup contributor team](https://forms.gle/PfaiTxBQSVzciZQU9).

Rules, fees and lived experience change. Someone who has just completed a process will often
notice something that an older notice or desk-research pass missed. Even a small correction helps
keep the manual trustworthy.

Choose the simplest path:

| What you have | How to help |
|---|---|
| You spotted a problem | Use **Report a mistake** |
| You know exactly what the sentence or fact should say | Press **Edit** |
| You know the topic or are prepared to research it | Complete a **to be written** page |
| You want to contribute regularly | [Apply to join the contributor team](https://forms.gle/PfaiTxBQSVzciZQU9) |

## Join the contributor team

You do not need a form to work on one or two pages. Submit those contributions directly. If you
want to contribute regularly or take ongoing responsibility for an area, you can join the team.

1. [Complete the short application](https://forms.gle/PfaiTxBQSVzciZQU9).
2. We will review it and email you about a first task that fits your experience, interests and time.
3. Start with that task. Consistent contributors can later take on review or section ownership.

## 1. Report a problem

Press **Report a mistake** if a page has a wrong fact, stale fee, broken link or unclear
explanation. Describe what looks wrong and why. Add corrected information or an official source if
you have one, but report the problem even if you do not. The form opens on GitHub, which may ask
you to sign in before submitting it.

## 2. Make a small fix

1. Press **Edit** on the page.
2. Sign in with Google. No GitHub account is required.
3. Fix the text. If needed, use **Add image** for a PNG, JPEG or WebP.
4. Add a one-line summary of what changed and why.
5. Press **Send for review**.

The site creates a pull request. Nothing publishes before review. A contributed image stays in
private quarantine until a reviewer approves that image separately.

If you prefer GitHub, use **Edit on GitHub** at the bottom of the page.

## 3. Complete an unwritten page

[Browse all guides](/en/guides) for topics marked **to be written**. If there is a matching GitHub
issue, comment that you are working on it before you start. This helps two people avoid
unknowingly writing the same guide.

Before drafting, answer three questions:

1. Who needs this information, and in what situation?
2. What decision or action should the reader be able to take after reading?
3. Where could wrong advice cost the reader money, time or create legal risk?

Not every guide needs identical sections. Keep only what helps the topic. This is a useful
starting shape:

```text
# Title

> **In short:** the main answer in 3–4 lines.

## Who needs this, and when
## Step by step
## Costs and time
## Common mistakes
## Checklist
## Relevant Sources
```

Remove the **to be written** notice only when the guide is genuinely complete. You may submit
partial work, but do not mark it as finished.

## Six checks before submitting

- Research from official or primary sources; do not turn guesses into facts.
- Write the Bengali source directly in natural Bangla rather than translating an English draft.
- Explain who needs the answer, where to go, what to bring and what to do next.
- Put sources close to legal, fee, threshold and regulatory claims.
- Date fees and other figures that can change, or give the verification date.
- Do not copy other people's writing; teach the idea in your own words and cite it.

Use [STYLE.md](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/blob/main/STYLE.md) for Bangla examples
and [EDITORIAL.md](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/blob/main/EDITORIAL.md) for
research, teaching and evidence.

## Review and license

Reviewers check facts and sources, whether the next action is clear, possible harm and copyright
in both text and images. Legal, tax and high-risk financial content may need expert review.
Feedback should be about the page, not the person who wrote it.

By contributing writing, you agree to publish it under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
