# How to Contribute

Deshi Startup is free for everyone, and the guides are built by the community. You can make a
one-time correction or work with us regularly - start with the option that fits you.

## Join the contributor team

Join the team if you want to work regularly on research, writing, translation, editing, or a
subject you know well. We will match your experience and interests with work the project currently
needs.

<ContributionInvite locale="en" kind="team" />

You do not need this form to fix a page or send a one-time contribution.

## Contribute to a page

You do not need to apply before making a one-time contribution.

- **Report a problem:** use **Report a mistake** when you find outdated information, a broken link,
  or an unclear explanation. Tell us what looks wrong even if you do not have the answer yet.
- **Improve a page:** press **Edit**, sign in with Google, make your change, and press **Send for
  review**. You do not need a GitHub account.
- **Write a new guide:** [browse unwritten topics](/en/guides) and start with one you know well. You
  can send an incomplete draft for review.
- **Suggest a new topic:** if it is not on the site, use the [short GitHub form](https://github.com/Deshi-Startup/deshistartup/issues/new?template=new-topic.yml)
  to tell us what the topic is and who needs it.

## Share your work or resources

This option is especially for founders, operators, creators, consultants, researchers, and subject
specialists. If you have a published article, report, video, podcast, source collection, or a
practical lesson relevant to building a startup in Bangladesh, tell us about it.

<ContributionInvite locale="en" kind="share" />

We will not be able to publish everything. If there is a fit, we will discuss where the material
belongs, what else we need, and how permission and credit should work. If the work belongs to
someone else, send the original link; we will not copy or translate it without permission.

## Review, permission, and credit

Nothing publishes automatically. A reviewer checks the facts, sources, risks, and copyright first.
If we use your work, we agree in advance on how to credit you, your organization, or the original
publication.

Final content is published under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Once a contribution is accepted, the contributor's public name may appear on the
[contributors page](/en/contributors). The [privacy policy](/en/privacy) explains how to request a
rename or removal.
