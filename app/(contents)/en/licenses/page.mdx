# Licenses and Approvals: Check Your Actual Activity

> **In short:** There is no single permission checklist for every startup. Describe what you will do, where and for whom, then check the requirements that apply before beginning the activity.

## Prepare an applicability brief

Write the business activity, location, premises, products, production process and any handling of food, health, money or other sensitive services. Include whether you manufacture, store, import or only supply software. A broad label such as “tech startup” is not enough to identify requirements.

| Question | Starting guide |
|---|---|
| Which local business category should I investigate? | [Trade license category](/en/licenses/trade-license-category) |
| Does the premises need a fire-safety approval? | [Fire license](/en/licenses/fire-license) |
| Does the activity require environmental clearance? | [Environmental clearance](/en/licenses/environmental-clearance) |
| Will we produce or handle food? | [Food safety](/en/licenses/food-safety) |
| Are we setting up a factory? | [Factory license](/en/licenses/factory-license) |
| Are existing documents due for renewal? | [Trade license renewal](/en/licenses/trade-license-renewal) |

These are questions to investigate, not a claim that every approval applies to you or that this list is exhaustive.

## Leave with a record, not a guess

For each possible requirement, record **authority / applicability confirmed or unresolved / official source and date / documents / owner / next action**. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority using your activity brief. Keep unresolved permissions visible in the launch plan.

Use the [legal roadmap](/en/legal-roadmap) to connect these checks with structure, tax, contracts and ongoing duties.
