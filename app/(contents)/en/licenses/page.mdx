# Licenses and Approvals: Check Your Actual Activity

> **In short:** There is no single permission checklist for every startup. Describe what you will do, where and for whom, then check the requirements that apply before beginning the activity.

## Example: describe the activity before choosing a category

An **issuing authority** is the body responsible for the particular approval. The portal taking an application may be a different service. A **trade license** and company incorporation are separate administrative processes; local trade licensing is handled by the relevant city corporation, municipality or union parishad.[^license-authority]

A fictional founder plans to sell packaged food from premises in a municipal area. Writing only “online business” would omit food handling and storage. The founder instead prepares this brief:

```text
Activity: preparing, packing and selling food, not merely running a website.
Premises: exact operating and storage address, including whether they differ.
Process: ingredients received, preparation, packaging, storage, dispatch.
Questions: local license category; food-related requirements; premises safety.
Evidence held: premises documents and a written process description.
Next step: obtain the current checklist from the responsible local authority
and identify the bodies responsible for the food/premises questions.
```

The founder records **local category: awaiting confirmation** and **food requirements: unresolved**. They do not mark an approval “not applicable” because a competitor has not mentioned it. If the business only supplies software to food sellers, that is a different activity description and needs its own assessment.

This is how an applicability record changes the launch: an unresolved requirement has an owner and must be resolved before the affected activity, rather than disappearing behind a general “paperwork done” label. Use official requirements for your specific activity; this example is not a complete food-business licensing list.

## Prepare an applicability brief

Write the business activity, location, premises, products, production process and any handling of food, health, money or other sensitive services. Include whether you manufacture, store, import or only supply software. A broad label such as “tech startup” is not enough to identify requirements.

These are questions to investigate, not a claim that every approval applies to you or that this list is exhaustive.

## Leave with a record, not a guess

For each possible requirement, record **authority / applicability confirmed or unresolved / official source and date / documents / owner / next action**. Confirm current requirements with the relevant authority using your activity brief. Keep unresolved permissions visible in the launch plan.
