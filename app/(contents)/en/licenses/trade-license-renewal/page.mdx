# Trade Licence Fees and Renewal: Who Actually Sets the Number

> **In short:** A city corporation cannot set the trade licence fee at whatever it likes. The government's model tax schedule fixes a maximum annual tax for each class of business, and no rate above it may be imposed.[^kor-tofsil] For a limited company the ceiling follows paid-up capital, not what the business does — between Tk 10 lakh and Tk 25 lakh of capital, the maximum is Tk 4,500 a year.[^kor-tofsil] Renewal is annual, but the fee may be collected for up to five years at once, with any shortfall or excess adjusted later.[^kor-tofsil] And an unpaid fee is not an ordinary bill: taxes and fees imposed by a city corporation are recoverable as a public demand.[^cc-act]

What a trade licence is, who needs one, and how to get the first one is in [Trade licence](/en/trade-license). This page is about the money and the renewal cycle.

---

## Where the number comes from

The figure is built in three stages, each in different hands.

**One, the Act permits it.** Under the Local Government (City Corporation) Act 2009, a corporation may — with the government's prior approval, and by regulation — impose any or all of the taxes, rates, tolls, and fees described in the Fourth Schedule.[^cc-act] That Fourth Schedule includes fees for licences, approvals, and permissions granted by the corporation.[^cc-act] Everything imposed must be published in the official gazette.[^cc-act]

**Two, the government sets a ceiling.** The Act requires the government to make a model tax schedule, which corporations follow as the pattern when imposing a tax or fee.[^cc-act] That schedule is the City Corporation Model Tax Schedule 2016, and it applies to every city corporation.[^kor-tofsil] It says plainly that no tax may be imposed at a rate higher than the one in its table.[^kor-tofsil]

**Three, the corporation sets its own rate.** What is charged under the ceiling is the corporation's business. The schedule measures the size of a business establishment in square feet, and corporations send proposals on how size is determined for each class of business to the Local Government Division, applying them once approved.[^kor-tofsil]

So do not budget against a number from a blog post. Find the ceiling for your class in the tables below, then confirm your own corporation's current rate.

## For a limited company, the fee follows the capital

This is the least-known part. Once you have registered a company, the trade licence class is not decided by what your business does — it is decided by paid-up capital.[^kor-tofsil] Software company or trading house, it is the same table:

| Paid-up capital | Maximum annual tax |
|---|---|
| Up to Tk 1 lakh | Tk 1,500 |
| Tk 1 lakh to Tk 5 lakh | Tk 2,000 |
| Tk 5 lakh to Tk 10 lakh | Tk 3,500 |
| Tk 10 lakh to Tk 25 lakh | Tk 4,500 |
| Tk 25 lakh to Tk 50 lakh | Tk 5,500 |
| Tk 50 lakh to Tk 1 crore | Tk 7,500 |
| Tk 1 crore to Tk 5 crore | Tk 10,000 |
| Above Tk 5 crore | Tk 12,000 |

From the table under section 10 of the City Corporation Model Tax Schedule 2016, serial 1.[^kor-tofsil] These are ceilings, not final bills.

A practical decision follows. How much paid-up capital to register with is not only a question of RJSC fees and bank paperwork — the ceiling on your trade licence moves with that number every year. Read [Share capital, directors, and shareholders](/en/registration/shares-and-directors) before fixing it.

## Without a company, the fee follows the trade

For a sole proprietorship or partnership the table is different. Serials 7 to 32 of the schedule cover manufacturers and distributors, and apply only where the business is not a limited company.[^kor-tofsil] This part of the table carries two columns — one rate for Dhaka North, Dhaka South, and Chattogram, another for the remaining city corporations.[^kor-tofsil]

Some examples:

| Type of business | Dhaka North, Dhaka South, Chattogram | Other city corporations |
|---|---|---|
| Importers of all kinds | Tk 2,000 | Tk 1,000 |
| Exporters of all kinds | Tk 1,000 | Tk 1,000 |
| Distributors of all kinds | Tk 2,000 | Tk 1,000 |
| Travelling agent | Tk 2,000 | Tk 2,000 |
| Clearing and forwarding agent | Tk 2,500 | Tk 1,500 |
| Builders, developers, and real estate | Tk 6,000 | Tk 4,000 |
| Third-class contractor | Tk 2,000 | Tk 1,000 |

The schedule holds many more classes — banks and financial institutions, educational institutions and coaching centres, and mills and factories of various kinds.[^kor-tofsil] Which serial your work falls under has its own guide in [Choosing the trade licence category by city corporation](/en/licenses/trade-license-category).

## Renewal: two different routes across Dhaka

The two corporations describe different procedures in their citizen charters, and the difference matters.

**Dhaka North.** Renewal goes through the corporation's e-revenue portal using the existing user account. On a first application there, the manual licence has to be uploaded in the prescribed format. The licence and advertisement supervisor verifies it and sends it up for approval. Once approved, an SMS and an email ask for the fee; after payment the printed licence is posted or couriered, and the holder can also log in and print it. The corporation puts the time at two working days.[^dncc]

**Dhaka South.** Here a renewal challan book comes with the licence book itself. Renewal runs year by year, by depositing the prescribed fee at the relevant branch of Sonali Bank, and the corporation puts the time at one day.[^dscc] There is also a separate online application link.[^dscc]

One thing holds in both places: renewal is the act of paying a fee, not making the whole application again. But if the address, the type of business, or the ownership has changed, it stops being a plain renewal and picks up a correction as well.

For a new licence, Dhaka South asks for an application on the prescribed form 'ka' with three photographs, the rent agreement and rent receipt, and the tax payment receipt. For a limited company, a copy of the Memorandum goes with it. The licence supervisor then inspects the premises, and the licence is issued if everything checks out — three days, or seven if the health or another department needs to inspect.[^dscc]

## One year, or five at once

The model tax schedule contains an option most founders do not know about. The trade licence fee, though annual, may be collected together for up to five years, and if the amount collectible over that period turns out to be more or less, it is adjusted afterwards.[^kor-tofsil]

Who is it useful for? Anyone who loses time to the June–July scramble each year, and anyone whose licence expiry keeps holding up bank or payment-gateway document checks. Paying several years in one go removes that friction.

Who should skip it? Anyone planning to change capital or class of business. A change of class changes the rate, and then the adjustment has to be worked out. Ask your corporation's revenue department exactly how that adjustment is made.

## If you let it lapse

Late payment costs you in two ways.

The first is the corporation's own late fee or surcharge, which varies by area and by the regulations in force. The second comes from the Act: all taxes, rates, tolls, and fees imposed by a city corporation are recoverable as a public demand.[^cc-act] An unpaid licence fee does not simply sit there as an old invoice.

If you dispute the assessment, the Act provides a route: an objection must be raised before the prescribed authority in the prescribed manner.[^cc-act] So if the fee looks wrong, object in writing rather than saying nothing.

In practice, though, the penalty is rarely the biggest cost. An expired licence stalls bank document checks, payment-gateway merchant files, and corporate vendor registrations all at once.

## Numbers you have to get from your own office

The tables here give the national ceilings. What they cannot tell you:

- **Your corporation's current rate.** Each one sets its own beneath the ceiling, and no single source publishes all of them.
- **The renewal deadline and the late fee.** Check the expiry printed on your own licence, and get the current surcharge rule from the revenue department.
- **Rates in municipality and union areas.** The 2016 model schedule is for city corporations.[^kor-tofsil] A municipality has its own schedule.
- **Signboard and advertisement tax.** In many corporations this arrives separately from the licence fee.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Budgeting against a fee seen on a blog | The real bill does not match | Find your class's ceiling, then confirm the corporation's rate |
| Reading a company's fee off the type of business | For a limited company it is calculated on capital | Use the paid-up capital table |
| Remembering after expiry | A late fee, and stalled bank and payment work | A calendar reminder a month before expiry |
| Renewing after an address change | The old address stays on the paperwork | File the correction along with the renewal |
| Not keeping receipts | No proof of payment later | Scan and file every challan and receipt |
| Treating arrears as an ordinary bill | In law it is recoverable as a public demand | Do not let arrears build |

## Checklist

- [ ] Identified which serial of the schedule the business falls under
- [ ] For a limited company, checked the ceiling against paid-up capital
- [ ] Confirmed the corporation's current rate with the revenue department or portal
- [ ] Put the expiry date printed on the licence into the calendar
- [ ] Decided between one year and several years of fees at once
- [ ] Checked whether the address, ownership, or type of business has changed
- [ ] Filed copies of the challan and the receipt
- [ ] Checked whether signboard or advertisement tax applies separately

Rule of thumb: **The fee is not a number to memorise but a class to understand. Get the class right and you know the ceiling — and knowing the ceiling is how an overcharge becomes visible.**
