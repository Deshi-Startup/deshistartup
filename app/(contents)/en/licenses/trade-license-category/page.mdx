# Trade Licence Class: Finding Yours Among 294

> **In short:** The City Corporation Model Tax Schedule 2016 lists 294 separate classes of business, and a software or services business can plausibly sit in at least four of them — IT business at Tk 3,000, consultant at Tk 3,000, graphics design at Tk 2,000, and computer design firm at Tk 750.[^kor-tofsil] Change the class and the annual ceiling moves by a factor of four. For a limited company, though, that list is not yours: the class follows <Term name="paid-up-capital">paid-up capital</Term> instead, and serials 7 to 32 apply only where the business is not a limited company.[^kor-tofsil] The class is a money question, but not only a money question — banks, payment gateways, and corporate buyers later check the business written on the licence against the business you actually run.

The fee tables and the renewal cycle are in [Trade licence fees and renewal](/en/licenses/trade-license-renewal). This page is only about choosing the class.

---

## What the list actually looks like

The model tax schedule applies to every city corporation, and the part covering tax on businesses, professions, and industrial establishments is one long numbered list — 1 to 294.[^kor-tofsil] Each serial carries a maximum annual tax, and some serials break into sub-classes (ka), (kha).[^kor-tofsil]

Reading it in three parts makes it manageable:

| Part | What it covers | Fee columns |
|---|---|---|
| Serials 1 to 3 | Limited companies, banks and financial institutions, educational institutions | One rate |
| Serials 4 to 32 | Contractors, developers, agencies, manufacturers, distributors | Two — Dhaka North, Dhaka South, and Chattogram separately from the rest |
| Serials 33 to 294 | Shops, services, factories, professions — nearly every everyday business | One rate |

The schedule has one more rule that causes more confusion than the fee does. The size of a business establishment is determined in square feet, and each corporation sends its proposal on which class falls into which size band to the Local Government Division for approval.[^kor-tofsil] So two shops in the same class can get different bills.

---

## For a limited company, the list is not yours

Once you register a company, the calculation moves to serial 1, where the rate follows paid-up capital — from Tk 1,500 up to Tk 1 lakh of capital, to Tk 12,000 above Tk 5 crore.[^kor-tofsil] The full table is in [Fees and renewal](/en/licenses/trade-license-renewal).

The schedule makes this explicit in one place. The heading over serials 7 to 32 says they cover manufacturers and distributors, and apply only where the business is not a limited company.[^kor-tofsil] A garments factory or a cold storage operating as a limited company does not take those rates.

For the remaining serials the schedule states no such exception. So whether a company can sit in a shop or service class is a matter of interpretation by the corporation's revenue department. Ask before you apply — do not fill in the form on an assumption.

## Where software, agencies, and service businesses land

This is where most founders get stuck, and for a simple reason. The schedule gives technology and services not one class but several adjacent ones, and the names are hard to tell apart.

| Serial | Class | Maximum annual tax |
|---|---|---|
| 237 | Consultant | Tk 3,000 |
| 238 | Computer design firm | Tk 750 |
| 251 | Marketing business | Tk 3,000 |
| 246 | Marketing or distribution business | Tk 5,000 |
| 253 | IT business | Tk 3,000 |
| 262 | Computer compose | Tk 1,000 |
| 267 | Educational consultancy | Tk 3,000 |
| 271 | Computer hardware and accessories | Tk 1,500 |
| 281 | Graphics design | Tk 2,000 |
| 287 | Information supply or news agency | Tk 1,000 |
| 250 | Telecommunication | Tk 8,000 |

All figures come from the table under section 10 of the Model Tax Schedule 2016, and each is a ceiling.[^kor-tofsil]

Read the table once and it is clear why "pick the cheapest" is dangerous. Computer design firm and IT business are four times apart. But if you build and sell software while the licence says design firm, you have saved about Tk 2,250 a year — and stalled the bank's document check.

## There is no class for an online business

Nowhere among the 294 classes in the 2016 schedule is there a separate class for e-commerce, an online shop, or a marketplace.[^kor-tofsil] The list belongs to a moment when social commerce was not yet what it is now.

Two things follow. First, an online seller gets placed in whatever nearby class the office thinks fits — the shop class for whatever they sell, or IT business, or marketing business. Second, two similar online businesses in two wards can end up in two different classes, and neither is wrong.

So if you sell online, the fastest route is to ask the question at the office directly. And remember that DBID, the separate registration for digital businesses, is not an alternative to a trade licence — see [Digital Business Identification](/en/registration/dbid).

## How to choose

**1. Write the real work in one sentence.** "We build order-management software for restaurants and charge a monthly fee" — that sentence is the basis of your class. If you do several things, lead with the one that earns most.

**2. Settle the structure first.** Limited company means serial 1 and the capital table. Sole proprietorship or partnership means the rest of the list.

**3. Shortlist two or three candidate classes.** Note the names and serial numbers from the schedule. Asking "is it 253 or 238?" at the counter works far better than announcing "mine is an IT business."

**4. Confirm the corporation's current rate.** The schedule's figures are ceilings, not bills. Get the current rate from your corporation's revenue department or e-revenue portal.

**5. If you do several things, ask whether one licence covers them.** Some corporations want separate licences, or an additional fee, for multiple classes.

---

## Where a wrong class catches up with you

The practical obstacles arrive well before any penalty does.

- **Opening a bank account.** During corporate account checks the bank compares the business named on the licence with the business you describe.
- **In the payment gateway's merchant file.** Gateways set risk categories from the type of business.
- **At VAT registration.** VAT classification is its own system, but two documents describing two different businesses invite questions.
- **On a corporate client's vendor list.** In government and large-enterprise procurement, the work on the licence has to match the work in the tender.
- **When the business changes.** Moving from services to products, or the reverse, changes the class — and that does not happen by itself at renewal.

---

## What you have to get from your own office

- **Your corporation's current rate and size bands.** The ceiling is national; the rate is local.
- **Rules on multiple classes for one business.** These differ by corporation.
- **The class list for municipality and union areas.** The 2016 schedule is for city corporations.[^kor-tofsil]
- **How to correct a class, and what it costs.** Get the procedure from the revenue department.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Picking the cheapest class | The work on paper stops matching the work | The class nearest the real work |
| Copying the shop next door | Their business and structure are different | Choose from your own structure and work |
| A limited company assuming a shop class | The calculation should be capital-based | Use the serial 1 table |
| Keeping an old class after the business changed | Renewal without correction leaves the mismatch | Correct the class along with the renewal |
| Working from a category list on a blog | It may be outdated or partial | Use the schedule as published in the gazette |

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Written the real work in one sentence
- [ ] Settled whether this is a limited company first
- [ ] Noted two or three candidate serials from the schedule
- [ ] Asked the revenue department by serial number
- [ ] Confirmed the current rate and the size band
- [ ] Checked whether one licence covers multiple activities
- [ ] Compared the business named on the licence with the bank and VAT paperwork

Rule of thumb: **The question when choosing a class is not "which one is cheapest" but "which one tells an outsider what I do." The fee gap is a few thousand taka a year; the cost of a mismatch is much larger.**
