# Food Business Paperwork: What BFSA Issues, and Where Licences Come From

> **In short:** Starting a food business, many people go looking for a "BFSA licence." No such service appears anywhere in the Authority's own citizen charter.[^bfsa-charter] What BFSA issues is standards and regulations, **grading** for hotels, restaurants, bakeries and sweet factories, and the **health certificate** for exports.[^bfsa-charter] The licences come from other doors — the city corporation's trade licence, the district administration's licence, a premises licence, the Department of Environment's clearance, the fire service's safety clearance, and health certificates for food workers.[^bfsa-grading] Those are exactly the papers a grading application asks for, which makes the list a practical checklist in its own right.

If you are in food, groceries, or agro-processing, this page is for understanding the order of the paperwork. Each document has its own guide, linked below.

---

## What BFSA does, and what it does not

The services promised in the Authority's citizen charter split into citizen services and institutional services.[^bfsa-charter] The ones that matter to a business:

| Service | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Health certificate for exported food products | 10 working days | A processing fee — see below |
| Advice and information on industrial-premises inspection and the compliance checklist | 3 working days | Per the Right to Information Rules |
| Information and advice on fixing food safety faults in hotels and restaurants | 3 working days | Per the Right to Information Rules |
| Grading (hotels, restaurants, bakeries, sweet factories) | 30 working days | Free |

On the citizen side there is the 16155 call centre for adulterated-food complaints, information on laboratories and food standards, explanations of the Act and regulations, and evening training on food safety — Tk 10,000 a head, or Tk 8,000 a head for groups of 20 or more.[^bfsa-charter]

Note what is absent from that list: a food business licence. BFSA's role is setting standards, monitoring, and coordination — not permission to open a shop or a factory.

## Where the document list is actually written down

The most useful list hides inside the conditions for a grading application. To apply for grading of a hotel, restaurant, bakery, or sweet factory, you submit:[^bfsa-grading]

- The completed application form from the website
- Two attested photographs of the owner
- National ID copies of the owner and the applicant
- **A copy of the current trade licence**
- **A copy of the district administration's licence**, where applicable
- **A current premises licence** and **copies of the food workers' health certificates**
- **A copy of the Department of Environment's clearance**
- **A copy of the safety clearance issued by the Fire Service and Civil Defence**
- **A copy of the water connection**
- Any other documents applicable in special cases

That list is really a map. It sets out, in one place, which offices a food business ends up dealing with — local government, the district administration, the Department of Environment, the fire service, the water utility, and the health department.

Where each document comes from:

| Document | Office | Our guide |
|---|---|---|
| Trade licence | City corporation or municipality | [Trade licence](/en/trade-license) |
| Environmental clearance | Department of Environment | [Environmental clearance](/en/licenses/environmental-clearance) |
| Fire safety clearance | Fire Service and Civil Defence | [Fire licence](/en/licenses/fire-license) |
| Registration and licence of the establishment | Department of Inspection for Factories and Establishments | [The DIFE licence](/en/licenses/factory-license) |
| District administration licence, premises licence, workers' health certificates | Deputy Commissioner's and Civil Surgeon's offices | Check with your own district |

## The regulations that sit directly on your product

BFSA issues no licence, but its regulations sit on your product — what goes on the label, what may be added, what may be claimed. The subjects covered by the regulations published on the Authority's law page:[^bfsa-laws]

- Labelling of packaged food
- Use of food additives
- Chemical contaminants and toxins
- Microbial contaminants
- Pesticides and other chemical residues
- Residues of veterinary and fishery drugs
- Food contact materials
- Food fortification
- Health foods and dietary supplements
- Sample collection, testing, and analysis
- Food recall
- Advertisement and claims

The last one is comparatively new. The Safe Food (Advertisement and Claims) Regulations 2025 were issued on 30 November 2025 and take effect six months after the date of gazette publication.[^bfsa-ad] Read it before you write a health claim into a Facebook ad.

Before a product goes to market, at least three of these deserve a look — labelling, additives, and advertisement and claims. Catching a labelling error after the wrappers are printed means reprinting the whole batch.

## Grading: not mandatory, but useful

BFSA grades hotels, restaurants, bakeries, and sweet factories — **free of charge**, in **30 working days**.[^bfsa-grading] The application goes in directly or by post, on the form from the website.[^bfsa-grading]

Why bother? Because the grade goes on the wall and customers read it. It also helps in conversations with food delivery platforms and in corporate catering.

And the less-discussed benefit: applying for grading forces you to assemble the entire document list above in one go. Doing that while the business is small saves a scramble later.

## Exporting: the e-health certificate

The health certificate for exported food products does come from BFSA, applied for through the e-Health Certification System.[^bfsa-charter]

The processing fee follows the FOB value of the consignment:[^bfsa-charter]

| FOB value | Processing fee |
|---|---|
| Up to Tk 10 lakh | Tk 2,000 |
| Tk 10 lakh to 50 lakh | Tk 3,000 |
| Above Tk 50 lakh | Tk 5,000 |
| Re-issue or duplicate | Tk 500 |

Two further amounts go on top of the fee — **15% VAT** and **10% income tax**, each deposited under its own economic code by treasury challan or through the Sonali payment gateway.[^bfsa-charter]

What goes with it: the parameters required by the importing country or the importer, or the Codex parameters; an undertaking on the exporter's letterhead; the renewed ERC certificate and the trade licence; the packing list and commercial invoice; and a copy of the contract with the importer or the proforma invoice.[^bfsa-charter] Under BEZA, the project clearance.[^bfsa-charter]

The timeline is 10 working days, though it can shorten or lengthen with the product and the type of testing.[^bfsa-charter] Keep those ten days in hand when fixing a shipment date.

## What could not be verified for this page

- **Which law the premises licence and the district administration licence sit under, and what they cost.** The charter names both documents, but the issuing offices' own rules and fees could not be verified for this page. Check with your Deputy Commissioner's office.
- **The process for food workers' health certificates.** These come from the Civil Surgeon's office or an approved health centre, but the detail varies by area.
- **The specific requirements inside each regulation.** The list above is of subjects, not sections. Open the regulation that applies to your product and read it.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | What happens | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Hunting for a "BFSA licence" | No such service exists; time is lost | Licences at local and district offices; standards and grading at BFSA |
| Printing wrappers before reading the labelling rules | The whole batch of wrappers is wasted | Check the regulation before the design is final |
| Writing health claims into ads | There is a regulation on advertisement and claims | Read it before writing the claim |
| Not keeping the water connection paperwork | It is on the grading list too | File the copy |
| Applying for the certificate after fixing the shipment date | The certificate takes 10 working days | Apply while setting the date |
| Skipping workers' health certificates | The document list asks for them explicitly | Arrange them with the hiring |

## Checklist

- [ ] Current trade licence in hand
- [ ] District administration licence taken where applicable
- [ ] Premises licence current
- [ ] Health certificates arranged for food workers
- [ ] Department of Environment clearance obtained
- [ ] Fire service safety clearance obtained
- [ ] Water connection paperwork on file
- [ ] Labelling, additives, and advertising regulations checked
- [ ] Decided whether to apply for grading
- [ ] For exports, ten working days allowed for the e-health certificate

Rule of thumb: **The document list on BFSA's grading application is the best available checklist for a food business. Even if you never apply for grading, assemble your file against that list.**
