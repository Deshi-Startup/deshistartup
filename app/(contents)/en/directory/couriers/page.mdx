# Courier Services in Bangladesh: Compare Your Delivery Options

> **In short:** Shortlist couriers that serve your actual delivery areas. Compare delivery, <Term name="cod">cash on delivery (COD)</Term> and return charges together, then test a small batch before committing all orders. A low headline delivery rate is only one part of the cost.

Search by provider or delivery area below. Open the provider's name for its website; the source and checked date show where the listed details came from. Confirm the rate for your parcel's weight, route and service in the current merchant portal.

## Ask for a Quote You Can Compare

Give each courier the same parcel weight, pickup area, destination and amount to collect. Ask for:

- Delivery charge and any extra weight or remote-area charge.
- COD fee, its calculation basis and when collected cash reaches your account.
- Failed-delivery, second-attempt and return charges.
- Compensation limits, evidence required and deadline for a loss or damage claim.
- Pickup cutoff, tracking and the support contact for unresolved parcels.

Record the answers together. “Nationwide coverage” alone does not confirm the service, price or delivery time for a particular address.

## Run a Small Delivery Test

Choose representative routes from your own orders, including locations outside your easiest delivery area. Record the promised and actual delivery dates, customer contact, charges, returns and settlement date. Reconcile the collected cash against the order IDs before increasing volume.

Use this blank record for each provider:

```text
Provider and quote date: ___
Pickup / destination / parcel weight: ___
Delivery + COD + other charges: ___
Failed delivery or return cost: ___
Promised delivery / actual delivery: ___
COD collected / money received / settlement date: ___
Problem and resolution: ___
```

To check whether returns erase your margin, use the [COD risk guide and calculator](/en/operations/cod-risk). For matching deposits to orders, follow [payment reconciliation](/en/payments/reconciliation).
