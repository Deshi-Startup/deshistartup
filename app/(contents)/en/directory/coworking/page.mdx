# Coworking Spaces in Dhaka: Find a Desk That Fits Your Work

> **In short:** Choose a location you can reach regularly and a plan that covers the hours you need. Compare the total cost of passes, desks and meeting rooms. Ask for a visit or trial before paying for a longer commitment.

Search the directory by provider or area. Open a space's name for its website, and check the source date beside its details. Published prices and desk availability need confirmation with the provider.

## Compare the Cost for Your Actual Month

An hourly allowance, a day pass and a full-month desk buy different amounts of access. Write down how many days and hours you need, then ask what happens when you exceed the allowance.

```text
Working days needed: ___; hours per day: ___
Desk or pass cost: BDT ___
Extra hours, meeting rooms and printing: BDT ___
VAT or other charges: BDT ___
Travel cost for those days: BDT ___
Total monthly cost: BDT ___
Deposit payable separately: BDT ___
Cancellation / notice period: ___
```

A cheaper desk farther away may cost more after transport and take time away from work. Include meeting-room use if you regularly meet customers; do not assume it is part of the desk price.

## Check the Space Before Booking

Visit during the hours you would normally work. Test a video call, check noise and seating, and ask how internet and power outages are handled. Confirm opening days, after-hours access, guest rules and secure storage if those affect your work.

For a team, check the number of seats available now and how adding or removing a seat changes the agreement. If you need the address for registration or official correspondence, confirm the provider's terms and the receiving authority's requirements before relying on it.

Keep the written price, included services, deposit terms and cancellation policy with the booking confirmation.
