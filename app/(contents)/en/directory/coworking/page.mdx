# Coworking Space Directory

> **TL;DR:** Before you take a desk, check three things: how long the commute actually takes, whether the internet and seating suit your work, and whether the monthly cost fits your current revenue. Most spaces let you sit for a day first — do that before you pay. Prices change, so confirm the published rate by phone.
