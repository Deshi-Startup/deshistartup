# Government Portals for Startup Registration and Tax in Bangladesh

> **In short:** Choose the task first, then the office or portal that handles it. Keep the application number, payment receipt and issued document together. A completed payment is not by itself proof that a licence or certificate was approved.

This is an independent directory of official services. Applications are made on the relevant authority's portal. Search by office or service below, open its name for the official website, and check the source date before acting.

## Prepare for the Task You Need

- **Company name and incorporation:** follow [RJSC name clearance](/en/registration/name-clearance), then [private limited company registration](/en/registration/private-limited).
- **Income-tax identity:** use the [e-TIN guide](/en/tax/e-tin). If someone asked for a filing acknowledgment, check the difference between [TIN, BIN and return proof](/en/e-tin-vat-bin).
- **VAT registration or turnover-tax enlistment:** use the [VAT/BIN guide](/en/tax/vat-bin) and confirm which status applies.
- **Trade licence:** check the [trade-licence guide](/en/trade-license) for the authority serving the business address.
- **Investment and coordinated services:** use the [BIDA and BanglaBiz guide](/en/registration/bida-banglabiz) to understand the applicable route.

## Keep a Record You Can Follow Up

Before submitting, compare names, addresses and identification numbers across the form and attachments. Save the version you submitted, application or tracking number, official receipt and any query from the authority.

When a portal asks for a correction, respond to the specific request and retain the updated version. Check for the final certificate, licence or approval status before marking the task complete. Use the contact details published by the authority if the status or payment is unclear.
