# Payment Gateways Directory

> **TL;DR:** The real cost of a gateway is not just the fee, it is when the money reaches you. Compare the commission, the settlement time and whether your customers' preferred methods are supported, then decide. Fees change, so re-check the pricing page before you sign.

Shortlist providers here, then use the [gateway fee comparison](/en/payments/gateway-comparison) to calculate a first-year cost from your card and mobile-wallet sales. It shows how to compare an aamarPay quote with SSLCOMMERZ's published fees.

## Check the Quote and Application Requirements

Before applying, match the provider's document list to your business structure. For the entries checked on 13 September 2026, use [SSLCOMMERZ's onboarding requirements](https://sslcommerz.com/onboarding-requirements/) and [aamarPay's FAQ](https://aamarpay.com/index.php/faq). aamarPay's [registration form](https://registration.aamarpay.com/) lists setup prices, while its [pricing page](https://aamarpay.com/index.php/pricing) asks for a quote. Confirm transaction rates, VAT on fees and settlement terms in writing.
