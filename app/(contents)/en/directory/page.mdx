# Directory: Shortlist Services and Support

> **In short:** Use the directory to find candidates for a specific need, then check their current fit and terms directly. Inclusion is not an endorsement.

## Choose a category

- **Run the business:** [payment gateways](/en/directory/payment-gateways), [couriers](/en/directory/couriers), [legal and accounting](/en/directory/legal-accounting).
- **Find support:** [accelerators](/en/directory/accelerators), [coworking](/en/directory/coworking).
- **Investigate funding:** [investors](/en/directory/investors), [government funding](/en/directory/government-funding).
- **Find an official process:** [government services](/en/directory/government-services).

## Turn a listing into a decision

Write your location, stage, service need, budget and timing before contacting providers. Compare candidates against the same requirements. Ask about eligibility, total cost, exclusions, contract terms and how problems are handled.

Use the source links and check dates on entries as context. Confirm current details with the organisation; a previous check cannot guarantee current availability or quality. Keep the response and unresolved questions beside your shortlist.

If you do not know which type of organisation to approach, start with the [ecosystem guide](/en/ecosystem). Report a broken or outdated listing through [contact](/en/contact), including the page and supporting source.

## Example: compare answers, not just names

A fictional software team needs an accountant who can handle its actual transactions and recurring filings. It asks two candidates the same questions. Candidate A quotes a monthly fee but leaves annual work unspecified. Candidate B lists monthly and annual work separately, names the responsible person and states what is excluded.

The team cannot yet call A cheaper: the scope differs. Its next action is to obtain comparable written scopes, total costs, deadlines, access requirements and responsibility for corrections. This is a comparison method, not an endorsement of either fictional candidate. Your shortlist is ready for a decision when you can explain the fit and remaining uncertainties using the same criteria for each option.
