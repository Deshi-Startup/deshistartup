## How to use the directory

Start with the job you need done, then open the matching category. Shortlist organisations that fit your location, business stage and need. Give each candidate the same brief: what the work involves, where and when you need it, and any budget or volume that affects the offer.

For a service provider, compare written scope, total cost, exclusions, deadlines and who handles problems. For a programme or funder, first confirm stage, eligibility, terms and whether applications are open. A listing is a starting point for your own checks, not an endorsement or a “best provider” ranking. If you are unsure whether you need a provider, adviser or funding organisation, the [ecosystem guide](/en/ecosystem) explains those roles.

## Check before you decide

Each entry includes a source link and last-checked date. Use them to see where the information came from, then confirm details directly because prices, availability and terms can change. Keep replies together so you can compare the same need across candidates. If a listing is outdated or a link is broken, send the page and a supporting source through [contact](/en/contact).
