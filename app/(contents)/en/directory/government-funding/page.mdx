# Startup Grants and Government Funding in Bangladesh

> **In short:** First distinguish a grant, an investment and a loan. They have different obligations and application routes. Use the directory to find a program that fits your stage, then confirm eligibility, terms and the current application window with the official source.

Filter by funding type or stage below. Each entry includes its application route, source and checked date. A listing is not confirmation that applications are open or that funding will be awarded.

## Choose the Funding Type Before Preparing an Application

- **Grant:** read the permitted uses, milestones, reporting and repayment or recovery conditions. “Non-equity” does not mean there are no conditions.
- **Equity or quasi-equity investment:** understand the ownership or financing terms and the investor's decision process. This is different from applying for a grant.
- **Loan or bank financing:** check repayment, interest or profit charges, security and the lender's appraisal. Follow the application route in the entry; a central-bank fund may operate through participating lenders.

The program's own call and agreement decide what applies. Do not compare headline maximum amounts without checking whether your stage and proposed use of funds qualify.

## Prepare One Clear Evidence Folder

Start with the application form's actual requirements. Keep reusable material together:

- The problem, intended customer and product or demo.
- Founder and team information, and company documents where required.
- Evidence of demand, separating paying customers from pilots or expressions of interest.
- The amount requested, a spending breakdown and the milestone that money should achieve.
- Financial records or forecasts, with assumptions clearly marked.

Use a [pitch deck](/en/funding/pitch-deck) to explain the case and a [data room](/en/funding/data-room) for supporting documents. Match the package to the program instead of attaching everything you have.

## Before You Submit

Confirm the official application address, deadline and time zone, required attachments and contact for questions. Save the submitted version and acknowledgment. Keep unapproved funding out of the cash you count on for payroll or other commitments.

For the bank-financing route, read the [Bangladesh Bank Startup Fund guide](/en/funding/bb-startup-fund).
