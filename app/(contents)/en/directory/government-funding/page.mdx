# Government Funding and Programs Directory

> **TL;DR:** News about government funds often circulates on Facebook before the official circular arrives. Before applying, check the official page for who is eligible, how much is offered and the deadline. If something is not written there, do not assume it. Disbursement can take months, so plan your runway accordingly.
