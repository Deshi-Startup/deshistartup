# Investor CRM: Track Deals from Intro to Cash in the Bank

> **In short:** In Bangladesh, a warm coffee chat in Gulshan is not a commitment, and a verbal commitment is not cash in the bank. Track angels, NRBs and institutional funds in one shared sheet. Record the exact currency, instrument, next agreed step and bank wire status. Cash only counts when it clears your company account.

## Copy the tracker

Fundraising is a structured pipeline. In Bangladesh, founders typically engage three distinct investor groups: local angel syndicates and business families, Non-Resident Bangladeshi (NRB) tech professionals, and institutional venture funds.

A <Term name="funding-rounds">funding round</Term> fails when founders lose track of follow-ups or confuse polite interest with financial commitment. Use a simple two-tab spreadsheet: one for investor profiles and one for active conversation logs.

```text
TAB 1: INVESTOR PROFILES — one row per fund, syndicate or angel
Name | Category: Local Angel / NRB Angel / Institutional VC / Family Office
Official Website / LinkedIn | Primary Contact Name & Role | Warm Introducer
Investment Mandate: Sectors | Stages (Pre-seed / Seed / Series A) | Geography
Typical Cheque Size & Currency (BDT / USD) | Lead or Follower
Known Past Investments in Bangladesh / Emerging Markets | Source URL
Fit Assessment: High Fit / Speculative / Hard Pass
Why We Fit (1 concrete sentence) | Confidentiality / Competing Portfolio Check

TAB 2: PIPELINE CONVERSATIONS — one row per interaction or committed cheque
Investor Name | Primary Founder Owner | Communication Channel (Email / WhatsApp)
Instrument: SAFE (Post-money) / Convertible Note / RJSC Ordinary Shares
Amount Discussed | Currency (BDT or USD) | Implied Valuation / Valuation Cap
Pipeline Stage:
  [1] Researched -> [2] Warm Intro Sent -> [3] First Meeting Done
  -> [4] Diligence / Data Room Sent -> [5] Term Sheet / SAFE Issued
  -> [6] Agreement Signed -> [7] Wire in Transit -> [8] Cash Cleared in Bank
Current Blocker or Diligence Question | Teammate Responsible
Latest Pitch Deck Version Sent | Data Room Access Scope (Summary vs Full)
Next Action & Agreed Deadline: [e.g., "Send cohort retention sheet by Thursday 3 PM"]
Investor Objections / Pass Reason | Permission for Quarterly Updates: Yes / No
Bank Inward Remittance / Clearing Reference (for USD funds via Bangladesh Bank)
```

Keep notes restricted to founders and legal advisers. Never store investor passwords, confidential banking coordinates, or unredacted cap tables in an unprotected public sheet.

## Begin with a round brief and dual-currency discipline

Before filling rows, define your round parameters clearly:
1. **Target Milestone:** What exact operational result does this capital fund (e.g., reaching BDT 20 lakh monthly revenue or finishing clinical trials)?
2. **Target Amount & Currency:** If you are raising from local angels in Dhaka, terms are often negotiated in BDT. If raising from overseas venture capital or NRB angels in North America or Singapore, figures are usually denominated in USD.
3. **Legal Instrument:** Are you issuing a standard post-money SAFE, a convertible note, or directly issuing equity shares registered at RJSC? Each has completely different legal and regulatory requirements under Bangladesh Bank foreign exchange rules.

Keep one dated set of audited numbers. Distinguish **Revenue** (actual net billings earned by your company) from <Term name="gmv">GMV</Term> (Gross Merchandise Value: total value of goods transacted across your platform). Conflating GMV with revenue will immediately break your credibility during institutional due diligence.

## Verify mandate fit before initial outreach

Do not send generic cold emails to famous global venture capitalists who have never funded a company in South Asia.

Review verified local deal data. ExitStack's funding dashboard documents historical investments raised by Bangladeshi startups.[^exitstack-dashboard] Use past deals to identify active investors, but always verify their current investment mandate on their official website.

Sort your prospective list into three clear tiers:

| Tier | Mandate Evidence | Next Action |
|---|---|---|
| High Fit | Active fund or angel investing in Bangladesh at your current stage and sector | Secure warm introduction via mutual portfolio founder |
| Speculative | Funds peer emerging markets (e.g., Pakistan, Indonesia, Nigeria) but no BD deals yet | Reach out with a short thesis on why Bangladesh fits their model |
| Hard Pass | Explicitly excluded by geography, B2B/B2C focus, or requires Series A traction | Remove immediately; do not waste fundraising cycles |

## Follow institutional application standards

Different institutional programmes enforce strict reporting rules. Accelerating Asia requires application figures in USD and explicitly penalizes confusing GMV with revenue.[^aa-application] Conversely, Startup Bangladesh Limited's official portal requires revenue, GMV, and past funding reported in BDT.[^sbl-application]

When converting between BDT and USD:
- Always document the exchange rate, rate date and source.
- Provide both numbers side-by-side in your pipeline sheet.
- Check the official route: Startup Bangladesh's general contact page explicitly notes that general inquiries do not count as formal investment applications.[^sbl-contact]

## Use evidence-backed pipeline stages

Never mark an investor as "committed" because they bought you coffee or praised your pitch deck.

| Pipeline Stage | Required Evidence |
|---|---|
| 1. Researched | Verified investment mandate matching your sector and stage |
| 2. Contacted | Sent personalized message via warm introducer or official portal |
| 3. First Meeting | Held call or in-person meeting; founder debrief recorded |
| 4. Diligence | Specific questions received; <Term name="data-room">Data Room</Term> access granted |
| 5. Term Sheet / SAFE | Written draft agreement received with explicit valuation and cheque amount |
| 6. Signed Agreement | Both parties executed the SAFE or share subscription agreement |
| 7. Wire in Transit | Investor shared SWIFT MT103 or bank transfer voucher |
| 8. Cash Cleared | Funds credited to company bank account (bank credit advice or statement confirmation); transaction-specific documentation (such as Encashment Certificate / PRC if converted to BDT, or inward credit documentation if held in an eligible FC account) tracked alongside |

An investor can pass at any point before stage 8. Until cash clears your company bank account, that money cannot be spent on salaries, inventory, or infrastructure.

## Write a concise outreach email

When approaching an investor, keep the first touchpoint under 150 words:

```text
Subject: [Company Name] — [One-line description], raising [Amount in BDT/USD]

Dear [Investor Name],

[Mutual contact, if applicable] suggested I reach out given your focus on [Sector/Market].

We help [Target customer] do [Job to be done]. Over the last [Timeframe], we achieved:
- [Traction metric 1: e.g., Monthly Revenue grown from BDT X to BDT Y]
- [Traction metric 2: e.g., Unit economics: Contribution Margin positive at Z%]

We are raising [Amount] on a [SAFE / Equity round] to [Specific operational milestone].

Here is our 10-slide deck: [DocSend or tracked link].
Are you open to a brief 15-minute conversation next week to see if this fits your current mandate?
```

Send a short <Term name="pitch-deck">pitch deck</Term> that explains the unit economics and the team. Do not attach heavy uncompressed PDF files or share sensitive customer lists on the first email.

## Worked example: managing an 18-investor Dhaka round

Imagine a Dhaka B2B software startup raising an angel round of BDT 60 lakh (~$50,000 USD). The scenario below illustrates effective pipeline tracking.

The founder identifies 18 potential leads:
- **4 Institutional funds:** 2 pass immediately (minimum cheque size $250k); 2 enter the formal application review process.
- **8 Local Dhaka angels:** 3 pass after first meeting; 3 express verbal enthusiasm over coffee; 2 ask for audited revenue statements.
- **6 NRB angels:** 2 do not respond; 4 take introductory Zoom calls from London and New York.

### The verbal commitment trap
Of the three local angels who said "I'm definitely in for BDT 10 lakh" at a restaurant in Banani:
- Angel A signs the SAFE within 5 days and initiates an RTGS bank transfer. Once the bank statement confirms the funds have credited, the stage moves to **Cash Cleared**.
- Angel B stops replying to WhatsApp messages once the formal agreement is sent. Stage remains **Term Sheet Issued**, and the founder does not budget this money.
- Angel C asks to renegotiate the valuation cap down by 40%. The founder logs the objection and pauses discussions.

Of the 4 NRB angels:
- Two agree to invest $10,000 each via SAFE notes. The founder provides foreign inward remittance instructions, tracks the international SWIFT transfers, and confirms when the Authorized Dealer (AD) bank credits the company account. Once bank credit is confirmed, both move to **Cash Cleared** (with transaction documentation, such as the inward credit advice and encashment certificate if converted to BDT, filed for regulatory reporting).

Total confirmed cash cleared in the bank: BDT 10 lakh + $20,000 USD (~BDT 24 lakh) = **BDT 34 lakh**.

By tracking exact pipeline evidence instead of multiplying verbal promises by an arbitrary "70% probability," the founders knew their exact runway and avoided making hiring commitments they could not fund.

## Run a weekly 30-minute pipeline review

Every Monday morning, spend 30 minutes updating the fundraising tracker:
1. **Identify stalled deals:** If an investor has held the data room for over 10 days without a response, send a polite check-in or revoke access.
2. **Review objections:** If three different angels raise doubts about your customer churn or courier return rates, stop pitching and fix the underlying metrics before booking more meetings.
3. **Audit cash reality:** Check the company bank balance against committed wires.

- [ ] Every investor row has a source-backed fit justification.
- [ ] Currencies (BDT vs USD) and exchange rates are explicitly labeled.
- [ ] No verbal promise is counted as cash until bank clearance is confirmed.
- [ ] Data room links are tracked and access permissions are controlled.
- [ ] Every active conversation has a named founder owner and a scheduled next step.

When due diligence begins, use the [data room checklist](/en/funding/data-room). For investors who pass but express interest in your progress, add them to your [investor update template](/en/funding/investor-updates).
