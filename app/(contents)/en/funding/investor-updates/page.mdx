# Monthly Investor Update Template

> **In short:** Disappearing after raising capital is one of the biggest mistakes early-stage founders make. Sending brief, honest, monthly updates maintains trust, leverages investor networks, and lays the groundwork for follow-on funding rounds.

In the Bangladeshi ecosystem, founders often hesitate to share bad news due to cultural norms. However, hiding operational challenges is a missed opportunity. Communicating your struggles early allows investors to jump in and actually help you solve them.

---

## Why Consistent Investor Updates Matter

1. **Building Trust & Transparency:** Sharing both good news and bad news demonstrates maturity. It reassures investors that you are in control and not hiding reality.
2. **Unlocking Investor Help:** Investors provide more than just capital. Their networks, industry connections, and hiring leads are massive assets you should tap into.
3. **Paving the Way for Follow-on Funding:** Existing investors who observe your execution month over month are far more likely to participate in subsequent funding rounds.[^shoumik-investor-updates]

---

## The 3-Section Update Format

A great investor update should take less than 3 minutes to read. Structure your email into these three core sections:

### 1. Highlights (3 Items)
Briefly state your 3 biggest milestones. For example: "MRR grew from BDT 5 Lakh to BDT 7 Lakh this month."

### 2. Lowlights & Challenges (2 Items)
Clearly detail at least 2 experiments that failed or operational bottlenecks. For example: "Customer churn increased to 8%."

### 3. Specific Asks (2 Items)
Be specific rather than vague. Instead of "Please introduce us to customers," ask for at least 2 specific things you exactly need: "We need an intro to a reliable logistics partner in Chittagong."

---

## Concrete Email Template Example

Imagine you are running an e-commerce startup in Dhaka. Your monthly update should look something like this:

**Subject:** DeshiMart Investor Update – August 2026 (MRR BDT 7L, 13 Months Runway)

**Highlights:**
* MRR grew from BDT 5,00,000 to BDT 7,00,000.
* We successfully launched 2 new delivery hubs in Chittagong.
* The beta version of our new mobile app was released.

**Lowlights:**
* Customer churn rate increased to 8% this month, up from 5% last month.
* We are experiencing slight delivery delays due to supply chain bottlenecks.

**Asks:**
* We need an intro to a reliable logistics partner. Do you know the CEO of any top-tier logistics company in your network?
* We are looking for a Lead Engineer. If you know any strong candidates, please send them our way.

**Key Metrics:**
* MRR: BDT 7,00,000
* Burn Rate: BDT 3,00,000
* Runway: 13 months

Thanks for your continued support!

## Context for Bangladeshi Founders

In Bangladesh, some angel investors might go completely silent after wiring the money. It's a mistake to stop sending them updates just because they don't reply. Keep sending them. Furthermore, since our local startup ecosystem is relatively small, highly targeted networking asks (like naming a specific company you want an intro to) work significantly better than broad requests.
