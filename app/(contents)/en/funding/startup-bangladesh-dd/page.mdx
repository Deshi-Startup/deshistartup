# Applying to Startup Bangladesh: Terms, Process and Checklist

> **In short:** Startup Bangladesh Limited is the ICT Division's venture capital company. Its current FAQ gives an investment range of **BDT 25 lakh to BDT 5 crore per round**, with a maximum **49% stake** in a company. Reaching a term sheet, the proposed investment terms, can take **one to four months or longer**. That is not a promise of when money reaches your bank.[^sbl-faq] Start with the checklist below, then open the [official application page](https://startupbangladesh.gov.bd/apply-for-investments) and choose **Startup**.[^sbl-application]

This guide helps a founder check whether to apply and prepare the information the fund asks for. An application, a meeting or an expression of interest does not mean the investment has been approved.

## Who should apply, and who should not

The FAQ describes its target as businesses registered in Bangladesh with strong growth potential and/or significant social impact. It includes seed, early-stage and growth-stage startups and impact enterprises.[^sbl-faq]

- **You have a working product and evidence that people use it.** Prepare your customer numbers, revenue, costs and what you want the investment to help you achieve. Explain how the business can grow.
- **You have only an idea.** Start by testing the problem and building a small first version, or <Term name="mvp">MVP</Term>, that real users can try. The fund's About Us page mentions pre-seed support, while the current founder form offers Seed and Growth choices. Ask the investment team which route fits before assuming your idea qualifies.[^sbl-who-we-are][^sbl-application]
- **You manage a venture capital fund.** Use the separate [Fund of Funds route](https://startupbangladesh.gov.bd/funds-of-funds). It has its own eligibility rules and request for expressions of interest.[^sbl-what-we-do]
- **You need money within eight weeks.** Do not build your cash plan around this application. The published time to a term sheet can be longer than that, and the payout date is uncertain.[^sbl-faq]

The FAQ lists technology and innovation sectors such as financial services, healthcare, software, education, agriculture and logistics. The list is not exhaustive. Check both the sector fit and the evidence behind your growth or impact claim.[^sbl-faq]

## What this fund is, and why it matters

Startup Bangladesh Limited says it began in March 2020 under the ICT Division. Its FAQ describes investment through equity or quasi-equity, along with business support.[^sbl-faq] <Term name="equity">Equity</Term> means ownership shares. Quasi-equity is funding with some features of ownership investment; ask the team to explain the exact form and obligations in any offer. Do not assume the founder application is a grant application.

The fund also asks about social impact and fit with national goals. Its FAQ still names the SDGs, National ICT Policy, the 8th Five-Year Plan and Vision 2041. Treat these as the fund's published selection wording, and confirm any updated requirements with the team.[^sbl-faq] SDGs are the United Nations' Sustainable Development Goals. Explain the actual problem your business helps solve rather than adding logos with no evidence.

## The published terms

These are the terms on the official FAQ, checked **3 October 2026**. They describe the published range, not a funding promise to every applicant.[^sbl-faq]

| Term | What the fund states |
|---|---|
| Investment amount | BDT 25 lakh to BDT 5 crore per round |
| Ownership taken | No minimum target; maximum 49% stake in each company |
| Expected exit | 5 to 8 years after investment, subject to market conditions |
| Time to a <Term name="term-sheet">term sheet</Term> | 1 to 4 months or longer |
| Deal terms | Depend on the business model, <Term name="valuation">company valuation</Term>, industry, market and proposal |

A **term sheet** sets out the proposed deal. **Valuation** is the value used for the company in that deal. An **exit** is how the investor eventually sells or otherwise cashes out its investment; the stated period is an expectation, not a guaranteed sale date.

The **49% ceiling** only describes the size of the stake. Before signing, ask a lawyer to check the full agreement, including who can approve decisions, board appointments and any conditions on releasing money. Use the [term-sheet guide](/en/funding/term-sheets) to prepare your questions.

## The five steps from application to investment

The FAQ describes this sequence.[^sbl-faq]

1. **Initial screening.** The team looks at the problem, solution, evidence of customer demand, market, business model and team.
2. **Due diligence.** The team checks the business, financial and legal records. This is what due diligence means: checking whether the claims and records hold up.
3. **Deal structuring.** The parties work out the proposed amount, investment form and terms.
4. **Committee and board decisions.** The investment committee must give the go-ahead and the board must approve.
5. **Signed documents and payment.** The Share Subscription Agreement, the contract to buy shares, and other documents are signed before the agreed payments are made.

For promising applications, the FAQ also describes an Indication of Interest and a meeting with the people involved. That signals interest in exploring the proposal, not final approval.[^sbl-faq]

**Plan around an uncertain payout date.** The one-to-four-month estimate is for reaching investment terms. The FAQ does not give a separate deadline for each check, approval or payment. Recheck your runway, meaning how long your existing cash can cover costs, without assuming this money will arrive on a chosen date.

## What the application actually asks

Open [Apply for Investments](https://startupbangladesh.gov.bd/apply-for-investments) and choose **Startup**, the founder option. The form reviewed on **3 October 2026** has nine sections.[^sbl-application]

| Section | What to prepare |
|---|---|
| Company | Name, address and a product or service description labelled with a 400-character limit |
| Founders and contact | Founder and cofounder contact details, CV link, other business involvement, loans and loan-guarantor information |
| Team | Full-time, part-time and technology-team numbers |
| Business sector and stage | Sector, stage, revenue and GMV in BDT, and individual and business customer counts |
| Company status | Formation type, incorporation date, existing investor count and investment already raised |
| Pitch deck | A link to your short presentation about the business |
| Website | Your website link |
| Social media | Links, if any |
| Startup video | A link, if any |

**GMV**, or gross merchandise value, is the total value of goods sold through a platform. It is not automatically the platform's own revenue. **B2C** means individual customers; **B2B** means business customers. Use the same dates and definitions in your form, accounts and pitch deck. If a field does not state the reporting period, label the period in your supporting material and ask the team if unsure.

For the 400-character description, write the customer, problem and what your product does in two short sentences. Then count the characters and shorten it if needed. Do not assume two sentences will always fit.

**Prepare the founder disclosures honestly.** A guarantor is someone who agrees to cover another borrower's debt if the borrower does not pay. The form asks about this, but the public pages do not explain how each answer affects the decision.[^sbl-application]

> **Example:** Say Tasnia runs a healthtech startup in Dhaka with four staff, 900 paying users and enough cash for eleven months. She has also guaranteed another business's loan. She records whose loan it is, the amount and its current status, then asks the team what supporting documents they need. She does not guess that the guarantee will either rule her out or be ignored. These are fictional details to show how to prepare, not an application outcome.

## The documents

The FAQ asks for this information package.[^sbl-faq]

- [ ] **Investment pitch deck:** business model, financial forecasts, evidence of customer demand, impact, fit with government goals, and founder and team information
- [ ] **Certificate of Incorporation:** the company registration certificate
- [ ] **Memorandum and Articles of Association:** the company's founding documents and rules
- [ ] **Trade licence**
- [ ] **e-TIN and VAT registration certificates**
- [ ] **Tax-holiday permission**, if any, plus other licences or permissions relevant to the company

Separately, prepare the links and founder answers listed in the form above. If you lack a document or are unsure whether it applies to your company, ask the team before submitting. This is the fund's document list, not a statement that every startup has the same tax obligations.

Keep the supporting records in a [data room](/en/funding/data-room), a set of folders you can share with an investor when needed. Check who can open each link. Share sensitive records only through the channel agreed with the investment team.

**Check the email address before sending documents.** The FAQ still lists `investment@startupbangladeshvc.gov.bd`, but the Contact Us page and footer list `investment@startupbangladesh.gov.bd`.[^sbl-faq][^sbl-contact] Use the [current contact page](https://startupbangladesh.gov.bd/contact-us) to confirm the right address. The public pages do not establish whether both inboxes work.

## Explain impact with evidence

For the impact part of the pitch, cover three things:

1. **What changes for the customer.** Name a specific result your product is meant to improve.
2. **How you measure it.** Give the period, sample and result if you have data. If you do not, state what you still need to measure.
3. **What it costs to keep delivering that result.** Explain who pays and how you plan to cover the cost as the business grows.

For Tasnia's fictional healthtech company, 900 paying users alone would not prove better health outcomes. She could measure how long users wait for a qualified consultation. Until she has that evidence, she should describe it as a measurement plan, not a proven benefit.

## Checklist

- [ ] Checked the current FAQ and whether the founder or fund-manager route applies
- [ ] Matched the amount requested to the published range and explained what it will fund
- [ ] Rechecked cash needs without assuming a payment date
- [ ] Written and counted the product description against the 400-character label
- [ ] Prepared working CV, pitch-deck and website links
- [ ] Written down other business involvement, loans and guarantees accurately
- [ ] Kept dates and definitions consistent across customer, revenue and GMV figures
- [ ] Gathered the listed company records or asked about missing documents
- [ ] Separated measured impact from goals and assumptions

**You are ready to apply when:** the answers match your records, the required links open for the intended reviewer, and you have raised any eligibility or document questions. Submit through the current form and keep a copy of what you sent, the date, and any receipt or reference it provides. A submitted form is not a funding approval.

The application page reviewed did not state an application fee or closing date. Check the current page and ask the team if needed; do not infer that applications are always open or that every related cost is zero.[^sbl-application]
