# Team and Hiring: Define the Work Before the Role

> **In short:** Hire for a clear outcome you cannot sustainably cover with the current team. Leave with a role brief, a fair assessment method and the responsibilities you need to prepare for.

## Start with the work

List the repeated tasks that are falling behind, the results required and the time they take. Decide what can be simplified or stopped before adding a role.

Use [first hires](/en/team/first-hires) to compare priorities. Then use the [hiring scorecard](/en/team/hiring-scorecard) to define the outcomes, evidence and consistent questions you will use to assess candidates.

## Draft a role brief

Include **purpose / expected outcomes / responsibilities / working arrangements / pay budget / manager / review date**. Name the access and information the person needs, and who will train them.

A useful assessment checks the work the role actually involves. Explain the process, time commitment and how work samples will be used. Keep candidate records limited to the hiring purpose.

## Prepare for the relationship

Before making an offer, check the terms and obligations that apply to the actual working arrangement. Calling someone an intern or freelancer does not settle that question. Include supervision, payment, leave where applicable, confidentiality and handling of access when the relationship ends in your preparation.

Use the [first-employee journey](/en/journeys/hire-first-employee) for the sequence. If you are sharing ownership and founding responsibility, use [co-founders](/en/cofounders) instead.
