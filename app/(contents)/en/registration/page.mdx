# Business Registration: When, How and Which Structure

> **TL;DR:** Business registration is not synonymous with incorporating at RJSC. Your stage, liability, customers, co-founders, funding plan and sector determine whether a sole proprietorship, partnership, private limited company or another structure fits. Start with the reason you need formal documents now.

Some founders incorporate before testing the idea and take on filing work too early. Others begin regular sales without clean records. The right timing depends on risk, transactions, customers, team and sector rules.

## Use this page for the structure decision

For the broader launch sequence, use the [legal roadmap](/en/legal-roadmap). Here, your output is a structure decision with reasons, outstanding questions and an estimate of recurring work. If you already incorporated, go directly to [after registration](/en/registration/after-registration).

Check requirements before the relevant activity begins; a research or pilot label is not a blanket exemption from permissions, safety or data obligations.

## Ask What Is Blocked

| What you need | What to consider |
|---|---|
| Business bank account | Trade license, <Term name="e-tin">TIN</Term> and entity documents |
| Payment provider | Business documents, bank, tax and website requirements |
| Corporate customer | Invoice, entity or <Term name="trade-license">trade license</Term>, contract |
| Co-founder | Ownership, roles, equity and written terms |
| Equity funding | Private limited company, clean <Term name="cap-table">cap table</Term> and accounts |
| Regulated activity | Sector licenses and approvals |

Customer interviews and free tests usually do not require an incorporated company. Before charging, hiring, renting premises, entering a regulated field or signing corporate contracts, check which documents and permissions are required.

## Main Structures

| Structure | Plain description | Common fit | Main caution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | A business operated by one person | Small shop, solo service or early commerce | Business liability can reach the owner personally |
| Partnership | Several people carry on a business together | Service firm, trading or a small operation | Partners generally have unlimited personal liability |
| Private limited company | A separate legal entity limited by shares | Scalable startup, corporate work or equity funding | More accounts, audit and filings |
| One person company | A company form with one member | A solo founder seeking a company structure | Confirm current eligibility and procedure with a professional |

For the decision in detail, read [Company Types](/en/company-types).

## Sole Proprietorship

A sole proprietorship generally begins with the relevant trade license, the owner's tax records, business banking or payments, and <Term name="vat-bin">VAT registration</Term> or enlistment where applicable. There is no separate incorporation that shields the owner from business liabilities.

Even at a small scale, keep business income, refunds, costs and personal spending separate.

## Partnership

A partnership deed should state capital contributions, profit and loss sharing, decision rights, bank authority, responsibilities, exits and dispute resolution. Under the Partnership Act, partners may be jointly and separately liable without limit for acts of the firm. A private agreement does not necessarily remove that exposure to outsiders.[^partnership-liability]

## Private Limited Company

RJSC handles incorporation. The process starts with <Term name="rjsc-name-clearance">name clearance</Term>, followed by the <Term name="moa-aoa">MoA</Term>, AoA, prescribed forms, director and shareholder details, registered office and government fees.

This structure commonly fits when you need clear co-founder equity, corporate contracts, company-owned IP, a board or equity investment. It also brings recurring accounts, audit and filing duties.

Use the current [Private Limited Company Registration](/en/registration/private-limited) guide for the documents, fee example and application steps.

## The Usual Company Sequence

1. Check the proposed name with RJSC and consider domain and brand risk.
2. Prepare the MoA, AoA, prescribed forms, director and shareholder details and registered office.
3. Submit through the RJSC portal and pay the government fees.
4. Respond to any review and obtain the <Term name="incorp-certificate">Certificate of Incorporation</Term> if approved.
5. Complete the post-incorporation work: banking, trade license, tax, VAT analysis, share records, accounts and compliance calendar.

Do not copy an MoA, AoA or share structure without understanding it. These documents affect the company's permitted activities, control and later investment.

## Budget Beyond Incorporation

Separate one-time costs from annual costs. Government registration fees are only one part. Budget for accounts, audit, tax and VAT work where applicable, license renewal, RJSC filings and professional advice.

## Work through the structure decision

**Fictional example:** Rafi currently delivers a small software service alone. A friend wants to join as a co-owner, and a prospective customer asks whether it can contract with a company. Rafi's decision is now about ownership, customer requirements and continuing duties, not simply the cheapest certificate.

He writes three questions before paying anyone to file documents:

1. Will the friend be a co-owner, an employee or a contractor? Record the intended rights and responsibilities.
2. Does the customer actually require a company, or merely a valid supplier identity and bank details? Obtain the written requirement.
3. Can the chosen structure support the intended ownership and financing, and who will maintain its records and recurring filings? Compare the setup quote with the ongoing workload.

If they choose a company after that review, their output is a documented ownership decision, an accurate activity/address description, a filing checklist and an owner for continuing compliance. Incorporation does not resolve their founder agreement, tax obligations, trade license or customer contract automatically. If they remain undecided, resolve the ownership and procurement questions first instead of filing on a guess.

### Compare the recurring budget too

For illustration only, suppose Rafi receives a quote of BDT 2,000 a month for bookkeeping plus BDT 18,000 for specified year-end work. That totals **12 × 2,000 + 18,000 = BDT 42,000 a year**, before any excluded filing fees, renewals, tax payments or additional advice. This is a fictional quote, not a Bangladesh fee estimate.

He asks exactly which accounts, audit, tax and filing work is included, who submits each item and who checks completion. A cheaper setup quote does not answer those recurring questions. Keep the written scope and calendar alongside the structure decision.
