# How to Get a Digital Business Identity (DBID) If You Sell Online

> **In short:** Many people assume you need to register a company with RJSC before you can sell products on a website or Facebook page. In reality, selling online requires a separate identity called the Digital Business Identity, or DBID. It isn't company registration or a trade license — it's a distinct registration under the Ministry of Commerce, which RJSC issues.[^dbid-guideline-2022] Even without a trade license, you can apply with just an NID and a live website or Facebook page, and there's no government fee.[^prothomalo-en-dbid] The problem isn't the law, it's the execution: more than ten thousand applications have come in since 2022, but only 1,870 businesses had received a certificate as of November 2025. RJSC itself has acknowledged budget and staffing shortages.[^prothomalo-dbid-2025] So apply today, but don't expect the certificate quickly.

---

## What DBID actually is

In 2021, a cluster of e-commerce platforms including Evaly and E-orange took advance payments and never delivered products, defrauding customers of enormous sums. According to a Daily Star report, one Bangladesh Bank estimate put these platforms' withdrawals at roughly 60.5 billion taka in just four months.[^daily-star-dbid-2022] Right after this scandal, the Ministry of Commerce issued the Digital Commerce Operation Guideline 2021, and on 6 February 2022 launched the Digital Business Identification, or DBID, system.[^fe-dbid-2025] The goal was singular: separate real sellers from fraudulent ones.

RJSC handles DBID registration, but it isn't like company registration. The Ministry of Commerce's Central Digital Commerce Cell owns the policy and oversight of the whole system; RJSC just runs the application process.[^fe-dbid-2025] The 2022 guideline required businesses operating at the time to apply within 90 days of publication. The process was later formally relaunched in May 2024 with a revised guideline and terms.[^fe-dbid-2025]

---

## Who needs a DBID

Under the guideline, nearly every business that sells products or services online or through digital channels needs a DBID:[^daily-star-dbid-2022]

- E-commerce businesses running their own website or app
- F-commerce and social-commerce sellers operating through a Facebook page or group
- Marketplaces or digital commerce platforms with multiple sellers

A business can be a company, a sole proprietorship, or have no registration at all — the DBID requirement doesn't change based on that. A business with multiple branches still gets issued only one DBID card.[^fe-dbid-2025]

---

## DBID, company registration, and trade license are three separate things

This is where the biggest misconception forms: the assumption that you need to register a company first to sell online. In reality, these are three separate things.

| Item | Company registration | Trade license | DBID |
|---|---|---|---|
| Issued by | RJSC | City corporation or municipality | RJSC (under the Ministry of Commerce) |
| What it proves | The business is a separate legal entity | Permission to operate a business in a specific area | A registered identity for selling online or digitally |
| Can you sell online without it | Yes, as a [sole proprietorship](/registration/sole-proprietorship) or with no registration at all | Yes, with just an NID and a website/page | Not supposed to, under the guideline |

That means even a Facebook page run from home, with no business registration of any kind, falls under DBID. Conversely, a registered [private limited company](/registration/private-limited) selling on a website or Facebook still needs a separate DBID. Company registration doesn't substitute for it.[^daily-star-dbid-2022]

---

## How to apply, and what you need

You apply through the official portal dbid.gov.bd, which logs you in through the government's mygov.bd national identity system.[^legalseba-dbid] What's typically required with the application:[^daily-star-dbid-2022][^fe-dbid-2025]

- The applicant's (owner's or chairman's) NID number
- A working email and mobile number
- A link to your website or Facebook business page
- A trade license, if you have one — its absence doesn't block the application
- Proof of business address; if it's a rented home, the rental agreement

Once RJSC receives the application, it checks whether another registered DBID already exists under the same name, and whether the website or page has its terms of service written in Bangla. There's no government fee — registration is free.[^prothomalo-en-dbid] Once approved, displaying the DBID number on your website or page is mandatory.[^daily-star-dbid-2022] To get a physical DBID card, you need membership in the SME Foundation, e-CAB, or another government-approved trade body — confirm this requirement with your own organization.[^fe-dbid-2025]

## The real situation right now

This is where the gap between the law and reality is widest. From launch in February 2022 through February 2025, 10,218 applications came in through the portal, but only 1,458 — 14.76 percent — received a certificate. Most of the rest were rejected over paperwork gaps, like being unable to get a landlord's NID or a website missing Bangla-language terms of service.[^fe-dbid-2025] By 26 November 2025, the total number of certified businesses had risen to 1,870, with roughly 12,000 applications still pending. RJSC itself had told the Ministry of Commerce it was facing technical issues, budget constraints, and a shortage of skilled staff, and that no separate allocation existed for this in the FY2025-26 budget. One applicant reportedly applied four times over two years without receiving a certificate.[^prothomalo-dbid-2025]

The total number of DBIDs issued doesn't match across sources. An April 2026 opinion piece put the total issued at 15,000 to 20,000, against an estimated 500,000 online sellers nationwide.[^dhakajournal-dbid-2026] That figure doesn't match RJSC's own count (1,870 as of November 2025), and the piece doesn't specify where its number comes from. One thing can be said with confidence: the number of businesses that have gotten a DBID is still very small compared to how many actually sell online. For an accurate, current count, check the portal or RJSC's latest figures rather than treating any single report's number as final.

The barriers aren't uniform either. Home-based entrepreneurs, particularly women, often struggle to get a trade license or rental agreement, since many landlords won't hand over their own NID for a tenant's business paperwork.[^fe-dbid-2025][^dhakajournal-dbid-2026] Amid 2026 discussions of new digital-economy laws, proposals have surfaced to simplify DBID and give small sellers a grace period. But this remains a proposal, not a finalized rule.[^dhakapress-dbid-2026]

## What happens if you don't get one

Under the guideline, running an online business without a DBID isn't supposed to happen.[^daily-star-dbid-2022] But no official source clearly specifies a fine or penalty amount — this part needs verification. In practice, the bigger problem so far isn't a fine, but the difficulty of proving you're a legitimate business without one when applying for a bank loan, payment gateway, or merchant account. Industry voices including e-CAB want banks to treat DBID as proof of legitimacy the way they treat a trade license, which implies not every bank weighs it equally yet.[^egovreview-dbid] So treat not having a DBID not as "I haven't been caught yet," but as a real obstacle to growing the business.

---

## What to do now

- The later you apply, the further back in line you'll be. Apply now, no matter how small the business.
- Handle the two most common rejection reasons in advance: make sure NID details match, and add Bangla-language terms of service to your website or page.
- If you're a member of e-CAB, the SME Foundation, or another government-approved association, mention it in your application.
- Plan for the certificate to take months, sometimes a year, to arrive. Move other paperwork — trade license, TIN — forward separately in the meantime, rather than waiting on DBID.
- If your application keeps getting rejected or you're unsure about the process, talk directly to e-CAB or a business consultant.
