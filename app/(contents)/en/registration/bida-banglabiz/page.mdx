# Invest Bangladesh OSS and BanglaBiz: Five Approvals in One Application

> **In short:** Setting up a company has meant knocking on separate doors — <Term name="rjsc-name-clearance">name clearance</Term> at RJSC, TIN at NBR, trade licence at the city corporation. The second version of BanglaBiz, launched on 1 February 2026, brings five of those approvals into a single application: name clearance, a temporary bank account, company incorporation, <Term name="e-tin">e-TIN</Term>, and the trade licence. The platform's claim is that registration completes in three working days.[^banglabiz-launch] Alongside it, the One Stop Service now operated by Invest Bangladesh carried 142 services from 47 agencies under predecessor BIDA as of April 2026.[^bss-oss-services] The plain read: worth trying, but the three-day figure is the platform's own design promise, not an independently measured outcome. Apply through BanglaBiz — and keep the direct RJSC route in mind as well.

The two names get confused constantly, so start by separating them.

---

## Invest Bangladesh OSS and BanglaBiz are not the same thing

**Invest Bangladesh OSS**, formerly called BIDA OSS, is the single-window services portal for investors. The official link still opens at `bidaquickserv.org`; that legacy address remains current even though the authority's name has changed. Bangladesh Sangbad Sangstha reported on 19 April 2026 that the portal then carried 142 services integrated from 47 agencies, and that predecessor BIDA planned to grow this to over 150 services across 60 institutions.[^bss-oss-services]

**BanglaBiz** is the digital gateway built jointly by predecessor BIDA and the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA). The first version launched on 28 September 2025 as an information portal. The second version arrived on 1 February 2026, and that is where the Business Starter Package appeared.[^banglabiz-launch]

On 23 August 2026, BIDA, BEZA and PPPA began operating as **Invest Bangladesh**. The official launch notice says existing investor services will continue and Invest Bangladesh will continue to operate BanglaBiz.[^invest-bangladesh-transition]

Put simply: Invest Bangladesh OSS is the service portal, and BanglaBiz is the front door that brings several startup tasks together.

---

## What the Business Starter Package actually contains

The starter package pulls five approvals into a single application:[^banglabiz-launch][^bss-banglabiz]

| Approval | Usually issued by |
|---|---|
| Name clearance | RJSC |
| Temporary bank account | A listed bank |
| Company incorporation | RJSC |
| e-TIN | NBR |
| Trade licence | City corporation or municipality |

Why those five matter together becomes clear from the sequence. As [What to do after company registration](/en/registration/after-registration) sets out, no TIN blocks the trade licence, and no trade licence blocks the bank account. Most of the lost time comes from waiting on one thing to unlock the next. The package is an attempt to break exactly that chain.

It also introduces the **BanglaBiz ID, or BBID**, a unique business identifier. One login gets you into the systems of several investment promotion agencies, and application status is tracked in one place.[^banglabiz-launch] At launch, the central interface linked the OSS portals of five bodies — BIDA, BEZA, BEPZA, the Hi-Tech Park Authority, and BSCIC.[^banglabiz-launch] BIDA and BEZA are now part of Invest Bangladesh; BEPZA, the Hi-Tech Park Authority and BSCIC were not part of that merger.[^invest-bangladesh-transition]

---

## How to read the three-day claim

The announcements state that these five approvals can be completed within three working days.[^banglabiz-launch][^bss-banglabiz] Two things are worth holding in mind alongside that number.

First, it is a promise from how the system is designed, not a measurement of how long applications actually take. The package only launched in February 2026, and as of this page's writing (5 August 2026) no published figure shows what share of applications genuinely finished in three days.

Second, as [RJSC fees and timelines](/en/registration/rjsc-fees) shows, on the direct RJSC route time expands when documents don't match or a query comes back. A single-window portal doesn't remove those mismatches — it only puts the applications in one place. Inconsistent paperwork will draw a query here too.

So don't set a contract, launch, or investment date against three days. Leave yourself room, and get the documents right in advance.

## Which one applies to you

| If you are | Then |
|---|---|
| Starting a domestically-owned private limited company | Try the BanglaBiz starter package first; fall back to the direct RJSC route if it stalls |
| Starting as a sole proprietorship | RJSC isn't involved; the trade licence is the main task |
| Bringing in foreign equity for an eligible industrial or service project | Check whether Invest Bangladesh registration applies, then use <Term name="bida-oss">Invest Bangladesh OSS</Term> if it does[^invest-bangladesh-scope] |
| Needing a work permit, visa recommendation, or sector approval | Check Invest Bangladesh OSS's current service list |
| Setting up in an economic zone, EPZ, or hi-tech park | Use Invest Bangladesh for economic zones, BEPZA for EPZs, or the Hi-Tech Park Authority for hi-tech parks |

One point deserves to be stated plainly. Invest Bangladesh's current FAQ says registration is available to manufacturing and service industries, but is **not required for commercial and trading activities**.[^invest-bangladesh-scope] A foreign shareholder alone therefore does not prove that every company needs this registration. RJSC incorporation, the banking trail and Bangladesh Bank reporting remain separate questions. The foreign-ownership steps are in [Starting a foreign-owned company](/en/registration/foreign-owned-company).

## What could not be verified

A few things could not be confirmed while writing this page, and it would be unwise to plan around them:

- any independent measure of how well the three-working-day claim holds in practice
- whether the starter package's trade licence step works across every city corporation and municipality in the country
- the individual timeline and fee for each service
- whether every label inside the legacy OSS interface has already changed from BIDA or BEZA to Invest Bangladesh; use the service name and application status, not the old logo, to identify the current process

Before applying, log into the portal yourself and check the current steps, fees, and document list. A service appearing on a list and that service working today are two different things.

## Checklist

- [ ] Opened a BanglaBiz account and read the starter package's current steps and document list
- [ ] Prepared 3–5 name options, NID, photos, and address documents in advance
- [ ] Fixed the share split and directors' details in writing
- [ ] Did not commit to a contract or launch date on the strength of three working days
- [ ] Checked whether Invest Bangladesh registration applies before treating it as a required foreign-investment step
- [ ] Checked Invest Bangladesh OSS's current services if a work permit or another approval is involved
- [ ] Know the direct RJSC steps as a fallback if the portal stalls
- [ ] Kept every application number, challan, and receipt in one folder

Rule of thumb: **A single-window portal cuts the running around, not the paperwork mismatches. Apply through BanglaBiz, but prepare the documents well enough that they'd pass at RJSC's counter directly.**
