# Private Limited, OPC, or Sole Proprietorship: A Decision Guide

> **In short:** Three questions settle it for most founders: is there one owner or several, is ownership likely to be shared within the next year, and how much does limited liability matter. A foreign shareholder overrides whatever those three answers are and sends you down a separate path. Walk through the questions below and most readers land on their structure in two minutes. The full cost and timeline comparison lives on [a separate page](/en/registration/structure-comparison) — this one is just the decision path.

---

## Question 1: One Owner, or Several?

**One owner** – go to Question 2.

**Several owners** (co-founders, partners, or family starting together) – skip to Question 4. A sole proprietorship or OPC isn't legally available to multiple owners at all.[^companies-act-structure]

---

## Question 2 (Solo): Is Ownership Likely to Be Shared Within 12 Months?

Answer "yes" if a co-founder, investor, or family member getting shares is even on your mind, whether or not it's decided.

**Yes, it's likely.** Plan for [private limited](/en/registration/private-limited), but an ordinary private company needs at least two members at incorporation. Do not add a nominal co-owner just to fill that requirement. Agree real ownership first, and compare waiting, an eligible OPC, or a suitable interim structure. A later change may require updating the <Term name="trade-license">trade license</Term>, bank account and contracts.

**No, I want to run this alone.** Go to Question 3.

---

## Question 3 (Solo, No Sharing Planned): How Much Does Limited Liability Matter, and Can You Raise BDT 2.5 Million?

This is really two conditions at once — both need to be true for the second option to make sense.

**You understand and can accept personal liability.** A [sole proprietorship](/en/registration/sole-proprietorship) may fit. It has less company paperwork, but business debts can reach personal assets. Low capital or a need for bank credit does not make that risk disappear.

**Limited liability matters, and BDT 2.5 million is on hand or raisable.** Check all the eligibility conditions, including preceding-year turnover, in the [OPC guide](/en/registration/opc). You get a limited-liability company without a co-founder, though bank loans can be harder since there's no separate guarantor to offer — the OPC guide has a real example of that risk.[^opc-capital]

---

## Question 4 (Multiple Owners): Will Ownership Be Split into Shares, or Is a Profit-Sharing Agreement Enough?

**Just profit-sharing, small-scale service or trading, no rush to scale.** Consider a partnership. Partners agree to share the profits of a business, and one partner can bind the firm when acting in the usual course of that business. There is no share capital or board of directors.[^partnership-act-structure] [Founder agreement](/en/cofounders/founder-agreement) covers what a written agreement needs to cover.

**Ownership split into shares, an investor might join, or you want larger corporate contracts.** Look at [private limited](/en/registration/private-limited). Settle the equity split and <Term name="vesting">vesting</Term> in writing before registering — see [equity splits and vesting](/en/cofounders/equity-and-vesting).

---

## Any Branch: A Foreign Shareholder Changes the Path

Whatever the four questions above answer, if any shareholder is not a Bangladeshi citizen or capital is coming in from abroad, go straight to [basics of starting a foreign-owned company](/en/registration/foreign-owned-company). It covers the temporary bank account and encashment certificate, plus whether Invest Bangladesh registration applies to the activity.[^bida-foreign-structure]

## Quick Results Table

| Your answers | Structure |
|---|---|
| Solo, no sharing planned, liability isn't a big worry | Sole proprietorship |
| Solo, no sharing planned, liability matters, capital available | OPC |
| Solo, but sharing is likely | Plan private limited once there are at least two genuine members |
| Multiple owners, profit-sharing only | Partnership |
| Multiple owners, shares | Private limited |
| Foreign shareholder, any answer above | Foreign-owned company path |

Rule of thumb: **Match the structure to real ownership, liability, eligibility and compliance capacity. If personal liability is unacceptable but OPC conditions are not met, resolve that gap before taking on risky work.**
