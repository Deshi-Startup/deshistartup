# Case Studies

Explore how Bangladeshi startups began, found customers and built their businesses. The case studies cover the full story, including setbacks and lessons, with links to the interviews and reports behind them.

The articles draw on founder interviews, company announcements and independent reporting. Each study identifies company-reported figures and gaps in the public evidence so you can judge the story in context.
