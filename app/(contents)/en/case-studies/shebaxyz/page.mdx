# Sheba.xyz

<CaseCompany slug="shebaxyz" locale="en" />

> **In short:** Sheba.xyz set out to make home services easier to find and trust. It changed how it managed service quality, but the wider business still faced repeated funding crises. April 2026 reporting describes job cuts and overdue pay.[^ds-crisis] For a new founder, the warning is clear: test demand beyond your first neighbourhood, and check whether cash can support the promises you make to customers and workers.

This case is for founders connecting customers with service workers, or adding new products to an existing business. It examines useful choices alongside serious setbacks. It does not establish that Sheba has closed or that every business under its name has the same finances.

## A small start in Mirpur DOHS

Whom do you call when a pipe breaks? What should the job cost? Will the worker do it properly? In a 2016 interview, co-founder Adnan Imtiaz Halim described finding reliable service providers as the problem Sheba wanted to solve.[^fs-launch]

A 2019 profile names three co-founders: Adnan Imtiaz Halim, Ilmul Haque Sajib and Abu Naser Md. Shoaib. It describes an early call centre serving Mirpur DOHS.[^ds-profile] A separate 2018 founder account says the directors went door-to-door.[^pa-early]

Starting in a small area gave the team a chance to speak to customers and follow actual jobs. That is a useful way to learn before expanding.

In March 2022, Adnan said earlier trials in three cities outside Dhaka had failed because the market was not ready.[^fs-model] That was his explanation. It does not mean demand could never develop there.

**What to copy:** begin where you can observe the whole job. Before entering another city, check whether people there will book, pay and book again. Keep track of the cost of finding customers, travel, support and fixing mistakes. A successful first neighbourhood does not answer those questions for the next one.

## Matching people was not enough

Adnan said the early model checked providers and offered several booking channels.[^fs-launch] The 2019 profile describes prices, ratings and customer feedback.[^ds-profile] These could help a customer choose. They did not prove that every job went well.

The local lesson is practical: a worker entering someone's home carries a trust problem that an app listing alone cannot solve. If your company promises the result, decide who checks the work, who handles a complaint and who pays to fix it. Budget for those tasks before advertising the promise.

## More products created more things to fund

The wider Sheba business moved beyond household services. sManager helped small businesses keep records. sBusiness offered business tools and services to companies. Startup Bangladesh's portfolio describes the intended connection between the marketplace, those records and business customers.[^sbl-portfolio] An intended connection does not prove that one product successfully paid for another.

Selling a household job and selling access to software are different offers. Each needs its own costs and payment plan. A useful connection between products is not enough to show that the whole business can pay its bills.

The concern was visible before the later crisis. In December 2019, Future Startup's Ruhul Kader questioned why merchants already using the marketplace needed a separate paid tool, and how software subscriptions would fit with marketplace commissions.[^fs-smanager] That was contemporary analysis, not proof that sManager caused the funding problems.

For a new founder, each added product needs its own answer: who pays, what they pay for, what it costs to serve them, and which existing business covers any shortfall. A large number of users or transactions cannot answer that on its own. Check [the costs of one sale](/en/metrics/unit-economics) before treating growth as financial health.

## A rescue did not end the funding risk

Sheba had early support. Grameenphone's May 2016 release names it among the first teams in its accelerator, a programme for young companies. Each received more than Tk 10 lakh in early investment, plus workspace and other support.[^gp-accelerator] Startup Bangladesh's own portfolio lists 2021 as its investment year.[^sbl-portfolio]

In February 2023, Adnan recalled telling employees about a crisis in May 2022. He said potential foreign investors had asked for changes to the company's structure and ownership.[^ds-interview] A later report gives his explanation for the missed investment: problems with company registration and approval to receive the money.[^ds-comeback] That account does not establish a rule that every Bangladeshi startup must move to Singapore.

In December 2023, Adnan said Sheba had raised Tk 32 crore locally since May 2023 and paid Tk 7 crore in overdue salaries and vendor bills. Another Tk 18 crore was planned.[^ds-comeback] Planned money, received money and settled bills are separate facts. This recovery account did not prove that all dues were settled or that the business could fund itself.

The lesson is to plan against money the company can actually use. A funding discussion cannot pay today's payroll. Keep a plan for a delayed or smaller round, and cut new commitments before overdue promises become the way the business survives. [Cash and profit can tell different stories](/en/metrics/cashflow-vs-profit).

## Workers carried the cost of the next crisis

On 26 April 2026, Adnan told The Daily Star that more than 300 jobs had been cut, leaving around 110 employees. Salaries were paid in instalments. Expected investment was delayed and revenue had fallen.[^ds-crisis] Those are April statements. They do not establish today's headcount or explain every loss.

Asia Post reported on 4 March 2026 that a former chief operating officer had complained about tax-payment receipts not being provided. It reported an 18 February warrant against Adnan and a separate labour-court claim for unpaid salary. Adnan said the company was cooperating over disputed dues. The report calls him Sheba.xyz's non-executive chairman.[^ap-dispute]

The employee's suspicion of unpaid tax is an allegation. This study found no final judgment or settlement. Do not read a reported warrant as proof of tax fraud.

The human cost should not disappear behind a funding story. Workers need wages when they are due, not just confidence in the next investor. Founders need clear payment records, realistic commitments and a plan for reducing work before cash runs out.

## What remains unknown

Public sources checked on 4 October 2026 do not establish:

- the current profit or loss of each business;
- how group cash was shared between home services, software and payments;
- whether every overdue employee and vendor has been paid;
- the outcome of the reported legal dispute;
- current booking volumes or independently measured service quality.

Sheba.xyz's official LinkedIn page carried a hiring notice with a 20 September 2026 deadline and a ten-year anniversary post.[^sheba-current] That shows continued public activity. It does not prove financial recovery or completed jobs today.

The sources use different company names: Sheba Platform Limited in Startup Bangladesh's portfolio, and Sheba.xyz Services Limited in the hiring post.[^sbl-portfolio][^sheba-current] Those sources do not establish the legal relationship between them or who owes each debt.

## What a founder can take from this case

Copy the small, observable first test and the willingness to change a weak service model. Carry the cost of quality control into your prices and staffing plan.

Before expanding, test demand in the next market. Before adding products, check each product's costs and how any losses will be covered. Keep payroll plans grounded in available cash. The evidence does not prove which product caused Sheba's later losses. It does show why an earlier rescue was not enough evidence of lasting financial health.

For another Bangladesh example, compare [Chaldal's expansion and cash pressures](/en/case-studies/chaldal). The operations differ, but both stories require separating a useful service from a company that can keep paying its bills.
