# How to File the Income Tax and VAT Returns

> **In short:** A company's return is more than a form. The Income Tax Act requires three things to accompany it: audited financial statements, evidence of compliance with the National Board of Revenue's verification criteria, and a separate computation sheet explaining the difference between the profit in the accounts and the income in the return.[^ita-return-contents] On both sides the order is the same — pay first, file second.[^ita-payment-before-filing][^vat-filing] And the most common mistake comes at the last step: the Act states plainly that a return is not deemed complete merely because you obtained an acknowledgement of receipt.[^ita-incomplete-return] So don't close the file after submitting — keep the paperwork within reach in case a notice arrives.

Which return falls due when is in [the tax calendar](/en/tax/tax-calendar). This page is about the act of filing.

---

## What a company's income tax return must carry

The Act puts companies directly on the list of persons required to file, whatever the income.[^ita-return-contents] The return goes in on the Board's prescribed form, accompanied by the prescribed schedules, statements, accounts, and annexures.[^ita-return-contents]

For a company, the Act names three documents specifically:[^ita-return-contents]

- audited financial statements
- evidence of compliance with the criteria the Board prescribes from time to time for verification
- a separate computation sheet explaining the difference between the profit shown in the financial statements and the income shown in the return

The third is the one people leave out. Accounting profit and taxable income are not the same thing — depreciation treatment, disallowed expenses, and exemptions pull them apart. That gap has to be shown on paper.

Where there are international transactions within the Act’s transfer-pricing definition, a statement of international transactions goes in with the return too.[^ita-return-contents]

Who signs? For a company, the Act says the principal officer.[^ita-return-contents]

---

## Pay first, file second — on both sides

**Income tax.** The tax payable must be paid on or before the date of filing. The amount is A − B, where A is the tax payable on the basis of the return (or under the minimum tax provision, whichever is higher) and B is tax paid at source plus advance tax.[^ita-payment-before-filing] Failing to pay it without reasonable cause makes the person an assessee-in-default.[^ita-payment-before-filing]

**VAT.** The net payable tax must be paid before the return for that period is filed.[^vat-filing]

Both create the same practical problem: two jobs on the last day. Generate the challan, get the money to the bank, then file. Leave it to the deadline and one stall takes both down.

## What self-assessment changes

The Finance Act 2026 introduced section 170A. Individuals file under self-assessment; other taxpayers, including companies, may file for self-assessment under section 180 or assessment by the Deputy Commissioner under section 183. It is no longer accurate to say every filer must use the same route.[^ita-self-assessment]

Under the amended section 180, a return submitted for self-assessment is treated as assessed only when section 169 is complied with and tax is paid under section 173. This does not remove the power to examine the return, ask for missing documents or carry out an audit.[^ita-self-assessment]

## Online or on paper

NBR runs its e-Return portal at etaxnbr.gov.bd and publishes a user manual for it.[^nbr-ereturn]

Do not reuse an old year's paper-filing permission deadline. The cited 2025 report described an individual-taxpayer mandate, exceptions and a 31 October application date for that year's arrangement; it does not establish the deadline for a later tax year.[^nbr-ereturn] Check NBR's current order for your taxpayer category and year before choosing online or paper filing.[^nbr-ereturn]

Whether a company can file through this portal — or must — could not be confirmed as of this page's writing on 5 August 2026. Rather than assume, check on the portal itself or with your tax adviser.

VAT returns go through the VAT Online portal. The full monthly workflow is in [VAT returns after getting a BIN](/en/tax/monthly-vat-return).

## An acknowledgement is not the finish line

This part of the Act is unusually explicit, and almost nobody knows it.

Where section 169 or a direction of the Board has not been followed, the return is treated as incomplete.[^ita-incomplete-return] The Deputy Commissioner of Taxes then sends a notice, with reasons specified, asking for the relevant information, verification, statement, or document within a stated time.[^ita-incomplete-return]

Under section 176 as amended in 2026, failing to comply properly with the notice can make the return void or ineffective. The Deputy Commissioner must then use the applicable return-notice procedure under section 172, or section 212(1) after the relevant tax year. Respond within the notice's deadline and retain proof of your response.[^ita-incomplete-return]

And the line at the end is worth memorising: a return shall not be deemed a complete return merely by reason of obtaining an acknowledgement of receipt.[^ita-incomplete-return]

A “Submitted” status or counter stamp proves receipt; it does not by itself prove completeness or settle the tax assessment. Answering the notice on time is the real work.

## What to have ready before filing

- audited financial statements and the auditor's report
- the computation sheet reconciling profit with taxable income
- certificates for tax deducted at source and challans for advance tax
- bank statements and documentation for significant transactions
- last year's return and assessment paperwork
- on the VAT side: invoices, payment receipts, and filing acknowledgements for every tax period covered by the return

After filing, keep the acknowledgement, the challans, and a list of the annexures together. These are exactly the documents a bank, an investor, or a tender will ask for.

---

## When to bring in a professional

A company's first return, foreign transactions, a large exemption claim, or an error surfacing in a prior year's return — any of those four is a reason to sit down with a chartered accountant or tax adviser. The audit legally has to be done by a chartered accountant anyway, so the relationship is needed regardless.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] Completed the audit and have the financial statements in hand
- [ ] Prepared the computation sheet reconciling profit with taxable income
- [ ] Obtained the principal officer's signature
- [ ] Gathered evidence of tax deducted at source and advance tax paid
- [ ] Paid the tax due before filing
- [ ] Paid the net VAT payable before filing that return too
- [ ] Kept the acknowledgement, challans, and annexure list in one folder
- [ ] Kept the paperwork accessible in case a notice arrives

Rule of thumb: **An acknowledgement means submitted, not complete. File the paperwork in full — and keep the file open for a few months after.**
