# VAT Returns After Getting a BIN: Periods, Credits and Filing

> **In short:** Keep the books monthly, but do not assume the return is still due every month on the 15th. From 1 July 2026, the ordinary VAT return covers three tax periods and is due within 15 days after the third period ends. Monthly filing is optional, with filing allowed during the following month. Special classes, including nil-return filers, have a 20-day deadline for the three-period return.[^vat-return-deadline] Before filing, reconcile sales, eligible input credits and adjustments, pay the amount due, and save the acknowledgement.

This page is for a business that already has a <Term name="vat-bin">BIN</Term>. First check whether the certificate says full VAT registration or turnover-tax enlistment. The input-credit calculation below applies to a registered business entitled to credits; an enlisted turnover-tax business does not use it. See [BIN registration](/en/tax/vat-bin) and [turnover tax](/en/tax/vat-thresholds).

## Set the filing period before the deadline

The Finance Act 2026 replaced section 64 of the VAT Act. The ordinary schedule covers three monthly tax periods. A taxpayer may instead submit voluntarily for each monthly period during the following month. Government, semi-government and autonomous bodies, banks, insurers and nil-return filers have the special 20-day deadline when filing for three periods.[^vat-return-deadline]

| Filing basis | Deadline | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary three-period return | Within 15 days after the third period | July–September 2026: 15 October 2026 |
| Special classes, including nil returns | Within 20 days after the third period | July–September 2026: 20 October 2026 |
| Voluntary monthly return | During the following month | July 2026: by 31 August 2026 |

The ordinary 15-day rule expressly moves a government-holiday deadline to the next working day. Do not assume every other deadline has the same extension. Check any applicable NBR order and the period selected in [VAT Online](https://vat.gov.bd), especially when changing filing frequency. Keep monthly records even when submitting one return for three periods.

## Work out the tax from the records

The net-tax calculation is output tax and supplementary duty, less eligible input credits, plus increasing adjustments, less decreasing adjustments. Pay the net amount before submitting the return.[^vat-net-tax]

For an **illustrative** business entitled to input credits:

| Item | Taka |
|---|---:|
| Output VAT | 20,000 |
| Eligible input credit | −12,000 |
| Increasing adjustments | +2,000 |
| Decreasing adjustments | −1,000 |
| Net payable | **9,000** |

The example teaches the reconciliation, not a tax rate or an automatic right to claim every purchase. Match each entry to its invoice, import document or withholding certificate. [Mushak invoices](/en/tax/mushak-63) and [TDS/VDS](/en/tax/tds-vds) explain the supporting records.

## Keep a deadline for each input credit

The old three-period claim window is obsolete. The Finance Ordinance 2025 extended section 46(1)(c) from the purchase period plus four succeeding tax periods to the purchase period plus **six** succeeding tax periods.[^vat-input-credit] A monthly tax period and a return covering three periods are different things: do not count six quarterly returns.

For example, a qualifying July purchase falls within July and the six following monthly periods through January. Other eligibility and documentary conditions still apply. Record the purchase period, amount, supporting document, last eligible period and the return in which the credit was actually claimed. Chase missing invoices while there is time to resolve them.

A purchase invoice by itself does not establish eligibility. Check the current section 46 conditions for the supply, payment method, documents and applicable VAT treatment before taking a credit.

> **Warning: Pay over BDT 1 lakh in cash, and the credit is lost.** Under section 46(1)(a) of the VAT Act, if the value of a taxable supply exceeds one lakh taka and consideration is paid in cash instead of through an approved banking channel or mobile financial service (MFS), no input tax credit can be claimed on that supply. Certain purchases are also permanently blocked from credits by law, including passenger vehicles (and their repair/fuel) and entertainment expenses, unless your core business is dealing in them.

## Close, check, pay and file

1. **Reconcile sales.** Match invoices, returns and credit notes to the sales register.
2. **Review purchases.** Separate eligible credits from blocked or unproven claims; record each claim's expiry period.
3. **Reconcile withholding.** Match increasing and decreasing adjustments to the corresponding certificates and payments.
4. **Select the right form and period.** Use the current portal form for your registration and credit entitlement; do not assume every BIN uses the same return.
5. **Check and pay.** Have another person review the reconciliation, then pay the net liability before submission.
6. **Submit and preserve evidence.** Save the return, acknowledgement and payment record alongside the underlying invoices.

Set an internal close date ahead of the legal deadline. A nil period does not mean the return obligation disappears. For a filing gap or incorrect return, identify every affected period and use the current correction procedure with your VAT adviser; do not silently roll old figures into a new period.

## What errors can cost

Unclaimed credits can expire; unsupported credits and omitted output tax can create tax, interest and penalty exposure. Do not use a blanket “twice the amount” penalty estimate. Section 85 distinguishes offences, and the Finance Act 2026 amended specified irregular-adjustment penalties to a 30–50% range.[^vat-return-penalty] The applicable consequence depends on the actual offence and current law.

## Checklist

- [ ] Confirmed registration versus enlistment and the applicable return form
- [ ] Recorded monthly tax periods, filing frequency and the correct deadline
- [ ] Kept sales, purchase and withholding records reconciled
- [ ] Recorded each input credit's eligible period and supporting evidence
- [ ] Paid the net liability before filing
- [ ] Saved the submitted return and acknowledgement, including nil periods
- [ ] Assigned an owner and an earlier internal close date

Next, add the dates to [the tax calendar](/en/tax/tax-calendar). The work is complete when the submitted return, payment and underlying records reconcile—not merely when the portal accepts a submission.
