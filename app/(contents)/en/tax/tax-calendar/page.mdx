# The Tax Calendar: What Is Due on Which Date

> **In short:** A company runs not one clock but four. With VAT registration, a return is due within 15 days of each month's end.[^vat-return] If you deduct tax from a vendor's bill, the withholding return is due by the 25th of every month.[^ita-withholding-return] If total income in the last income year exceeded 6 lakh taka, advance tax falls due four times a year.[^ita-advance-tax] And once a year comes the income tax return — for a company, Tax Day is the 15th day of the seventh month after the income year ends, so a 30 June year-end means 15 January.[^ita-tax-day] These dates don't depend on each other, so remembering one won't remind you of the rest. Put all of them on the calendar at the start of the year.

This page is the list of dates. How to actually file is in [How to file tax and VAT returns](/tax/filing-returns), and RJSC's own calendar is in [The RJSC filing calendar](/registration/annual-returns).

---

## Two dates every month

**The 15th — the VAT return.** Under the Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act 2012, the tax period for VAT is one month of the Christian calendar.[^vat-return] Every registered or enlisted person must file the return for each tax period within 15 days of that period expiring.[^vat-return] So July's return is due by 15 August.

Turnover tax runs on a different period: three-month periods ending 31 March, 30 June, 30 September, and 31 December.[^vat-return]

**The 25th — the withholding tax return.** Under the Income Tax Act 2023, companies, firms, associations of persons, private hospitals, clinics, and diagnostic centres must file a return of tax deducted or collected at source. The preceding month's return is due by the 25th of every month, or the next working day where the 25th is a weekly or public holiday.[^ita-withholding-return]

Note what the list says: companies, with no size threshold attached. The obligation arrives the moment you are registered as a company.

## Four dates a year: advance tax

If total income in the last income year exceeded 6 lakh taka, advance tax is payable in the current financial year.[^ita-advance-tax] It is split into four equal instalments of 25% each:[^ita-advance-tax]

| Instalment | Date |
|---|---|
| First | 15 September |
| Second | 15 December |
| Third | 15 March |
| Fourth | 15 June |

If the date falls on a public holiday, the instalment is paid on the next working day. A missed instalment is not forgiven — it compounds into the next one.[^ita-advance-tax]

Underpaying advance tax carries interest. Where advance tax plus tax deducted at source comes to less than 75% of the tax determined on regular assessment, simple interest of 10% per annum runs on the shortfall — and that rate is 50% higher if the return was not filed by Tax Day.[^ita-advance-tax]

## Once a year: working out your Tax Day

The Income Tax Act defines "Tax Day" separately. For a company it is the 15th day of the seventh month following the end of the income year — unless that date falls before the 15 September following the income year's end, in which case Tax Day is that 15 September.[^ita-tax-day] For an assessee other than a company, Tax Day is 30 November.[^ita-tax-day] If the day is a public holiday, it moves to the next working day.[^ita-tax-day]

Work it through. The income year ends 30 June 2026. The seventh month following June is January, giving 15 January 2027. That is not before 15 September 2026, so Tax Day stays 15 January 2027.

Most Bangladeshi companies have a 30 June year-end, because the Act defines the income year as the financial year immediately preceding the assessment year.[^ita-tax-day] Banks, insurance companies, and finance companies work differently — their income year is the 12 months beginning 1 January.[^ita-tax-day]

The Act is explicit that every assessee shall file the return on or before Tax Day.[^ita-tax-day]

## How the cost of being late is calculated

It grows from two directions, and both can be worked out in advance.

**On income tax.** Filing after Tax Day triggers a statutory formula adding 2% per month. The base is the tax you would have paid had you filed on Tax Day, less the advance tax and tax deducted at source already paid for that income year. Months are counted from the expiry of Tax Day, capped at 24, and a fraction of a month counts as a full month.[^ita-late-filing]

Say filing on Tax Day would have meant 5 lakh taka of tax, and 3 lakh had already gone in through advance and source tax. That leaves 2 lakh. Six months late adds 2,00,000 × 6 × 0.02 = 24,000 taka. Two years late stops the month count at 24, making it 96,000 taka.

**On VAT.** Failing to file the VAT or turnover tax return within the prescribed time carries a monetary penalty of 10,000 taka.[^vat-penalty] The Commissioner may permit late filing on application, but that permission cannot push the actual payment date out by more than one month, and it does not alter the liability for interest.[^vat-return]

## The whole year at a glance

For a company whose income year ends 30 June:

| When | What |
|---|---|
| 15th of every month | Previous month's VAT return |
| 25th of every month | Previous month's withholding tax return |
| 15 September | First advance tax instalment |
| 21 days after the AGM | Schedule X to RJSC |
| 30 days after the AGM | Audited balance sheet to RJSC |
| 15 December | Second advance tax instalment |
| 15 January | The company's income tax return — Tax Day |
| 15 March | Third advance tax instalment |
| 15 June | Fourth advance tax instalment |

The two RJSC dates hang off the AGM rather than a fixed calendar day. The Companies Act requires Schedule X within 21 days of the meeting and the balance sheet within 30.[^companies-act-filings] When the AGM itself must be held is covered in [The RJSC filing calendar](/registration/annual-returns).

Trade licence renewal is not on this list, because it varies by city corporation and municipality. Check your own licence's expiry and add it separately.

## What not to assume from this page

- **Rates.** These change with every Finance Act, so no rate appears here. Get current rates from NBR's publications or your accountant.
- **Deadline extensions.** NBR sometimes extends return deadlines. Don't plan on an extension arriving — treat one as a bonus if it comes.
- **Sector-specific dates.** Import/export, factory, and regulated-sector businesses carry additional renewal and reporting dates.

## Checklist

- [ ] Established exactly when the income year ends, and derived Tax Day from it
- [ ] Set the 15th and 25th as repeating monthly entries on the phone calendar
- [ ] Checked whether advance tax applies, and if so added all four instalment dates
- [ ] Added RJSC's 21-day and 30-day dates as soon as the AGM date was fixed
- [ ] Checked the trade licence expiry and noted the renewal date separately
- [ ] Filing every challan and receipt into one folder

Rule of thumb: **Four clocks run independently — two dates a month, four instalments a year, and one annual return. Remembering one won't remind you of the others, so put them all on the calendar at the start of the year.**
