# Applying for a BIN: Reconcile These Records Before Mushak-2.1

> **In short:** A BIN is the 9-digit business identifier in the VAT system. Both full <Term name="vat-bin">VAT registration</Term> and turnover-tax enlistment produce a BIN, so decide which route applies before starting: enlistment generally covers turnover above 30 lakh up to 50 lakh, and full registration covers turnover above 50 lakh. Create the VAT Online user for the correct person, complete the current Mushak-2.1 form, and then check the tax type, name, and effective date on the Mushak-2.3 certificate. Receiving a BIN starts invoice, return, and recordkeeping obligations.

## Who needs this, and when

You need this page if you are about to apply for a BIN — or have been told you need one — and you want the route decided correctly before touching the form. Registration and enlistment both produce a BIN and both start from the same Mushak-2.1 form, so the decision comes first.

Haven't confirmed whether you need a BIN at all? Start with [When Full VAT Registration or Turnover Tax Applies](/en/tax/when-vat-required).

This page covers an ordinary business applying through VAT Online. By the end you will have a filed application or a checked Mushak-2.3 certificate, plus the invoicing, bookkeeping, and return work assigned.

## BIN, TIN, and the Old 13-Digit Number

| Number | System | Identifies |
|---|---|---|
| **TIN** | Income tax | An individual, firm, or company taxpayer |
| **BIN** | VAT or turnover tax | A registered or enlisted business |

NBR's current FAQ states that the BIN in the current VAT system has 9 digits. NBR also maintains a separate search for legacy 13-digit BINs. Do not copy a legacy number into a new application. Use the current BIN shown on the Mushak-2.3 certificate.

It is normal for the same business to have both a TIN and BIN. They are not interchangeable. See the [e-TIN Guide](/en/tax/e-tin) for the income-tax side.

## Decide Between Registration and Enlistment

Two thresholds decide the route, and the preceding 12-month turnover is the only input:

<DataBars
  max={50}
  data={[
    { label: "Enlistment threshold", value: 30, display: "30 lakh" },
    { label: "Registration threshold", value: 50, display: "50 lakh" },
  ]}
/>

| Preceding 12-month turnover | Route |
|---|---|
| Below 30 lakh | Generally no obligation |
| Above 30 lakh, up to 50 lakh | Turnover-tax enlistment |
| Above 50 lakh | Full VAT registration |

**Full VAT registration:** Generally above BDT 5 million (50 lakh) of turnover over 12 months, for a special mandatory class, or voluntarily. A fully registered person issues Mushak-6.3 tax invoices.[^vat-registration-and-enlistment]

**Turnover-tax enlistment:** Generally above BDT 3 million (30 lakh) and up to BDT 5 million (50 lakh). The rate is 4% of turnover. An enlisted person issues Mushak-6.9 invoices.[^vat-registration-and-enlistment]

**Example:** An agency's books show BDT 4.5 million (45 lakh) of turnover over the preceding 12 months. The decision is a comparison, not a calculation: 45 is above 30 and not above 50, so the agency falls in the enlistment band. That one result changes the invoice (Mushak-6.9 instead of Mushak-6.3) and the tax (4% of turnover instead of VAT on each supply).

**Copy this:** Preceding 12-month turnover: ______ lakh. Band: below 30 / 30–50 / above 50. Route: no obligation / enlistment / registration.

**Keep in mind:** NBR's English VAT FAQ still describes older figures — registration above BDT 8 million and turnover tax at 3%. The Act governs, and it sets BDT 3 million and BDT 5 million with turnover tax at 4%.[^vat-registration-and-enlistment] The Finance Act 2026 also lets the Government notify fixed sector-specific turnover-tax amounts of up to BDT 200,000 in place of the 4% rate; until such a notification is issued, 4% continues to apply.[^finance-act-2026]

If the answer is unclear, first use [When Full VAT Registration or Turnover Tax Applies](/en/tax/when-vat-required) and [Turnover Tax and VAT Thresholds](/en/tax/vat-thresholds).

## Reconcile the Information Before Opening the Form

NBR's FAQ says that separate documents are not required to be submitted for registration or enlistment. Do not copy a long mandatory-upload list from an old blog. Keep these supporting records nearby so the form is accurate:

- NID or applicable identity document for an individual
- <Term name="incorp-certificate">Certificate of Incorporation</Term> and RJSC particulars for a company
- Registration and partner information for a firm
- e-TIN, where applicable
- Legal and trading names
- Full principal and branch addresses
- Bank-account information
- H.S. or service codes for the supplies
- Preceding and succeeding 12-month turnover calculations
- Import, export, and withholding-entity status

The 2026 amended Mushak-2.1 form requests e-TIN “where applicable.”[^mushak-registration-form] Give the information of the person obtaining the BIN. Do not substitute a director's personal TIN for the company's TIN.

## Apply Through VAT Online

The nine steps below fall into four phases. The hollow stretch is where applications usually lose time:

1. Open [VAT Online](https://vat.gov.bd) and create the user in the name of the person to be registered or enlisted.
2. Keep the mobile number and email under the business's control, not an agent's personal account.
3. Open the current Mushak-2.1 under registration and select the correct ownership type.
4. Enter the legal name, trading name, <Term name="e-tin">TIN</Term> where applicable, addresses, bank details, activity, and codes.
5. Reconcile the requested full registration or turnover-tax enlistment with the turnover calculation.
6. If there are branches, understand central and separate registration. One central registration generally covers the same entity's economic activity, while a branch with independent records may seek separate registration.
7. Download the draft or preserve the screens and ask another person to check the names, figures, codes, and addresses.
8. Submit and retain the acknowledgment and application number. Respond to any query by following the comment.
9. After the certificate is created, download Mushak-2.3 and check the BIN, tax type, effective date, and address.

There is no government fee for the online application. Any adviser charge is for the service. Do not promise a certificate date. NBR's FAQ refers both to three working days after verification and to instantaneous online registration for a correct application.[^vat-online-application] Validation, queries, and portal conditions can change the actual timing.

## Check Five Fields on the Certificate

- Legal name of the person
- Trading name, if any
- 9-digit BIN
- VAT registration or turnover-tax enlistment
- Effective date and business address

Do not issue invoices from an incorrect certificate. Use the portal's information-change procedure. Current Rules require many changes to be reported within 15 working days.[^vat-registration-rules]

You'll know the application is done when Mushak-2.3 shows the right name, tax type, BIN, effective date, and address — and someone owns the invoicing, bookkeeping, and return work that starts now.

## Work That Starts After the BIN

| Status | Invoice | Core work |
|---|---|---|
| Full VAT registration | Mushak-6.3 | Output VAT, input-tax credits, withholding VAT, and returns |
| Turnover-tax enlistment | Mushak-6.9 | 4% of turnover, sales, and invoice records |

The calendar is now the same for both. The tax period is one Gregorian calendar month, and since 1 July 2026 that applies to enlisted persons too — the separate quarterly turnover-tax period was abolished. Quarterly return filing is the default for both statuses, due by the 15th day after the quarter ends, with monthly filing available as an option and due by the last day of the following month.[^finance-act-2026]

Both statuses require the certificate to be displayed, the BIN to be used in specified records, and changes to be reported. A zero return may still have to be filed.[^vat-registration-rules] Assign ownership of invoicing, bookkeeping, and returns before applying.

A BIN is also becoming unavoidable outside the tax office. Since 1 July 2026, a valid BIN or proof of registration is required to open or operate a business current or STD account, to take a loan from a bank or non-bank financial institution, to renew a trade licence, to open an <Term name="mfs">MFS</Term> merchant account, to hold trade-association membership, to get electricity and gas connections in the business's name, and to register a vehicle with BRTA.[^finance-act-2026]

## Common Mistakes

- Treating a legacy 13-digit BIN as a new e-BIN
- Entering the TIN and BIN interchangeably
- Applying without distinguishing full registration from enlistment
- Treating an old blog's document list as the current mandatory list
- Guessing the goods or service code
- Registering with an agent's mobile number and email
- Issuing Mushak-6.3 without checking the certificate's tax type
- Failing to assign return ownership after receiving the BIN

## Checklist

- [ ] Recorded the decision to register or enlist
- [ ] Applying in the correct individual's, firm's, or company's name
- [ ] Reconciled the legal name, TIN, and RJSC particulars
- [ ] Verified the activity and codes
- [ ] Kept the 12-month turnover calculation
- [ ] Business controls the mobile, email, and login
- [ ] Checked all five fields on the Mushak-2.3 certificate
- [ ] Assigned invoicing, bookkeeping, and return responsibilities

Rule of thumb: **Check the tax type on the certificate before the BIN number. Registration and enlistment use the same identification system but create different work.**
