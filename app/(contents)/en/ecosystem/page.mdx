# Bangladesh Ecosystem: Find the Right Kind of Help

> **In short:** Start with the problem you need help solving. An investor, adviser, accelerator and customer can all be useful, but they do different jobs.

## Match the need to the organisation

| You need | Where to start | What to establish |
|---|---|---|
| Registration or an official process | [Government services](/en/directory/government-services) | Responsible authority and current procedure |
| Legal or accounting support | [Legal and accounting directory](/en/directory/legal-accounting) | Relevant expertise, scope and fees |
| Structured support and introductions | [Accelerators](/en/directory/accelerators) | Stage fit, time commitment and terms |
| Investment | [Investors](/en/directory/investors) | Investment fit and evidence required |
| Public funding options | [Government funding](/en/directory/government-funding) | Current eligibility, application window and conditions |
| A workspace or local connections | [Coworking](/en/directory/coworking) | Location, cost and whether the community fits your needs |
| Payment or delivery infrastructure | [Directory](/en/directory) | Service coverage, eligibility and operating terms |

A listing is a starting point for checking fit, not an endorsement or a promise of support. Verify current details directly; programs and application windows change.

## Prepare a useful request

```text
We serve [customer] with [offer or proposed solution].
Our current stage and evidence are [brief facts].
We need help with [one specific decision or obstacle].
We have already tried [action].
We are asking for [specific assistance or next step].
```

Share only the information needed for that request. Ask about the next step, any cost or commitment, and who will handle the enquiry. Record the response and follow-up date.

## Judge the help by what it enables

After a meeting, identify the decision, introduction or work that moved forward. Event attendance and collecting contacts are not substitutes for learning from customers or improving delivery.

For the funding decision itself, use [funding](/en/funding). For what to do at your current stage, use the [roadmap](/en/roadmap). This page helps you find support for that work.
