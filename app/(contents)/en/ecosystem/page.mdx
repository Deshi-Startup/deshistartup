# Bangladesh Startup Ecosystem: Who Does What and Where to Start

> **In short:** Identify the obstacle before choosing whom to approach. Customers test whether your offer matters; service providers help operate it; public bodies administer particular rules; investors supply capital on terms. A helpful introduction cannot substitute for customer evidence or an official approval.

Use this page to understand the people and organisations around a Bangladesh startup and choose a sensible next contact. You will leave with a short support map and a specific request. The directory adds names and current listing details; you do not need it to understand the roles below.

## Learn the different roles

Start with the role you need, rather than trying to attend every event or contact every investor:

| Actor | What they can contribute | What they cannot establish for you |
|---|---|---|
| Customers and users | Experience of the problem, use, purchases and feedback | A few responses do not prove a whole market |
| Co-founders and team | Skills, time, decisions and delivery responsibility | A strong CV does not settle commitment or ownership |
| Peers, communities and mentors | Practical experience, critique and introductions | Advice from another sector may not fit your circumstances |
| Incubators and accelerators | Structured support; program terms may include mentoring, workspace or capital | Admission is not proof of demand; benefits vary by program |
| Investors and lenders | Capital with ownership, repayment or other conditions | Money alone does not fix an unworkable offer |
| Professional and operating services | Accounting, legal work, payments, courier service or workspace | A listing or sales promise is not proof of suitability |
| Public bodies and portals | Defined registration, tax, permission or regulatory processes | One document or portal does not cover every obligation |

An **accelerator** is a program, not necessarily an investor. **Equity** means an ownership interest; a **loan** must be understood through its repayment terms. An introduction is access to a conversation, not a recommendation to accept the resulting terms.

## Know which public function you are looking for

Keep the authority responsible for the decision separate from the portal through which you apply. The official service pages below are starting points checked on 9 September 2026; fees, eligibility and the applicable process still need checking for your case.

| Function | Starting point | First question |
|---|---|---|
| Company incorporation, name clearance and company returns | RJSC | Which entity and application or filing are involved? |
| Income tax, VAT and customs services | NBR | Which taxpayer, activity and reporting period are involved? |
| Banking, payment-system and foreign-exchange rules | Bangladesh Bank; your bank/provider for the transaction | Which rule and licensed service cover the proposed transaction? |
| Trade license for an operating address | Relevant city corporation, municipality or union parishad | Which local authority and activity category cover this address? |
| Finding investment-related government services | BanglaBiz and the listed responsible agencies | Who actually decides this application, and where is its current checklist? |

RJSC's portal separates name-clearance, registration and post-registration services. NBR similarly separates income-tax, VAT, customs and payment services. Local trade licensing is a different administrative function. These distinctions explain why a startup may need to speak to more than one office.[^public-functions]

For a launch, write the actual activity, premises, entity and planned transaction before contacting an office. A broad description such as “an online startup” may hide the information needed to identify the right process.

## Match help to a real situation

**Fictional example:** A Dhaka software team has two small training-centre pilots. It has three obstacles: a customer has not accepted the delivered work, the founders have not recorded who owns the code, and a payment provider has requested documents. They initially consider applying to an accelerator to “solve everything.”

A better support map separates the jobs:

| Obstacle | First contact or action | Evidence of useful progress |
|---|---|---|
| Customer acceptance is unclear | Customer's named decision-maker; compare delivery with the agreed scope | Written acceptance or a specific correction list |
| Code ownership is unresolved | Founders and relevant creator; prepare agreements for qualified review | A documented ownership question and reviewed terms |
| Provider needs documents | Provider onboarding team and the authority issuing each requested document | Exact document names, entity/address requirements and remaining gaps |
| Team wants broader feedback | A mentor or program with relevant business-software experience | A concrete decision or introduction, not just a meeting |

They resolve the acceptance question first because they cannot learn whether the pilot worked without it. They investigate the document and ownership issues before making new commitments. They might still join a program, but can now say what assistance they want.

This is an ordering example, not a claim that these three issues are every startup's priorities. If safety or permission is unresolved, that may be the first constraint.

## Evaluate advice, programs and providers

A useful mentor can explain the context behind advice: customer type, stage, resources, what was tried and what happened. Ask which parts transfer to your business. A consumer-sales tactic may not help a long corporate purchasing process.

Before joining a program, check eligibility, dates, time commitment, costs, any ownership or reporting terms, and what support is actually included. Before hiring a provider, agree scope, fees, exclusions, who performs the work and how problems are handled. Confirm claims directly; the directory is not an endorsement.

Communities can help with peer feedback, introductions and finding collaborators. Use an existing student, professional or local network if it reaches the people relevant to your task. A Dhaka event is not automatically more useful than a nearby customer conversation.

## Prepare the request and follow through

The fictional team's message to the provider could be:

```text
We supply scheduling software to training centres.
We are preparing to accept payments through your service.
Please confirm which account type covers this activity and provide
its current document checklist, charges and settlement terms.
Our operating entity and address are [details].
Who can clarify any document mismatch before we submit?
```

Do not send confidential records merely to obtain an introduction. Share enough context to answer the specific question, then use the appropriate secure process if documents are required.

For your own next contact, copy:

```text
Obstacle and why it matters now:
Type of help needed:
Who is responsible for the relevant decision:
Facts or records I already have:
Specific request:
Cost, terms or eligibility to confirm:
Reply / outstanding question / follow-up date:
What will count as progress:
```

You are done when you can explain why this person or organisation fits the obstacle and what result you are asking for. Count useful decisions, resolved questions and completed work—not just contacts collected.
