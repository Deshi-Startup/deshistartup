## More ready-to-use resources by task

The quick choices above are only a starting selection. These completed resources live in other subject sections, so they are listed here as well. Choose by the document or decision you need; not every resource is an interactive app or a downloadable file.

### Research and validation

- [Problem validation checklist](/en/ideas/problem-validation-checklist): check what you know about the problem and what remains uncertain.
- [Problem ranking scorecard](/en/ideas/problem-ranking-scorecard): compare possible problems with reasons and evidence.
- [MVP experiment planner](/en/validation/mvp-experiment-planner): define the assumption, method, limits and review rule.
- [Field notes template](/en/validation/field-notes-template): record observations from shops, markets and neighbourhoods.

### Registration and recurring compliance

- [Annual compliance checklist](/en/tax/annual-compliance): organise recurring obligations and evidence of completion.
- [RJSC annual returns](/en/registration/annual-returns): prepare the company filing workflow.
- [Tax calendar](/en/tax/tax-calendar): plan applicable tax tasks and verify dates.
- [Startup Bangladesh due-diligence preparation](/en/funding/startup-bangladesh-dd): organise evidence for that investment process.

These are preparation resources. Check which obligations and current requirements apply to your own entity and reporting period before using a checklist as a filing plan.

### Sales and customer support

- [Landing-page template](/en/customers/landing-page-template): draft a concrete offer, supporting evidence and a next action.
- [Support scripts](/en/customers/support-scripts): adapt replies for recurring customer questions and problems.

### Finance and investor communication

- [Unit economics](/en/metrics/unit-economics): work through what one sale contributes after specified costs.
- [Founder dashboard](/en/metrics/founder-dashboard): choose a small regular review of the business.
- [Investor CRM](/en/funding/investor-crm): track investor fit, conversations and next steps.
- [Pitch deck](/en/funding/pitch-deck): organise the argument and evidence for an investment conversation.
- [Investor updates](/en/funding/investor-updates): report progress, problems and requests consistently.

### Suppliers and production

- [Supplier sourcing](/en/manufacturing/supplier-sourcing): prepare comparable requests and investigate candidates.
- [Quality control](/en/manufacturing/quality-control): define acceptance checks before committing to production.

You do not need all these documents at once. For example, a founder preparing interviews needs the script and notes; an investor CRM does not make that research better. Start with the output that moves today's task forward.

## Use the output in your own context

Replace example values and placeholders. Mark estimates as estimates. For a financial exercise, check which costs and timing assumptions it includes before using the result in a decision. A teaching scenario is not your business forecast.

The demand planner works in your browser. Download the result before leaving; it does not save a copy for you. Scripts and worksheets are starting material to adapt, not messages automatically sent to anyone.

For the explanation behind an output, read the guide around it. For more subjects, use [Browse by Topic](/en/guides).
