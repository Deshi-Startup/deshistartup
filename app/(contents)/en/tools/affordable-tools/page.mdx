# Best Affordable Tools for Bangladeshi Startups

> **In short:** Start with free plans. Pick one tool for each job you actually do. A new startup usually needs a work email, shared files, a task board, a password manager and a simple way to track customers. Add design, payment, accounting or product tools when real work calls for them. The tables below show the best default, the cheapest useful choice and the better fit for special cases.

This guide is for a founder who knows what needs to get done, but does not want to spend a week comparing software. It covers tools for a solo founder, a small team and a growing startup. It also points out when a popular global tool is a poor fit for Bangladesh.

There are no affiliate links or paid placements here.

## Start with the smallest useful stack

You do not need every tool on this page. Start with the row that looks most like your business.

| Your stage | Start with | Add later |
|---|---|---|
| Testing an idea alone | Free email and files, Trello, Canva, Bitwarden, one AI assistant and a one-page site | Paid email, a customer tracker, bookkeeping and team plans |
| Selling in Bangladesh | Custom-domain email, a local payment route, a cashbook or accounting app, and a simple customer list | Email marketing, support inbox and deeper analytics |
| Building a software product | GitHub, a hosting platform, product analytics, error tracking and a support inbox | Paid infrastructure, advanced monitoring and team controls |
| Growing past 5 people | Company-owned accounts, a team password manager, one task system and automatic computer backup | Specialist tools only when a clear owner will use them |

The rule is simple: **pay when a free limit blocks real work, not because growth might happen one day.**

## How to choose without getting lost

Use the same five checks for any tool:

1. **Name the job.** “Send a weekly email” is clearer than “do marketing.”
2. **Try it with real work.** Use the free plan or trial for one week.
3. **Check the full cost.** Look at price per user, annual billing, tax and usage charges.
4. **Check the exit.** Make sure you can export your contacts, files or reports.
5. **Give it an owner.** Record who controls the account, billing email and recovery method.

Prices below were checked on **23 August 2026**. Unless another currency is shown, prices are in US dollars. A low monthly price often requires annual payment. Tax, exchange rates, regional offers and Bangladeshi card acceptance can change the final amount, so check the checkout page before paying.

## 1. AI tools

AI is useful for a first draft, research plan, summary, spreadsheet formula or code suggestion. It is not a final source of truth. Check important facts yourself, especially anything about law, tax, payments, health or finance.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT** | **Best default.** You want one general assistant for writing, ideas, files, data and basic research | Free; paid individual and business plans | It can sound confident when it is wrong |
| **Claude** | **Best for long documents.** You often review reports, policies, interviews or detailed drafts | Free; paid plans vary by region | Fewer reasons to pay if your main need is short everyday work |
| **Perplexity** | **Best for web research.** You want answers with links that you can open and check | Free; Pro is $17/month when billed yearly | A citation can be real while the answer still reads it incorrectly |
| **Gemini** | **Best for Google users.** Your work already lives in Gmail, Docs and Drive | Free; paid through Google AI and Workspace plans | The value depends on how much you use Google’s other tools |
| **GitHub Copilot** | **Best for developers.** You want code suggestions inside your editor | Free; Pro is $10/month | Suggested code still needs review, tests and security checks |

All five have a useful free or entry plan, but their limits and paid features differ.[^ai-tools]

**A safe starting rule:** never paste passwords, private keys, NID scans, bank statements, customer lists, unreleased financials or confidential contracts into a personal AI account. For sensitive company work, review the provider’s data controls and use a company-managed plan.

## 2. Email, files and everyday work

Choose one work suite. Paying for Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 for every person usually creates duplicate files, duplicate calendars and extra bills.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Workspace** | **Best default.** Your team wants simple email, Drive, Docs, Meet and Calendar | Business Starter: $7/user/month, billed yearly | Advanced storage, recording, booking and e-signing need higher plans |
| **Microsoft 365** | **Best for Office-heavy teams.** Excel, Word and PowerPoint files are central to the work | Business Basic: $6/user/month, billed yearly | Basic includes web and mobile Office apps, not the full desktop apps |
| **Zoho Workplace** | **Cheapest paid suite.** You need a custom email and office tools at a low price | Standard: $3/user/month, billed yearly | Clients and new hires may be less familiar with its apps |

Those prices and plan features come from the providers’ current business pricing pages.[^work-suites]

At the start, use the chat and meeting tools already inside your suite. Add Slack or another paid chat app only when your current setup is causing missed work.

## 3. Tasks, documents and team knowledge

One system is enough. If work is split across Trello, Notion, WhatsApp and a spreadsheet, nobody knows which one is current.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trello** | **Easiest start.** You want a clear board with “To do,” “Doing” and “Done” | Free for up to 10 collaborators; Standard is $5/user/month with yearly billing | It gets messy when projects have many links and dependencies |
| **Notion** | **Best all-round workspace.** You want notes, team pages and light project tracking together | Free; Plus is $10/member/month with yearly billing | It is easy to spend more time arranging pages than doing work |
| **Jira** | **Best for a software team.** Engineers need issue tracking, releases and a detailed backlog | Free for up to 10 users; Standard is $7.91/user/month | Too heavy for a small non-technical team |

The free limits and entry prices are based on the current Trello, Notion and Jira plans.[^project-tools]

For a team of two or three, start with Trello. Move to Notion when shared knowledge becomes the bigger problem. Choose Jira only when the engineering workflow needs it.

## 4. Websites, online shops and hosting

First decide what the site must do. A one-page launch site, a large content site, an online shop and a web app are different jobs.

### Simple websites

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carrd** | **Cheapest one-page site.** You need a clean landing page, waitlist or company profile | Pro Standard: $19/year | It is not made for a large blog, shop or web app |
| **WordPress.com** | **Best for lots of content.** You plan to publish guides, news or search-friendly pages | Free; Personal starts at $4/month yearly | Plugins and deeper control can raise the cost and upkeep |
| **Framer** | **Best polished marketing site.** Design quality matters and you do not want to code | Free to try; Basic is $10/month yearly | Extra editors and higher traffic can make it expensive |
| **Shopify** | **Easiest hosted online shop.** You want products, orders and store operations in one place | Paid plans; check current local checkout | Confirm that your chosen Bangladeshi payment route works before building the store |

Carrd, WordPress.com and Framer include hosting in their plans. Shopify also runs the store hosting, so do not buy a second hosting plan for any of them.[^site-builders]

For a Bangladesh-focused shop, also compare WordPress with WooCommerce. It takes more setup, but it may give you more choice when connecting local services. Check the actual plugin and gateway before choosing the platform.

### Hosting for a site your developers build

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare Pages** | **Best cheap static hosting.** Your developer builds a fast site from code | Free plan | Dynamic features may need Workers or another backend |
| **Vercel** | **Best for many Next.js teams.** You want an easy deploy and preview workflow | Hobby is free for personal, non-commercial work; Pro is $20/month with usage credit | Usage charges can grow as traffic and features grow |

Both providers publish free entry plans; Vercel’s paid plan adds team and commercial features.[^developer-hosting]

Do not rent a virtual server just because it looks cheap. A server also needs updates, security, monitoring and backups. Use one only when someone on the team can own those jobs.

## 5. Building an app or an MVP

An <Term name="mvp">MVP</Term> is the smallest version of a product you can use to test the main idea. It is often a manual test, not software. Take orders through a form, run the service by hand and learn what customers need before building a full app.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bubble** | **Best no-code web app.** A non-developer needs to build and test a working product | Free for development; Starter is $59/month with yearly billing for a live app | Usage affects the bill, and moving the app elsewhere later can be hard |
| **Lovable** | **Best AI-first builder for a web MVP.** You want to describe a product in plain language and get a working first version quickly | Free: 5 daily build credits, capped at 30/month; Pro starts at $25/month | Credit use changes with task complexity. Before handling real customer data or payments, have a developer review the code and security |
| **Replit** | **Best AI builder when you want access to the code.** You want Agent, an editable code workspace, a database and deployment in one browser tab | Starter is free; Core is $20/month with yearly billing or $25 month to month | The free app goes offline after 30 days, though you can republish it. Agent, hosting, databases and storage share usage credits |
| **Supabase** | **Best starter backend for developers.** You need a database, login, storage and APIs | Free; Pro starts at $25/month | It still needs engineering and cost monitoring |
| **GitHub + Cloudflare or Vercel** | **Best for a coding team.** You want code review, version history and automatic deploys | Check commercial eligibility: Vercel Hobby is personal/non-commercial; budget for a suitable paid plan or another eligible host | Someone must own security, testing and production support |

Bubble and Supabase publish clear free tiers and paid starting points.[^product-building]

Lovable and Replit also have free entry plans. Their prices and limits were checked on 24 August 2026.[^product-building]

Before choosing no-code, ask one developer to review the hard parts: payments, data export, permissions, integrations and expected usage. Rebuilding later costs far more than a one-hour review now.

## 6. Graphics and video

Use the lightest tool that can make the final asset. A social post does not need the same software as a product interface or a long documentary.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Canva** | **Best default for founders.** You make social posts, pitch slides, flyers and simple brand assets | Free; Pro is $144/year for one person | Popular templates can make every brand look alike; set a few brand rules |
| **Figma** | **Best for app and website screen design.** Designers and developers need to work on screens together | Free Starter; Professional full seat is $16/month | It is more tool than you need for everyday social graphics |
| **CapCut** | **Easiest video editor.** You make short social videos on a phone or laptop | Useful free tools; paid price varies by region and device | Some effects, exports and assets are locked behind paid plans |
| **DaVinci Resolve** | **Best free editor for serious work.** You edit long or high-quality video on a capable computer | Free; Studio is a $295 one-time purchase | It takes time to learn and needs a stronger computer |

These prices and free-plan details come from the current Canva, Figma, CapCut and Blackmagic Design pages.[^design-video]

Start with Canva and CapCut if the founder is making content. Use Figma for product design. Learn DaVinci Resolve when video quality and control matter enough to justify the time.

## 7. Sales, outreach, marketing and SEO

You do not need a large marketing stack to get your first customers. Keep one clean customer list, send useful messages and measure which channel brings replies or sales.

A **CRM** keeps leads, customer conversations and next steps in one place. **SEO** means helping people find and understand your website through search.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HubSpot CRM** | **Best free sales CRM.** Two people need contacts, deals, notes and follow-ups in one place | Free for up to 2 users and 1,000 contacts | Paid bundles can become expensive; check the upgrade path early |
| **Brevo** | **Best cheap email marketing.** You need newsletters, simple follow-ups or automatic emails such as receipts | Free for 300 sends/day; Starter starts at $9/month | The free plan keeps Brevo branding and has a daily limit |
| **Buffer** | **Best simple social scheduler.** One person posts to a few social accounts | Free for 3 channels and 10 queued posts per channel; paid from $5/channel/month with yearly billing | Paid cost rises with each connected account |
| **Google Search Console** | **Essential free SEO tool.** You want to see how Google finds and shows your site | Free | It reports Google Search performance; it does not plan your whole content strategy |
| **Ahrefs Free** | **Best free website SEO check.** You want audits, traffic data and link information for a site you own | Free for verified sites | Full competitor research needs an expensive paid plan |

Current limits and entry prices are on the providers’ own pages.[^marketing-tools]

For a brand-new startup, Search Console is enough to begin SEO. Publish pages that answer real customer questions. Buy an advanced SEO plan only when someone has time to use its reports and act on them.

Do not buy a contact list. It creates poor leads, spam complaints and a damaged sending reputation. Ten well-chosen personal messages are more useful than a thousand random emails.

## 8. Analytics, booking and customer support

Start by answering three questions: Where did people come from? What did they do? Where did they get stuck?

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Analytics** | **Most familiar website analytics.** Your marketer already knows it or you use Google Ads | Free standard product | Setup and consent choices need care; the reports can feel complex |
| **Microsoft Clarity** | **Best free view of user behavior.** You want heatmaps and session recordings to spot broken pages | Free with unlimited traffic, sites and team members | Recordings show what happened, not why the person did it |
| **PostHog** | **Best for a software product.** Developers need to track sign-ups and feature use, replay sessions and test changes | Free up to 1 million analytics events and 5,000 recordings a month | Usage-based pricing and setup need a technical owner |
| **Cal.com** | **Best free booking link.** A founder schedules interviews, demos or calls | Free for one person; Teams is $12/user/month with yearly billing | Sending bookings to the right team member needs the paid plan |
| **Crisp** | **Best free support inbox.** Two people need website chat and one place for customer messages | Free for 2 seats | Automation, a shared email inbox and more channels need paid plans |

The current free limits are published by Clarity, PostHog, Cal.com and Crisp.[^analytics-support]

Do not install every analytics tool at once. For a normal website, start with one traffic tool and Clarity. For a web app, PostHog may replace several separate tools.

## 9. Hiring employees and contractors

The best platform depends on the role. A local sales hire, a senior product leader and a two-week design project do not belong in the same search.

| Platform | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bdjobs** | **Best local reach.** You are hiring common full-time roles in Bangladesh | SME listing: BDT 500 + VAT; Standard: BDT 2,850 + VAT | The low-cost SME post has limits on company size, days and applications |
| **LinkedIn Jobs** | **Best for experienced or visible roles.** You need professional profiles and your network can share the post | Free posts may be available; promoted cost follows your budget | Popular roles attract noise, and promotion can get costly |
| **Wellfound** | **Best for startup and remote talent.** You want candidates already interested in startups | Unlimited job posts are free; sourcing and ads are paid | Its Bangladesh candidate pool may be smaller than local platforms |
| **Upwork** | **Best for a defined contract project.** You need a freelancer for design, research, development or operations | No monthly fee on Basic; client fee up to 7.99% of payments | A vague brief creates poor work and expensive revisions |

For a contractor, write the output, deadline, review points and ownership of the work before hiring. For an employee, use a proper interview and reference check. A platform profile is a lead, not proof that someone is right for the job.

## 10. Documents and electronic signatures

Draft the agreement in Google Docs or Word. Use an e-signing tool when you need a clear signing order, reminders and a record of who signed.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **PandaDoc** | **Best free basic e-signing.** A small team sends only a few simple agreements | Free for 60 documents/year; Starter is $19/seat/month with yearly billing | Free documents allow only 2 recipients and limited templates |
| **Google Workspace eSignature** | **Best if you already pay for it.** Your team works in Docs and Drive | Included from Business Standard at $14/user/month with yearly billing | Do not upgrade the whole team for this feature alone |
| **DocuSign** | **Best for a mature signing process.** Clients expect it or you need more control over agreements | Paid plans | It is usually unnecessary for a startup sending a few simple documents |

An e-signature tool proves a signing process; it does not make a weak contract good. Use a lawyer for founder agreements, investment papers, major employment terms, intellectual property transfers and large customer contracts.

## 11. Payments in Bangladesh and abroad

Choose the payment route before choosing the store or billing software. A beautiful checkout is useless if your customers cannot pay or your company cannot receive the money.

For local online payments, compare providers in the [Bangladesh payment gateway comparison](/en/payments/gateway-comparison) and the [payment gateway directory](/en/directory/payment-gateways). A <Term name="payment-gateway">payment gateway</Term> can combine cards and mobile financial services in one checkout.

For global software sales, a **merchant of record** is another option. This company runs the checkout as the seller and handles sales tax, then pays your share to you.

| Route | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SSLCOMMERZ** | **A common local starting point.** You need one checkout for cards and mobile payments | Published setup fee: BDT 25,500; 2.5% per successful transaction; 3.5% for AMEX | Compare onboarding, settlement, refunds, plugins and total fees with other local providers |
| **Another local gateway** | **Best when the fit is better.** aamarPay, shurjoPay or another provider has the right fee, support or integration | Setup and transaction fees vary | Get a written quote and test a real payment, refund and settlement before launch |
| **Lemon Squeezy or Paddle** | **Best to investigate for global software sales.** You want another company to run checkout and handle sales tax | No simple flat subscription; each sale carries a fee | Approval is not automatic, and these services are mainly for software or digital products |
| **Bank transfer and invoice** | **Best for early B2B or service exports.** Customers can pay against an invoice | Bank and foreign-exchange charges vary | Use the correct business account and keep the export and remittance documents |

SSLCOMMERZ publishes its setup and transaction price. Lemon Squeezy lists Bangladesh for bank payouts, and Paddle says it can work with software businesses outside its excluded countries.[^payment-routes]

Bangladesh does **not** appear on Stripe’s current list of supported countries for payments.[^stripe-availability] Do not build your whole payment plan around opening a direct Stripe account later.

For export income, read [bringing export proceeds into Bangladesh](/en/trade/export-proceeds). Payment, company structure, tax and foreign-exchange rules need to agree with each other.

## 12. Bookkeeping and accounting

Ask your accountant what they can review before choosing software. The cheapest app is not cheap if your accountant has to rebuild every report in another system.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Google Sheets or Excel** | **Cheapest for a tiny operation.** You have few monthly transactions and one careful owner | Free or included in your existing suite | Manual entry breaks down as sales, inventory, tax and staff grow |
| **HishabPati** | **Best low-cost local start.** You want Bangla-friendly mobile records, invoices and inventory | Free Basic; Premium BDT 119/month or BDT 1,190/year | Check that its reports and exports match your accountant’s needs |
| **Zoho Books** | **Best cloud workflow.** You want online invoices, expenses, customer records and integrations | Pricing and available plans vary by country | It is not a Bangladesh tax adviser; local setup still needs review |
| **TallyPrime** | **Best when the accountant already uses Tally.** Inventory and formal accounts matter | International 12-month plan shown at $342 | It is more complex than a simple cashbook and may need setup help |

HishabPati publishes its local free and paid plans. Zoho Books uses country-specific pricing, while Tally lists international plans separately.[^accounting-tools]

Software does not replace bookkeeping rules or a qualified accountant. Use [bookkeeping basics](/en/tax/bookkeeping-basics) to understand the records, then follow the [accounts and bookkeeping setup guide](/en/tax/bookkeeping-setup) before transactions pile up.

## 13. Passwords, security and backup

This is not an “add later” category. Turn on two-step login and stop sharing passwords in chat from the first week.

| Tool | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bitwarden** | **Best low-cost password manager.** A founder or team needs strong, shared logins | Free personal plan; Teams is $4/user/month yearly | Someone must still remove access when a person leaves |
| **1Password** | **Best polished small-team setup.** Up to 10 people want simple shared vaults and admin controls | Teams Starter: $24.95/month for up to 10, billed yearly | Costs more than Bitwarden for a very small team |
| **Backblaze** | **Best simple computer backup.** Important work lives on Mac or Windows laptops | $99/computer/year | Cloud file sync is still separate; test that you can restore a file |

The current team and backup prices are published by Bitwarden, 1Password and Backblaze.[^security-tools]

Use company email addresses for company accounts. Keep recovery codes somewhere safe. When anyone leaves, close their access the same day and change any shared credentials they knew.

## 14. Incorporating a company abroad

Do this only when there is a real reason, such as an investor requirement, a global software business or a customer contract that needs a foreign entity. If the business earns local taka and operates only in Bangladesh, a foreign company usually adds cost and compliance without fixing a real problem.

Read [foreign investment and the Singapore flip](/en/funding/fdi-rules) before paying an incorporation service. Bangladesh foreign-exchange rules still apply.

| Service | Pick this when | Cost to start | Main catch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stripe Atlas** | **Best-known US option.** A venture-backed or global startup needs a Delaware C-Corp | $500 including state fees and first-year registered agent; then $100/year for the agent | Tax, annual filings, banking, accounting and legal work continue after setup |
| **Sleek Singapore** | **Strong Singapore option.** A foreign founder wants remote incorporation and ongoing company services | Foreign-founder package starts around S$2,250 | A local director, address, secretary, tax and annual compliance can make the true cost much higher than the government fee |
| **A startup lawyer and accountant** | **Best for a funded or complex structure.** Investors, intellectual property or money will move between companies | Professional quote | More expensive now, but cheaper than repairing the wrong ownership or tax structure later |

Stripe Atlas and Sleek publish what their starting packages include and exclude.[^incorporation-services]

An incorporation service files forms. It does not decide whether the structure is legal, tax-efficient or fundable for your exact case.

## A quick buying checklist

Before adding any paid tool, check these boxes:

- [ ] We can name the job this tool will do this month.
- [ ] One person owns setup, billing and support.
- [ ] We tested the free plan or trial with real work.
- [ ] We know the full monthly or yearly cost for the whole team.
- [ ] We checked whether a Bangladeshi card and billing address work.
- [ ] We know how to export our data.
- [ ] We turned on two-step login and saved recovery codes.
- [ ] We recorded the renewal date and cancellation steps.
- [ ] We removed another tool if this one replaces it.

**You are done** when every paid tool has a real job, an owner and a renewal date, and your team knows which system is the source of truth for files, tasks, customers and money.
