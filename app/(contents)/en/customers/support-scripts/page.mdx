# Customer Support Scripts: Fast, Clear Answers for Real Operational Crises

> **In short:** Say what you know, what you will do and when you will update the customer. Give each case an owner. Promise only what that person can arrange. A fast apology is useless if the parcel gets returned anyway; solve the underlying operational friction.

## Copy the reply that fits

Use these when your startup handles support by WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, phone or email. These scripts address the most frequent operational crises early-stage Bangladeshi businesses face: courier breakdowns, Cash on Delivery (COD) disputes, MFS payment mismatches and system outages.

Replace every bracket with verified facts. Never guess a delivery date or blame a third party without checking the system first.

### 1. Courier delay or a disputed "unreachable" status

The tracking page says the customer could not be reached, but they say nobody called. Check the order details and ask the courier to investigate. The status alone does not tell you why delivery failed.

```text
Thanks for telling us, [Customer Name]. I am sorry your order has not arrived.

The tracking page for [Order ID / Consignment ID] says [exact status]. You have
told us [customer's account of what happened]. I have asked [Courier Name] to
check this and to pause the return while we confirm the next step.

The courier has [confirmed the hold / not yet confirmed the hold].
I will update you by [day and time], even if we are still waiting for a reply.

Once the courier confirms a delivery window, I will check whether it works for you.
```

### 2. Damaged or wrong item at the doorstep (COD rider waiting)

First check the merchant's return policy and the courier's inspection rules. For example, Pathao's policy has a separate rule for closed-box deliveries: checking the item needs the merchant's permission through the delivery agent.[^pathao-product-check] Do not tell every customer to open a parcel, refuse payment or hand it back without confirming the process for that order.

```text
I am sorry there is a problem with order [Order ID], [Customer Name].

Please tell us what is wrong. If the parcel is already open, a photo of the item
and packaging will help. Please keep your address and phone number out of the photo.

I am checking the return process with [Courier Name] now. I will confirm whether
the rider can take it back and what you need to do about payment.
I will update you by [time].

[Only after the return or replacement is approved:]
We have approved [return / replacement] under [policy that applies to this order].
The next step is [confirmed action]. Your delivery charge will be [confirmed amount].
We will send [tracking details / a progress update] by [day and time].
```

### 3. bKash / Nagad TrxID mismatch or "Send Money" instead of Payment

The payment did not match the order. Ask for the transaction reference exactly as shown in the app or SMS, without assuming a fixed length. Check your own payment records before marking the order as paid; a screenshot alone is not confirmation.

```text
We have not yet matched your payment to order [Order ID].

Please share these details so we can check:
1. Payment service and type: [e.g., bKash Payment or Nagad Send Money]
2. Last 4 digits of the sender's number: [e.g., 1234]
3. Receiver name or number shown in the transaction:
4. Amount, date and time of payment:
5. Transaction reference (TrxID), exactly as shown in your app or SMS:

Never share your PIN, one-time password (OTP), or account password.

We will check our payment records and update you by [day and time].
If we find the payment, we will confirm it against your order.
```

Ask only for details needed to trace the payment, through your private support channel. bKash warns customers never to share their PIN or security codes.[^bkash-security]

### 4. Refund status and expected timing

Keep three steps separate: your team approves the refund, the provider receives the request, and the money is returned. For example, SSLCOMMERZ records a refund request separately from its completed status.[^sslcommerz-refunds] Use the provider's current estimate for the specific payment. If you do not have one, say so.

```text
Here is the current refund status for order [Order ID]:

- Amount: BDT [Amount]
- Status: [Approved by our team / Request submitted to provider / Provider reports refunded]
- Refund method: [Confirmed method]
- Provider reference: [Reference, if the provider has issued one]
- Expected timing: [Provider's current estimate for this payment, checked on date / Not yet confirmed]

My next update will be by [day and time]. If the expected date passes without the
money arriving, I will follow up with [provider] using the refund reference.
```

Do not say the money has reached the customer just because your team approved the request. For more on checking payment records, see the [payment reconciliation guide](/en/payments/reconciliation).

### 5. Order cancellation request (before or after courier handover)

Check whether the warehouse can stop dispatch. A cancellation request does not prove that a parcel has stopped moving. Confirm any charge or refund against the order and the policy that applies.

```text
[Before courier handover, once the warehouse confirms dispatch has stopped:]
Your order [Order ID] is cancelled, and the warehouse has confirmed it will not
be sent. Your payment status is [no payment received / amount paid].
[If a refund applies, include its confirmed amount, method and current status
using Script 4.]

[After courier handover:]
[Courier Name] has already collected parcel [Consignment ID]. We have requested
a return, but the courier has not yet confirmed it. I will update you by [day
and time] with the confirmed next step and any charge or refund that applies.
```

### 6. B2B / SaaS service outage or sync failure

A retail POS, inventory system or B2B API is failing during active business hours. The customer is losing sales. Share only a workaround your team has checked for this incident.

```text
We are investigating a problem with [specific feature] on [Product Name].

What we know: [Confirmed facts, or say the cause is still being checked].
Current impact: [What works and what is paused].
Safe workaround: [Tested steps and their limits / No safe workaround confirmed yet].

Our next update will be posted here by [day and time]. We will also explain
how to check any missing or duplicate records once the service is restored.
```

### 7. You need time to investigate an operational issue

```text
Thanks for contacting us, [Customer Name]. I understand that [specific problem].
Your case reference is [Case ID], and I am personally handling this inquiry.

I am checking our [warehouse dispatch log / courier manifest / bank statement]
to verify what happened.

I will update you by [exact day and time, e.g., today at 4:00 PM], even if the
carrier or warehouse has not fully closed their investigation yet.
```

## Give every case one owner and a case log

GoZayaan's Marc Thiry describes support as part of the whole service, with human help where the product cannot resolve a problem.[^gozayaan-support] A script only works when the team can carry out the next step.

Keep a shared case log in a simple sheet:

```text
Case ID & Order/Consignment ID:
Customer phone & communication channel (WhatsApp / Call / FB):
Reported issue in customer's exact words:
Courier partner & Consignment Number (if applicable):
Investigation findings:
Urgency level (High: stranded COD parcel / Lost payment / System outage):
Owner (one specific teammate):
Promise given to customer (action & deadline):
Current state: Open / Escalated to Hub / Awaiting Bank / Resolved
Final resolution & date:
Confirmed cause (or "Not yet known") and supporting record:
```

Keep personal data restricted to team members directly handling fulfillment and support. Never ask for or store passwords, PINs or bank OTPs.

## Separate the reply clock from the fix clock

You control when you send a message, but you cannot control how long a courier takes to locate a lost flyer in their Tejgaon sorting hub.

Give an update time you can guarantee: "I will update you by 3:30 PM today." When 3:30 PM arrives, message the customer even if the courier has not replied:
> "We followed up with the Tejgaon hub manager at 3:15 PM; they are still locating the bag. My next check-in with you is at 5:00 PM."

Regular updates help customers know what is happening and when they will hear from you again.

## Worked example: an Agrabad COD delivery breakdown

This is a fictional example. An online footwear brand in Mirpur, Dhaka receives an order for shoes worth BDT 2,400 from a customer in Agrabad, Chattogram. The customer wants to pay on delivery.

On Thursday afternoon, the customer sends a WhatsApp message:
> "I took time off to receive the shoes. Nobody called me, but your tracking says my phone was switched off. Please cancel the order."

### Step 1: Check the records without guessing the cause

The support lead checks the order number, saved phone number and courier tracking. The tracking says "Customer Unreachable", which differs from the customer's account. A caller-ID app cannot prove whether the rider called. Any call log the team is allowed to access may also be incomplete.

The lead asks the courier hub to investigate and stop the return if possible. They record this as a request, not a confirmed hold.

### Step 2: Give a clear next update

The support lead replies:
> "I am sorry your delivery did not arrive. I can see the status you mentioned, but I have not confirmed why it was added. I have asked the Agrabad hub to check. I will update you by 5:00 PM today about your cancellation request, even if the hub has not replied."

At 5:00 PM, the lead sends the update. If the customer is still open to delivery, the lead first gets an available slot from the hub, then checks whether the customer wants it. They do not quietly replace a cancellation request with another delivery attempt.

### Step 3: Confirm the outcome before closing the case

In this example, the customer agrees to the hub's next available delivery slot. After the parcel arrives and payment is confirmed, the lead records the outcome. The cause of the first failed attempt stays "Not yet known" unless the courier provides evidence.

If the customer still wants to cancel, the lead follows Script 5 instead. The useful habit is the same: keep the customer informed and record only what has been confirmed.

## Review repeated problems each week

Every Sunday morning, review the support case log:
1. **Courier failure rate:** What percentage of delayed orders came from one specific courier or hub?
2. **Payment mismatch rate:** Are customers struggling to find the TrxID on the checkout screen? If so, add an annotated visual diagram to your payment page.
3. **Mismatched items:** Did the warehouse pack the wrong size because SKU barcodes are missing?

Fix the root operational defect so you do not have to write the same support script fifty times a week.

## Before using the scripts

- [ ] All team members know never to ask for bKash/Nagad PINs or bank OTPs.
- [ ] The case owner can check the needed courier and payment records through authorized access.
- [ ] Emergency escalation contacts for key courier hub supervisors are saved.
- [ ] Every open case has a single named owner and a promised follow-up timestamp.
- [ ] Refund status and any timing estimate have been checked for the specific payment.

Start by customizing the courier delay and bKash TrxID scripts. Use the [founder role matrix](/en/cofounders/role-matrix) to establish who can authorize goodwill replacements or immediate courier recalls.
