# Customer Support Scripts: Fast, Clear Answers for Real Operational Crises

> **In short:** Say what you know, what you will do and when you will update the customer. Give each case an owner. Promise only what that person can arrange. A fast apology is useless if the parcel gets returned anyway; solve the underlying operational friction.

## Copy the reply that fits

Use these when your startup handles support by WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, phone or email. These scripts address the most frequent operational crises early-stage Bangladeshi businesses face: courier breakdowns, Cash on Delivery (COD) disputes, MFS payment mismatches and system outages.

Replace every bracket with verified facts. Never guess a delivery date or blame a third party without checking the system first.

### 1. Courier delay or rider marked "unreachable" falsely

In Bangladesh, delivery riders facing tight routes often mark orders as "Customer phone switched off" or "Address not found" to close their daily quota. The customer is usually waiting at home with cash, furious.

```text
Thanks for alerting us, [Customer Name]. I am very sorry about this frustration.

I see that order [Order ID / Consignment ID] was marked as unreachable by
the courier hub today. I have flagged this directly with the [Courier Name]
local hub supervisor to put an immediate hold on any return (RTO).

Your delivery has been rescheduled for priority delivery tomorrow between
[Time window, e.g., 10 AM and 2 PM]. Our operations team will call you
tomorrow morning at [Phone number] before the rider departs.

If you do not receive a call by [Time], please message me directly here.
```

### 2. Damaged or wrong item at the doorstep (COD rider waiting)

The customer opened the flyer in front of the rider; the size or color is wrong, or the product is damaged. The rider is demanding full COD cash before leaving.

```text
Please do not worry, [Customer Name]. You do not need to pay the rider if the
parcel is incorrect or visibly damaged.

Please ask the delivery rider to record the parcel as "Damaged / Mismatched on
delivery" on their device and hand the parcel back to them. If practical, please
take one photo of the item inside the open flyer and share it with us here.

We will dispatch your correct replacement order [New Reference] today at zero
additional delivery charge. Your new tracking ID will be sent by [Time].
```

### 3. bKash / Nagad TrxID mismatch or "Send Money" instead of Payment

The customer transferred money via personal bKash/Nagad ("Send Money" or cash-in at an agent) instead of using your checkout gateway, or typed the wrong 10-character Transaction ID (TrxID).

```text
We have not been able to automatically match your payment to order [Order ID].

To reconcile this manually, please share the following details:
1. Sender bKash/Nagad wallet number (last 4 digits): [e.g., 017XXXX1234]
2. Receiver wallet number you sent money to:
3. Exact amount sent: [BDT Amount]
4. The 10-character Transaction ID (TrxID) from your SMS:

IMPORTANT: Never share your bKash/Nagad PIN, one-time password (OTP), or account password.

We will check our merchant statement and confirm your order within [30 minutes / Time].
```

### 4. Refund timeline: MFS payout vs Payment Gateway / Card

Customers expect instant bKash refunds. When payment was made through an online gateway (such as SSLCOMMERZ, Shurjopay or a credit card), settlement reversal takes multiple banking days.

```text
Your refund of BDT [Amount] for order [Order ID] has been approved.

Because your original payment was made via [Card / Payment Gateway / bKash Direct]:
- Method: [Original payment method]
- Reference ID: [Gateway Refund Reference]
- Expected turnaround: [For MFS: within 4 to 24 hours / For Debit or Credit cards:
  5 to 10 working days, depending on your issuing bank's settlement cycle].

We have already submitted the reversal instruction from our end. If the balance
does not reflect in your account by [Date], please send us a message with this
reference ID, and we will follow up with the gateway directly.
```

### 5. Order cancellation request (Before vs After Consignment Generation)

A customer changes their mind or ordered by mistake. Handling this before the courier picks up prevents an unnecessary return delivery fee (RTO charge).

```text
[If before courier dispatch:]
Your cancellation request for order [Order ID] is confirmed. No parcel will be
dispatched, and you have not been charged. If you placed an advance payment,
your refund of BDT [Amount] will be disbursed via [bKash/Nagad] by [Time today].

[If after courier handover:]
Your parcel [Consignment ID] has already been collected by [Courier Name] and is
in transit. Because it is already on the road, please inform the rider when they
call that you requested cancellation. We will recall the parcel directly from the hub.
```

### 6. B2B / SaaS service outage or sync failure

A retail POS, inventory system or B2B API is failing during active business hours. The customer is losing sales.

```text
We are actively investigating an issue affecting [specific feature, e.g., POS sync /
invoice generation] on [Product Name].

What we know: Our engineering team identified [short non-technical cause, e.g., a database
connection delay] at [Time].
Current impact: [What works vs what is paused].
Safe workaround: [e.g., If your app supports local offline mode, sales cached on your device will auto-sync once connection restores. If you issue manual paper receipts, our support team will help you bulk-reconcile and log them once the system is live].

Our next status update will be posted here by [Time, within 30–45 minutes].
We understand your shop operations depend on this and are treating it as priority one.
```

### 7. You need time to investigate an operational issue

```text
Thanks for contacting us, [Customer Name]. I understand that [specific problem].
Your case reference is [Case ID], and I am personally handling this inquiry.

I am checking our [warehouse dispatch log / courier manifest / bank statement]
to verify what happened.

I will update you by [exact day and time, e.g., today at 4:00 PM], even if the
carrier or warehouse has not fully closed their investigation yet.
```

## Give every case one owner and a case log

GoZayaan's Marc Thiry describes support as part of the whole service, with human help where the product cannot resolve a problem.[^gozayaan-support] A script only works when the team can carry out the next step.

Keep a shared case log in a simple sheet:

```text
Case ID & Order/Consignment ID:
Customer phone & communication channel (WhatsApp / Call / FB):
Reported issue in customer's exact words:
Courier partner & Consignment Number (if applicable):
Investigation findings:
Urgency level (High: stranded COD parcel / Lost payment / System outage):
Owner (one specific teammate):
Promise given to customer (action & deadline):
Current state: Open / Escalated to Hub / Awaiting Bank / Resolved
Final resolution & date:
Root cause (Wrong address / Rider fake return / MFS sync lag / Packing defect):
```

Keep personal data restricted to team members directly handling fulfillment and support. Never ask for or store passwords, PINs or bank OTPs.

## Separate the reply clock from the fix clock

You control when you send a message, but you cannot control how long a courier takes to locate a lost flyer in their Tejgaon sorting hub.

Give an update time you can guarantee: "I will update you by 3:30 PM today." When 3:30 PM arrives, message the customer even if the courier has not replied:
> "We followed up with the Tejgaon hub manager at 3:15 PM; they are still locating the bag. My next check-in with you is at 5:00 PM."

Predictable communication prevents customers from posting angry scam warnings on Facebook community groups.

## Worked example: an Agrabad COD delivery breakdown

Imagine an online footwear brand based in Mirpur, Dhaka. A customer in Agrabad, Chattogram orders shoes worth BDT 2,400 on Cash on Delivery. The scenario below illustrates an operational crisis resolution.

On Thursday afternoon, the customer sends a furious WhatsApp voice message:
> "I took half-day off work to receive the shoes. Nobody called me, but your tracking says 'Delivery attempted, customer phone switched off'! Cancel my order immediately, you guys are scammers!"

### Step 1: Immediate verification and containment (10 minutes)
The support lead opens the courier merchant dashboard (Steadfast/Pathao). The tracking indeed shows "Customer Unreachable / Phone Off."
The support lead checks the customer's phone number on Truecaller and checks recent outbound logs: no call was made by the rider. The rider ran out of daylight in Agrabad and closed the batch with an RTO status code.

### Step 2: Clear, de-escalating customer response
The support lead sends Script 1 on WhatsApp:
> "I apologize for this frustration, [Customer Name]. I just checked our system. The rider did not call you; the local Agrabad hub made an administrative error when closing their afternoon route. I have personally contacted the Agrabad hub supervisor and put a strict hold on your parcel (Consignment #CN-88219). Your shoes will NOT be returned to Dhaka.
>
> I have scheduled priority delivery for tomorrow morning between 10:30 AM and 1:00 PM. The hub supervisor has assigned a senior rider. I will track this personally and call you at 10:00 AM before the rider sets out."

### Step 3: Operational follow-through
The founder calls the courier relationship manager with the Consignment ID, warning them that repeated fake RTOs from the Agrabad branch will result in shifting shipping volume to a competitor. On Friday at 11:30 AM, the rider calls the customer, the shoes are inspected, and BDT 2,400 is collected.

The case is logged under root cause: "Courier fake RTO / Agrabad hub." The support script converted a potential brand crisis and a lost BDT 140 round-trip delivery fee into a loyal customer.

## Review repeated problems each week

Every Sunday morning, review the support case log:
1. **Courier failure rate:** What percentage of delayed orders came from one specific courier or hub?
2. **Payment mismatch rate:** Are customers struggling to find the TrxID on the checkout screen? If so, add an annotated visual diagram to your payment page.
3. **Mismatched items:** Did the warehouse pack the wrong size because SKU barcodes are missing?

Fix the root operational defect so you do not have to write the same support script fifty times a week.

## Before using the scripts

- [ ] All team members know never to ask for bKash/Nagad PINs or bank OTPs.
- [ ] Support staff have direct access to courier merchant dashboards and consignment IDs.
- [ ] Emergency escalation contacts for key courier hub supervisors are saved.
- [ ] Every open case has a single named owner and a promised follow-up timestamp.
- [ ] Refund turnaround times accurately reflect payment gateway vs MFS realities.

Start by customizing the courier delay and bKash TrxID scripts. Use the [founder role matrix](/en/cofounders/role-matrix) to establish who can authorize goodwill replacements or immediate courier recalls.
