# Landing Page Template: High-Converting Pages for Bangladesh

> **In short:** Give one kind of buyer one clear offer and one main action. Show real business details and clear prices, delivery terms and limits. Test the page on the devices your buyers use. Replace every sample claim with something your team can actually provide.

## Copy the page outline

A **landing page** is a web page dedicated to converting visitors from a specific ad, social post, campaign or referral link into leads or paying customers.

Ask only for the details needed for the next step. Test a short form against a direct WhatsApp conversation to see where your buyers get stuck. Explain why you need a phone number before asking for it.

Replace the brackets below with your own checked details. Use customer logos only with permission. Do not promise delivery, inspection, refunds or security features that you cannot provide.

```text
1. HEADLINE & HOOK
[State the exact job or problem you solve for a specific buyer in plain words.]

2. VALUE PROPOSITION & CURRENT STAGE
For [specific customer], [product/service] helps you [measured outcome] by [how it works].
Status: [In Stock / Available for Immediate Dispatch / Limited Pilot Cohort].

3. PRIMARY CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA)
[Button: "Order via WhatsApp" / "Book 10-Min Phone Demo" / "Buy Cash on Delivery"]
Payment and delivery promise: [State any advance payment, actual COD areas and exclusions.]

4. WHAT YOU GET & HOW IT WORKS
- [Concrete deliverable or software capability]
- [Concrete deliverable or hardware warranty]
- [Clear operational limit or excluded scope]
Three-step process:
Step 1: [What customer does] -> Step 2: [What we do/dispatch] -> Step 3: [Customer outcome]

5. TRANSPARENT PRICING & DELIVERY CHARGES
[Product or Subscription price in BDT, tax inclusive or specified]
Delivery charges: [Current charge by area and order size, checked with your courier.]
Payment terms: [Cash on Delivery / bKash Merchant Payment / Bank Gateway]

6. LOCAL TRUST & VERIFICATION ANCHORS
- Physical Address: [Registered office or workshop address in Bangladesh]
- Registration: [Trade License number / DBID number / RJSC entity name]
- Inspection and returns: [What buyers may check before payment, only if your courier permits it; return terms.]
- Real product video or customer logos used with permission

7. COMMON QUESTIONS & OBJECTIONS (FAQ)
- [Main operational objection, e.g., compatibility or return process]
- [Payment or technical support question]

8. REPEAT PRIMARY CTA & DIRECT CONTACT
[Same clear action button]
Direct WhatsApp hotline: [Working Bangladesh phone number]
```

## Adapt the page for Bangladesh mobile reality

Government content-design guidance starts with the task the reader needs to complete.[^gds-content] Test these three points with your own buyers:

1. **Devices and connection:** Try the page on the phones and connections your buyers use. Compress large images and remove scripts or autoplay media that do not help the task. Watch whether buyers can see the offer and use the main button without waiting or getting stuck.
2. **Questions before buying:** Some buyers may want to check stock, delivery or payment terms first. Offer a contact option your team can support. A WhatsApp button can open a draft such as "Hi, I want to learn more about [Product]". Check whether your buyers prefer that or a short form.
3. **Proof and terms:** Use real business contact details and applicable registration details. Explain delivery, returns and any advance payment. Offer COD only where you support it, and state exclusions clearly.

Accelerating Bangladesh's public course outline covers target customers, sales channels and service delivery.[^ab-page-gtm] Use those questions to check whether the promise on your page matches how your team works. No single contact method or trust detail guarantees a sale.

## Write the headline around the customer's job

Use words your customers use in daily conversation.
- **Vague:** *"Revolutionizing Bangladeshi Retail with AI Ecosystems."*
- **Clear:** *"Track your daily shop sales and print customer receipts from your mobile phone."*

Keep three facts visible above the fold on a 6-inch phone screen:
- Who this is for (e.g., clothing retailers, poultry farmers, Dhaka freelancers).
- What exact problem it solves.
- Whether it is available right now.

## Filled example: an inventory and SMS receipt tool for Dhaka retailers

## Check the entire page on a phone

Test on the devices and connections your buyers use, including a basic phone on mobile data if that matches your audience. A fast laptop check alone can miss problems.

GDS usability guidance emphasizes observing realistic tasks.[^gds-page-test] Hand the phone to someone matching your buyer profile and watch:
- Can they read the offer and use the main button without a long wait?
- Does the WhatsApp button immediately open the app with a pre-populated message?
- Can they find the full price and business contact details without help?
- Does any table, image or button overflow horizontally off the screen?

## Track conversions across the whole funnel

Page visits show how many people arrived; they do not show whether people became customers. For a WhatsApp-first landing page, measure each step you can actually observe:

```text
Visitor arrives on page -> Clicks WhatsApp CTA -> Sends first inquiry message
-> Attends 10-min demo / confirms order -> Confirms delivery / completes payment
```

If 100 people click your WhatsApp button but only 10 send a message, check whether your pre-filled text was confusing or whether the chat took too long to load. If 50 people chat but none buy, ask what stopped them before changing the offer. Price, delivery, unclear terms or poor fit are possibilities, not proven causes. A button click does not prove a message was sent; count messages and orders separately.

## Before launching the page

- [ ] Buyers can read and use the page on their usual devices and connections.
- [ ] Headline states the specific buyer, problem and solution.
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA button opens directly with a pre-filled text message.
- [ ] Pricing in BDT and shipping/delivery charges are stated clearly.
- [ ] Real business contact details and applicable registration details are visible.
- [ ] Return and inspection terms match what the business and courier allow.
- [ ] Someone handles the WhatsApp inbox during the stated hours.
- [ ] Sample prices, credentials, timings and security promises have been replaced with checked details.

Start by writing the headline, pricing and trust anchors. Use the [trust signals guide](/en/validation/trust-signals) to identify any unresolved doubts your buyers have before spending money on ads.
