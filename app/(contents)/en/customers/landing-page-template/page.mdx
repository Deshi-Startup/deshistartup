# Landing Page Template: High-Converting Pages for Bangladesh

> **In short:** Give one kind of buyer one clear offer, one main action and undeniable local proof. Most visitors browse on mobile connections; keep pages lightweight, fast and focused. In Bangladesh, skepticism is high: display your physical address, trade license or DBID, and clear delivery terms upfront.

## Copy the page outline

A **landing page** is a web page dedicated to converting visitors from a specific ad, social post, campaign or referral link into leads or paying customers.

On mobile screens, each additional form field adds friction — especially when buyers hesitate to share personal details before establishing trust. Test minimal inputs (such as name and phone number) or a direct WhatsApp conversation against multi-field forms to measure drop-off directly, while keeping page assets lightweight and delivery terms transparent.

```text
1. HEADLINE & HOOK
[State the exact job or problem you solve for a specific buyer in plain words.]

2. VALUE PROPOSITION & CURRENT STAGE
For [specific customer], [product/service] helps you [measured outcome] by [how it works].
Status: [In Stock / Available for Immediate Dispatch / Limited Pilot Cohort].

3. PRIMARY CALL-TO-ACTION (CTA)
[Button: "Order via WhatsApp" / "Book 10-Min Phone Demo" / "Buy Cash on Delivery"]
Commitment promise: [e.g., "No advance payment required. Cash on Delivery across 64 districts."]

4. WHAT YOU GET & HOW IT WORKS
- [Concrete deliverable or software capability]
- [Concrete deliverable or hardware warranty]
- [Clear operational limit or excluded scope]
Three-step process:
Step 1: [What customer does] -> Step 2: [What we do/dispatch] -> Step 3: [Customer outcome]

5. TRANSPARENT PRICING & DELIVERY CHARGES
[Product or Subscription price in BDT, tax inclusive or specified]
Delivery charges: [Inside Dhaka: BDT 60–80 | Outside Dhaka: BDT 120–150]
Payment terms: [Cash on Delivery / bKash Merchant Payment / Bank Gateway]

6. LOCAL TRUST & VERIFICATION ANCHORS
- Physical Address: [Registered office or workshop address in Bangladesh]
- Registration: [Trade License number / DBID number / RJSC entity name]
- Inspection guarantee: ["ডেলিভারি ম্যানের সামনে প্রোডাক্ট চেক করে নেওয়ার সুযোগ"]
- Real unedited product video or verified client logos

7. COMMON QUESTIONS & OBJECTIONS (FAQ)
- [Main operational objection, e.g., compatibility or return process]
- [Payment or technical support question]

8. REPEAT PRIMARY CTA & DIRECT CONTACT
[Same clear action button]
Direct WhatsApp hotline: [Working Bangladesh phone number]
```

## Adapt the page for Bangladesh mobile reality

Government content-design principles emphasize organizing pages around the user's immediate task.[^gds-content] In Bangladesh, that task must succeed under severe connectivity constraints.

Keep three local factors in mind:

1. **Mobile data constraints:** Most visitors browse on mobile networks with variable latency. Heavy pages loaded with uncompressed images, heavy scripts, or autoplay media take seconds to render, causing visitors to bounce before the offer loads. Keep total page weight light (under 1.5 MB) using compressed WebP images and minimal client-side JavaScript.
2. **Conversational buying preference:** For both B2B and consumer offers, buyers frequently seek human confirmation before paying. A direct WhatsApp click-to-chat button (pre-filled with a clear opening message like `"Hi, I want to learn more about [Product]"`) lowers friction compared to a long multi-field form, letting prospects clarify stock, delivery timing, or payment terms immediately.
3. **The trust deficit:** Past e-commerce delivery scams (like Evaly or Dhamaka) make Bangladeshi buyers cautious. If you hide your physical office location, refuse Cash on Delivery, or omit return conditions, buyers assume the worst.

Accelerating Bangladesh's go-to-market framework emphasizes matching your offer and delivery promise directly to buyer habits.[^ab-page-gtm] Visible trust anchors are not optional decorations; they are the core conversion mechanism.

## Write the headline around the customer's job

Use words your customers use in daily conversation.
- **Vague:** *"Revolutionizing Bangladeshi Retail with AI Ecosystems."*
- **Clear:** *"Track your daily shop sales and print customer receipts from your mobile phone."*

Keep three facts visible above the fold on a 6-inch phone screen:
- Who this is for (e.g., clothing retailers, poultry farmers, Dhaka freelancers).
- What exact problem it solves.
- Whether it is available right now.

## Check the entire page on a phone

Do not test your landing page on a high-end laptop with fiber-optic Wi-Fi. Test it on an entry-level Android smartphone over a cellular mobile data connection.

GDS usability guidance emphasizes observing realistic tasks.[^gds-page-test] Hand the phone to someone matching your buyer profile and watch:
- Does the page load in under 2.5 seconds?
- Does the WhatsApp button immediately open the app with a pre-populated message?
- Can they find the price and office address in under 10 seconds?
- Does any table, image or button overflow horizontally off the screen?

## Track conversions across the whole funnel

Tracking "page visits" alone is vanity. For a WhatsApp-first landing page, measure each step:

```text
Visitor arrives on page -> Clicks WhatsApp CTA -> Sends first inquiry message
-> Attends 10-min demo / confirms order -> Confirms delivery / completes payment
```

If 100 people click your WhatsApp button but only 10 send a message, check whether your pre-filled text was confusing or whether the chat took too long to load. If 50 people chat but none buy, your pricing, delivery timeline or trust anchors need revision.

## Before launching the page

- [ ] Page weight is under 1.5 MB with compressed WebP images.
- [ ] Headline states the specific buyer, problem and solution.
- [ ] WhatsApp CTA button opens directly with a pre-filled text message.
- [ ] Pricing in BDT and shipping/delivery charges are stated clearly.
- [ ] Physical office address, Trade License/DBID and phone hotline are visible.
- [ ] Return and inspection policies ("ডেলিভারি ম্যানের সামনে চেক") are clear.
- [ ] Someone is actively monitoring the WhatsApp hotline during business hours.

Start by writing the headline, pricing and trust anchors. Use the [trust signals guide](/en/validation/trust-signals) to identify any unresolved doubts your buyers have before spending money on ads.
