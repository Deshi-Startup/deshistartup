# Export-Oriented SaaS from Bangladesh

> **In short:** For software exports, separate the payment route, repatriation deadline and tax-exemption conditions. Non-physical software exports do not use an EXP form, but proceeds and supporting records still need the correct bank process. Eligible SaaS income may qualify for the specific IT-service exemption; receiving revenue through a bank alone is not enough. Hi-Tech Park space and export incentives are separate eligibility questions, not prerequisites or automatic benefits.

If you're building software or a service aimed at foreign customers rather than the local market, the first task isn't building the product – it's understanding how the money legally comes back into the country.

## How the money actually comes back

Bangladesh Bank’s 30 July 2026 export circular distinguishes goods declarations from non-physical software and data exports. Part H1(b) says EXP is inapplicable to the latter; the service-export provisions cover firms and companies as well as individuals.[^bb-fepd-2026] Proceeds must still be repatriated within the prescribed period. Confirm the applicable deadline and the event from which your bank counts it; the service-export instruction has used four months.[^tbs-repatriation-4-months] EXP exemption does not by itself settle whether an ERC or any other registration applies. Ask the authorised dealer bank and the relevant registration authority for your actual service and business structure.

Choose a payment route your authorised dealer bank can support and document. Provider availability, account eligibility and permitted foreign balances differ. Do not assume a Payoneer or Wise balance satisfies repatriation or tax conditions. Keep the contract, invoice, delivery evidence and remittance records, and agree the required payment-purpose information with the bank.

## Tax benefits on the income

Income Tax Act 2023, Sixth Schedule Part 1 paragraph 21, exempts income from specified services, including SaaS, for a resident person or non-resident Bangladeshi individual from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2027. The proviso requires **all income, expenditure and investment of the business wholly through bank transfer** from 1 July 2024. The Finance Act 2026 changes item (q) to freelancing and content creation, but does not remove this proviso or extend the window.[^legalseba-ites] Check that the actual service and taxpayer qualify, preserve the full transaction trail and complete the applicable return formalities; this is not a blanket exemption for every technology business.

The investment-promotion page also describes Hi-Tech Park tax holidays and a 6% software/ITES/hardware export incentive with a stated end date of 31 December 2025.[^bida-ites-incentives] Treat those as the page’s dated descriptions, not confirmed benefits available to your business today. For a park tax holiday, obtain the applicable NBR instrument and eligibility/approval conditions. For export cash assistance, check the current Bangladesh Bank circular and the authorised dealer bank’s application requirements.[^bida-ites-incentives] Do not include either benefit in the base-case cash forecast before eligibility is confirmed.

## Which government body actually matters

Bangladesh has several export-support routes, but they serve different kinds of business. Economic-zone functions formerly handled by BEZA moved into Invest Bangladesh when the new authority began operations on 23 August 2026; BEPZA continues to run export-processing zones.[^bida-ites-incentives] Both routes are primarily for factories, manufacturing, and export-oriented industries that need land and physical infrastructure. For a software team running from a home or ordinary office, neither is usually relevant.

One relevant body for a software team considering park facilities is the Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority (BHTPA), which serves domestic and foreign high-tech industries, particularly ICT, digital services, software, and startups, inside hi-tech and software parks.[^bhtpa] Renting space alone does not establish tax-holiday eligibility; obtain the applicable approval and conditions. Alongside this, BASIS, as the software and ITES sector association, is a good source for membership, policy context, and current export-related updates.[^basis]

## The practical path

For a software founder, the rough sequence looks like this.

1. Choose a bank-supported channel for receiving foreign-customer money, and confirm the funds ultimately land in a Bangladeshi bank account.
2. Confirm the non-physical-service EXP exemption, the applicable repatriation deadline and the payment-purpose records with your bank.
3. Check the specified-service, taxpayer and all-income/expenditure/investment bank-transfer conditions for the exemption, and claim it correctly on your annual tax return.
4. If your team grows or you need a fixed address, weigh the pros and cons of taking space in a Hi-Tech Park.
5. Consider BASIS membership for networking and current policy information.

## Checklist

- [ ] I've decided which channel foreign-customer money will arrive through into a Bangladeshi bank account
- [ ] I have confirmed EXP exemption for my non-physical service, the applicable repatriation deadline and its starting event with my bank
- [ ] I've verified the exemption’s eligible service, taxpayer and bank-transfer conditions for all income, expenditure and investment and its deadline (30 June 2027, as of what's known while writing this)
- [ ] I've checked the current Bangladesh Bank circular and bank requirements for any export cash incentive
- [ ] I have checked whether an Invest Bangladesh economic zone or BEPZA zone is relevant; for an ordinary software team, BHTPA is usually the more relevant body

Rule of thumb: **for export-oriented software, the first job isn't building the product – it's understanding how money comes back and what the tax-exemption conditions are. Check the applicable deadlines and current conditions before relying on them.**
