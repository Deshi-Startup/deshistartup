# Start Here: Choose Your First Useful Step

> **In short:** You do not need to read this website in order. Choose the question in front of you, do one small piece of work and use the result to decide what comes next.

Deshi Startup is a free operating manual for people building scalable businesses in Bangladesh. You can use it while studying, working, exploring an idea or already running a company. You do not need an account to read or use the learning tools.

## Where are you today?

| Your situation | Start with | Leave with |
|---|---|---|
| I am curious but have no idea | [Ideas and market research](/en/ideas) | A customer problem to investigate |
| I have an idea but little evidence | [Test demand](/en/journeys/test-demand) | One bounded experiment and a decision rule |
| I want to launch or formalise | [Legal roadmap](/en/legal-roadmap) | An applicability checklist and unresolved requirements |
| I need customers | [Customers and sales](/en/customers) | A specific buyer, offer and next sales action |
| I already operate and need better numbers | [Metrics](/en/metrics) | One measure connected to a decision |
| I need to fit this around other commitments | [Founder life](/en/founder-life) | A realistic time and money limit |

If startup terminology is unfamiliar, use [what is a startup?](/en/start-here/what-is-a-startup) or the [glossary](/en/start-here/glossary). A business can use software, sell services or operate online without those labels alone deciding whether its model is scalable.

## Write your starting note

```text
The people I want to help:
The task or problem I want to understand:
What I already know, and how I know it:
The most important unknown:
One action I can take with the time and money available:
When I will review what happened:
```

Keep observed facts separate from hopes. A conversation can reveal a problem; a compliment does not establish willingness to buy. A sale provides evidence about that transaction, not certainty about the whole market.

Before testing an activity involving payments, safety, sensitive information or regulated services, check the relevant requirements. A small test is not automatically exempt. The [legal roadmap](/en/legal-roadmap) helps you identify questions to resolve.
