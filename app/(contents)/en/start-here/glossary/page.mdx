# Startup Glossary: What do these terms mean?

When starting a business, you run into a lot of unfamiliar words. We have collected the English terms you will constantly hear in investor meetings, see on government forms, and read across this site, and explained them in plain, everyday language. 

We have also added a real-world Bangladeshi example to each term so you can see exactly how it works in practice. Simply type the word you heard to find it, or jump straight to its starting letter.

Knowing a word and truly understanding how it affects your business are two different things. Take the time to dig deeper into the concepts that your next big decision relies on. 

And remember one golden rule for any meeting or deal: **never sign a paper if it contains a term you do not fully understand.** Asking questions isn't a sign of ignorance—it is simply doing your own due diligence.
