# Contributors

Only reviewed work that becomes part of Deshi Startup is counted here. Every contributor has a
profile showing what they did, their role, the pages involved, and the public source. The order
follows how many accepted events credit that person. One event can cover several pages or roles
and still counts once, so the order does not measure volume, skill, quality, or impact.
